Checklist Types
Basic C/R
Challenge/Response

In this mode, the co-pilot will read the checklist item, and wait for you to reply with any affirmative or negative response.

Example:
Co-Pilot: "Landing Gear Down"
You: "Set"
No C/R
Timed Pause

In this mode, the co-pilot will read the checklist item, and then pause for a short period of time before moving on to the next item.

If you reply, you do not need to key up the mic on intercom. Just speak. The co-pilot will hear you and move on.

Example:
Co-Pilot: "Landing Gear Down?"
You: Say anything. No intercom PTT needed. The co-pilot moves on.
Advanced C/R
Challenge/Response with Specific Phrases

In this mode, the co-pilot will read the checklist item, then wait for you to respond with a specific phrase configured in Checklist Tuner.

Example:
Co-Pilot: "Doors?"
You: "Locked and Armed"
Full Automatic
Automatic Configuration

In this mode, the co-pilot will read the checklist item, and automatically perform the action configured in Checklist Tuner.

Example:
Co-Pilot: "Mixture? Set for best power."
