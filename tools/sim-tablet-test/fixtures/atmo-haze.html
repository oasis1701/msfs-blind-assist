Atmosphere (haze and sky)
The mod's haze and sky. Off: the sim's own atmosphere, exactly.
Haze from METAR
The haze follows the sim's own weather reports (METARs): its amount, height and colour, blended between stations as you fly. Off: the manual haze settings below.
Haze strength
How much of the haze the reports describe is drawn. 100 % is as reported; lower gives clearer air and longer visibility than reported (50 % about doubles it), higher gives more haze. The sim's own fog and mist stay as they are.
100 %
Distant clouds fade creamy, not grey
Distant clouds fade colour by colour, as the haze scatters blue light out of the view, so they turn creamy like real ones. Off: the sim's grey dimming.
Sun glare in haze
How much the haze glares around the sun: higher gives a brighter, tighter glow looking toward it (the sim's own is about 85 %). With haze from METAR, the weather adds its share to this.
57 %
Haze near the ground
How hazy the air near the ground is, as a multiple of the sim's own surface haze: about ×4 on a clean mountain day, ×8-14 in hazy or polluted lowlands.
×4.0
Haze layer top
The height above sea level where the hazy layer near the ground ends. Above it the air is clearer and the sky bluer.
1.5 km
Haze above the layer
How hazy the air is above that layer, as a multiple of the sim's surface haze: lower gives clearer views at altitude.
×0.6
Brown haze (pollution)
Light-absorbing particles such as soot and smog in the layer near the ground: a darker, brownish haze. 0 is none; 0.15-0.3 suits polluted lowlands.
0.05
Haze colour
The haze's colour, set by its particle size: grey (0), humid or sea-air white (0.3), continental blue (1.3), smoke (2).
1.3 continental blue
Real-world sky colours
Sky colours fitted to the real sky across the whole spectrum: a blue sky overhead at sunset and the right orange of a low sun, with ozone by season and latitude. Off: the sim's own colours.
Daylight white balance
The sim's sunlight has its colour from above the atmosphere, slightly warm on screen, so sunlit clouds look cream. On: sunlight looks white, as your eyes see it; sky, clouds and ground shift alike.
Cloud lighting
How the mod draws clouds: this tab's lighting, solid clouds and cloud types. Off: the sim's own clouds, exactly. (The cloud shapes from METARs and the thunderstorms stay; see the Clouds tab.)
Silver lining strength
How bright the silver lining is: the glowing rim of clouds close to the sun. 0 % is the sim's own.
100 %
Silver lining size
How far from the sun the silver lining reaches (its half width). Real cloud droplets give about 1-2°.
4.4°
Glory
Coloured rings around the point opposite the sun, where your shadow falls on cloud tops: blue inside, red outside. Off at 0.
0.5
Your aircraft's shadow on clouds
Your aircraft's shadow on the clouds below, at the centre of the glory: how dark its core is. It blurs with distance, as a real shadow does.
40 %
Sun-facing cloud sides
Light on the sides of clouds that face the sun, worked out as real, thick cloud reflects it. It fades out where the cloud is shaded. 0 % is off. With it on, set "Powder effect (grey rims, sun behind you)" to 0 %: the two don't go well together.
0 %
Bounced light at cloud edges
Light that has bounced around inside the cloud, already at its sunlit edges: brighter cloud sides with the sun to one side, and a slightly smaller silver lining.
40 %
Light deep inside clouds
How much sunlight reaches deep inside clouds, as a multiple of the sim's own: brighter interiors and bases.
×3.6
Powder effect (grey rims, sun behind you)
With the sun behind you, cloud edges and thin parts look grey and dense cores white, which outlines each turret. It fades out toward the sun.
50 %
Crisper cloud edges
Sharper cloud outlines: the sim fades every cloud edge over 220 m or more; 1 halves that, 3 quarters it. Far clouds keep softer edges, against flicker.
off
Glory ring size
The glory's size, as the angle of its first red ring: smaller cloud droplets make larger rings.
6.0°
Clouds follow the METARs
Near weather stations, the sim's live clouds take the layers the stations report: base, cover, CB and TCU. 100 % is the reported layers within the station radius. Live weather only.
100 %
Station radius
Each station's clouds apply fully within this distance and fade out over the same distance beyond.
50 km
Solid clouds
Clouds as thick as real ones: the sim's are see-through at bases and edges, and the sun glows through an overcast. Thickness at a cumulus core (1 is the sim's); only the look, not the lighting.
2.00
Visibility inside clouds
How far you see inside a cloud, around your aircraft: the wings fade into it as in a real cloud. The sim's clouds are so thin inside that you see for hundreds of metres. Only near you, so clouds seen from outside keep their look; ice clouds stay thin. Not below the reported cloud base (the nearest METAR's, or a weather preset's lowest layer): the sim's low decks can start well below it. A hotkey can switch it off for now (the Hotkeys tab).
10 m
Structure inside clouds
Patches of denser and thinner cloud around you that you fly through, each shaded by the cloud toward the sun: thinner parts brighter. 0 % is an even fog.
10 %
Cloud types
Tells cloud types apart by height, depth and cover: dense, crisp cumulus; thinner, softer stratocumulus, stratus, altocumulus and altostratus. 0 % draws every cloud like cumulus. Live weather only.
100 %
Smooth cloud bases
Flat cloud bases: the sim's round puffs fade out toward each cloud's base, while the tops keep their billows.
100 %
Thunderstorms (experimental)
Storm clouds where a station nearby reports thunder or CB/TCU, or the sim's rain is very heavy: towers up to the tropopause with anvils, each living 45-80 min. Visual only; live weather only.
Cloud bases: soft to solid

AtmosphereMod 0.7.3
New in AtmosphereMod 0.7.3
Hotkeys
The new Hotkeys tab changes the mod's keys: tap Change, then press the new keys in the sim. Glass Cockpit DLSS Fix, one of OptiShade's components, uses Ctrl+Shift+F9 and F11 by default: while it's loaded, the tab tells you if its keys are the same as the mod's.
In-cloud fog on/off
A key of your choice switches the fog inside clouds off for now, and on again: for when it shows just below a cloud. It has no key until you give it one in the Hotkeys tab.
Historical weather
The haze and the clouds now follow historical weather, and switching between live and historical weather in flight brings the other weather at once. The haze could also stay with a faraway station for the rest of a flight: fixed.
Installer
Updating and uninstalling no longer stop at a read-only folder in Community ("Access is denied"), and the installer also runs under Wine on Linux.
0.7.2
Low cloud bases: The in-cloud fog starts at the reported cloud base, so a low overcast no longer fogs the air down to the runway.
Performance report: Counts the mod's shaders that ran, asks for your username, and doesn't file the same report twice.
0.7.1
Performance report: A button at the end of the app's page measures what the mod costs on your PC, with and without it, and, if you like, sends me the result.
What's new: Also lists the changes in earlier versions, folded away.
0.7.0
Varied cloud tops: Clouds in one layer no longer all end at the same height: where the air rises more, they grow taller. On by default (C37 in tunables.ini).
Sun-facing cloud sides: A slider in the Cloud lighting tab, off by default: light on the sides of clouds that face the sun. If you turn it on, set Powder effect to 0 %.
Haze strength: A slider in the Haze tab, under Haze from METAR, for when the reported haze looks too strong or too weak. 100 % is as reported.
Smoother haze changes: When a station's report changes between an open-ended visibility (9999, 10SM, CAVOK) and a number, the haze fades over 5 minutes instead of jumping.
With RenoDX: An installer parameter lets the two mods work together: see "With RenoDX" in the README.
What's new: This window, once after each update.
0.6.1
Sliders on narrow tablets: Fixed: they collapsed to a lone thumb that couldn't be dragged.
Installer: No more Windows freeze while patching. It also finds PowerShell when it isn't on the PATH, recognises ReShade's standard build, and puts the add-on where ReShade's AddonPath points.
METAR stations: Near many stations the mod no longer keeps swapping the same ones every couple of minutes.
0.6.0
Presets: The Presets tab saves all your settings under a name, loads them again, and imports presets others share.
Fly-through clouds: Around your aircraft, liquid clouds get a real cloud's density, so the wingtips fade into the cloud (Visibility inside clouds, in the Clouds tab).
Smoother in cloud: The once-a-second stutter in cloudy weather is gone, and the tint on distant clouds is cheaper to draw.
Tablet layout: Laid out for every tablet size and orientation, with larger text.
Solid clouds: The default is 0.5 (was 2). An existing install kept its value: load Default on the Presets tab to get it.
0.5.1
OptiShade: The installer accepts OptiShade's ReShade, and warns when it finds two ReShades.
0.5.0
Haze from the stations around you: Every part of the view gets the haze of the METAR stations nearest to it, so the haze changes gradually from one area to the next.
Dust and smoke in colour: Dust looks beige, smoke ochre-brown and dry haze neutral, instead of grey.
Cloud decks: Altocumulus breaks up into finer cloudlets, stratocumulus into rows, and the sim's stratiform layers become decks with lumpy tops.
0.4.0
Thunderstorms (experimental): Off by default (Clouds tab): cumulonimbus with anvils grow where the weather shows a storm, and each lives 45 to 80 minutes.
Sky colours: Fitted to the sky computed at 95 wavelengths: blue overhead at sunset, the right orange for a low sun, and ozone by season and latitude.
Explanations: Hover over a setting's name for an explanation.
Cleaner clouds: No small white puffs in clear air, no stripes on far layers, and no repeating pattern in cloud decks.
0.3.0
Cloud types: Stratus, stratocumulus, altocumulus and altostratus are thinner with soft edges, while cumulus stay solid. Each layer's type comes from its height, depth and cover.
0.2.0
Settings on the tablet: The main settings as switches and sliders, applied at once and saved to tunables.ini.
Fixes: The mod no longer switches itself off when other software opens a graphics device of its own, and stations reporting a layer without its base no longer put clouds on the ground.
0.1.1
Installer: Works on non-English Windows, finds more installations, and asks when it can't find the sim or the Community folder.
0.1.0
First release: Haze with a top, set from the METARs; real-world sky colours; cloud lighting with silver linings, a glory and your aircraft's shadow; and clouds anchored to the METARs near stations.
