# COWS Diamond DA40 rules

Loaded with any DA40 code. Background: docs/da40.md and docs/da40-xls-variables.md. Full text of each rule: docs/invariants/da40.md.

- [DA40-1] `STATE_*` L:vars are READ-ONLY MIRRORS: a write sticks on read-back and drives nothing. Write the K: event or the input L:var and read the mirror to confirm. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-1
- [DA40-2] The definition follows COWS DA40 1.2.0 with no 1.1.5 compatibility; every failure is multiplied by `L:FAILURES_ON`, so every failure panel carries the read-only Failures Active row and the mode is never enforced. Inject indication failures through `FAILURES_SENS_*`, never the derived `FAILURES_DISP_*`. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-2
- [DA40-3] Every failure the random picker can raise has a row or a written reason (`CowsDA40FailureCoverageTests`, both directions), and no failure row is inert: a bound trip must pop a breaker or be read outside the picker (`NoBreakerTripRowIsInert`). Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-3
- [DA40-4] Every L:var the code writes must exist in the package (`EveryLvarTheCodeWritesExistsInThePackage`); a STOCK SimVar for a system the airframe lacks must be scoped out by hand in `VariantScope`. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-4
- [DA40-5] One airframe's variables never appear on the other: `CowsDA40Definition.VariantScope` removes `NgOnlyKeys`/`XlsOnlyKeys` from the variables AND every panel list after all builders run. Never gate a shared builder's rows alone; `CowsDA40PackagePresenceTests` checks whole names per variant. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-5
- [DA40-6] Controls and breakers are named by the cockpit placard VERBATIM (`TOOLTIPID`/`ANIMTIP`, breakers through `DA40BreakerPlacards`), never expanded or "corrected" to the AFM: four XLS breakers are wired differently from the real aeroplane. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-6
- [DA40-7] The DA40 carries NO panel `HelpText` on either variant and must not regain any (`NoDA40VariableCarriesHelpText`); a control that needs explaining is named wrong, and the background lives in docs/da40.md and code comments. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-7
- [DA40-8] Do not baby the pilot: a STATE announcement reports the aeroplane and never appends what it means or what to do. Validation errors and a refusal's reason are not affected. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-8
- [DA40-9] `IsAnnounced` governs BACKGROUND changes only (combo echo is global via `_uiSetEcho`): every settable switch is Continuous and announced, every numeric readout silent. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-9
- [DA40-10] A described state with ValueDescriptions that should interrupt (engine stopped, ECU latch, test results, transfer pump) must announce; a failure graded 0-100 announces its onset and a 25-point worsening, never the value. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-10
- [DA40-11] `AddFlag` leaves a flag OnRequest and unannounced: check a helper's defaults against what the state means before using it for anything that must interrupt. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-11
- [DA40-12] A panel listed in `GetPanelStructure()` with no controls and no display vars renders BLANK: every panel is built or on `CowsDA40PanelStructureTests.NotBuiltYetOnXls`, which is checked both ways and is empty today. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-12
- [DA40-13] A held button is rewritten every 15 ms (`HoldLVar`): the template drains a third per frame. The ECU test is held until `FADEC_ECUTEST_STEP:1` reaches 10 (30.6 s measured, 45 s ceiling). Every write replays the click, so keep holds short and say what the button did. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-13
- [DA40-14] After a flight load or reconnect the DA40's announcers RECORD and do not speak (`DA40LoadSettle`, `OwnerMute`, never `Suppressed`); never wipe baselines instead, and every timer-driven announcer ticks through `UnderLoadSettle`. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-14
- [DA40-15] A DA40 profile follows the airframe the sim has loaded (`DA40Airframe` + `MainForm.SwapDA40ProfileToMatch`): on connect from the TITLE and on every load from the AircraftLoaded path, NG<->XLS only. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-15
- [DA40-16] The interaction surface is enumerated, not waited for: `CowsDA40InteractionSurfaceTests` names every `<Component ID>` with its control or reason. Sweep the second axis too: what a pilot does to the aeroplane outside the cockpit and what the model tracks with no control. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-16
- [DA40-17] Coverage tests come from third-party and vendor inventories: FS Copilot's `COWS_DA40*.yaml` and `DA40 LVAR bindings.txt`. "Not in the package" needs a binary scan, and a button's `Name` is its own key, so never read gaps off it. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-17
- [DA40-18] Read the aircraft's own native checklist and POH before probing: its Tips pages hold the warm-up, speeds and the ECU-test battery reset; facts no variable can express belong in docs/da40.md, not in a call-out. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-18
- [DA40-19] `sim_running: false` is load-bearing: the gauge layer runs (mirrors, CAS, clock, even G_FORCE noise) while the engine and flight model are inert, with starter active, torque and battery load at exactly 0. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-19
- [DA40-20] The `REPAIR_AND_REFUEL` key binding cannot be triggered from outside (neither the K-event nor `TransmitClientEvent`): never add a button for it. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-20
- [DA40-21] The COWS DA40 has ONE avionics fit (stock WT G1000 plus GFC 700), no EFB, no equipment page and exactly two screens (PFD, MFD) with no captain/first-officer split; tail models vary by livery only. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-21
- [DA40-22] Anything doable on a G1000 display (pages, checklist, softkeys, the Engine-page menu options) does not ALSO get a panel; its variables stay defined and announced. Full: docs/invariants/da40.md#da40-22
