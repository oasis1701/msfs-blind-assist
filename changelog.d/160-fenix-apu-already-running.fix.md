Start the APU yourself, outside the First Officer window, and the Fenix checklist now notices. Before, "APU: ON and available" stayed unticked over a perfectly good APU, and ticking it by hand made the First Officer press the APU START pushbutton again on the running APU and then sit silent for up to three minutes before announcing "Unable to complete". Two things were wrong: it was watching the START pushbutton's ON light, which goes out the moment the APU becomes available, rather than the AVAIL light beside it — now settled by reading both lights in the sim; and ticking any checklist item always made the First Officer go and do it, even when you had already done it yourself. Ticking something the aircraft is already set for now simply ticks it, on every aircraft, without touching a switch. The "press and listen" self-tests — TCAS, weather radar, GPWS — still run every time you tick them.
