On the PMDG 737, the four "Window Heat On" lights are no longer announced by default.
The window-heat system switches each window's heater on and off by itself as it reaches
temperature, so these lights kept announcing on and off at random, mostly on the ground,
while nobody had touched a switch. They are muted once, the first time this version
runs. Unmute them in the monitor manager (Ctrl+M) if you want them back.
