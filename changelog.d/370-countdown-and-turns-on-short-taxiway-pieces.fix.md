Taxi guidance: the hold short countdown and the turn callouts now come on time where the scenery splits the taxiway into short pieces, which it does at most hold lines. At Newark, taxiing to Runway 04L via AA, the only countdown line was "Hold short runway 04L at AA in 75 feet. Stop.", 84 feet from the line; you now hear "in 300 feet.", "in 175 feet. Slow down." and "in 100 feet. Stop." at the distances your speed calls for. Turns after a run of short pieces now get their advance notice, for example "In 400 feet, sharp right onto taxiway F3, 90 degrees.", instead of only "Sharp right now", and "Slow for turn" comes in time to slow down.

As you approach the hold short point BA is about to stop you at, it no longer says "Passing the runway 04L at AA hold line." or "Crossing runway …" about it (a line drawn with two hold points may still be named as you pass the first), and "Runway 04L ahead." no longer cuts off the "Stop." of that countdown. An advance notice for a turn further along the route names the turn you will make there, and waits until you are rolling. A corner the scenery draws in two short pieces is called as one turn, sharp when it is, and a short piece drawn off the line no longer sounds like a turn.
