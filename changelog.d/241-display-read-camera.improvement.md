On the Fenix A320, the PMDG 777, the PMDG 737-800 and the iFly 737 MAX 8, the AI display reads now aim the simulator's camera at the display they are reading, so you no longer have to be looking at the right part of the panel first. Your own view is put back afterwards — including a custom cabin or wing view — and if the app cannot get you back it says so and tries again on your next read.
