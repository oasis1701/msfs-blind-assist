On the Fenix A320, the PMDG 777, the iFly 737 MAX 8 and the PMDG 737-800, the AI display reads now aim the simulator's camera at the display they are reading, so you no longer have to be looking at the right part of the panel first. On every aircraft that does this, the camera is put back where you were flying from afterwards — including a custom cabin or wing view — and if it cannot get back, the app says so and tries again on your next display read. On the Fenix, Alt+N reads your own navigation display, and Alt+I now finds the standby instruments whichever kind your aircraft is fitted with.
