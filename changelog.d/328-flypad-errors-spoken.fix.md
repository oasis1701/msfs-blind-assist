On the FlyByWire A32NX, A380X and Headwind A330, the flyPad's error and warning pop-ups are
now spoken. Before, they vanished after a few seconds, some after a quarter of a second,
without a word: a failed takeoff calculation said nothing at all. An error such as
"Beyond Takeoff Weight Limit" now stays at the top of the flyPad page with a Dismiss
button until you dismiss it or press Calculate again. Errors shown on the throttle
calibration page, such as two detents overlapping, are spoken too, and clear themselves
once you fix the problem.
