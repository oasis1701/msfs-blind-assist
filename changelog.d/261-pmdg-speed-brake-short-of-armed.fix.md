PMDG 777: the speed brake is no longer announced "armed" when the lever sits just past the Armed detent with the speed brakes already partly up. The lever is now read precisely, so "Speed brake armed" means the Armed detent itself, and anywhere past it you hear how far the speed brake is deployed. On the 737 and 777, a lever resting short of Armed reads as Down on the Control Stand panel and is announced "Speed brake down".
