PMDG 737 and 777: the speed brake is no longer announced "armed" when the lever sits just past the Armed detent with the speed brakes already partly up. "Speed brake armed" now means the Armed detent itself; anywhere past it, and wherever the lever comes to rest between positions (a hardware lever, for example), you hear how far the speed brake is deployed — which the 737 used to leave silent. A lever resting short of Armed reads as Down on the Control Stand panel and is announced "Speed brake down".
