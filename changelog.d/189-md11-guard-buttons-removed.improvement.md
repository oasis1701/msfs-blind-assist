The MD-11's panels no longer carry a separate guard-cover button for controls that lift their own cover. Pressing a guarded switch already opens the cover, settles and operates it, so the extra button was a second control for something already done — and one you could leave in the wrong position. A guard button now appears only where the app genuinely cannot lift the cover for you: the EVAC and GPWS switches, the main cargo door arm switch, and the three engine fire handles.
