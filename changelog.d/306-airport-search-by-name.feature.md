The Electronic Flight Bag's Airport Lookup can find an airport without its ICAO code. Type part
of its name or city, such as "Landvetter", "Göteborg", "ohare" or "charles de gaulle", and press
Enter. Case, accents and apostrophes don't matter. An exact code, or a single match, loads at
once and is spoken; otherwise a list opens, best match first, and Enter loads the one you pick.
With the offline location data installed, a town's name also finds the airports around it:
"Gothenburg" lists Landvetter and Säve, "London" lists Heathrow, Gatwick and Stansted before
London, Ontario, and a town without an airfield lists its nearest. Helipads are left out of town
results. On MSFS 2024, whose navigation data has no city names, a city is found through airport
names and the location data. When connected to the simulator, each result and the Airport
Information box say how far the airport is from you and in which direction, for example "212 nm
southwest". Typing a code still loads it as before, and pressing Tab now reads the airport's
code and name first: the Load button is gone, and the tab order is the field, Airport
Information, the runway list, then Runway Information.
