The Electronic Flight Bag's Airport Lookup can find an airport without its ICAO code. Type part
of its name or city, such as "Landvetter", "Göteborg" or "charles de gaulle", and press Enter
or the new Search button beside the field. Case and accents don't matter. With the offline
location data installed, a town's name also finds the airports around it: "Gothenburg" lists
Landvetter and Säve, and a town without an airport lists its nearest. One match loads at once;
several open a list, the biggest town's airports first, then the longest runways. When
connected to the simulator, each result and the Airport Information box say how far the airport
is from you and in which direction, for example "212 nm southwest". The old Load button is
replaced by Search, and the tab order is now field, Search, airport information, runway list,
runway information.
