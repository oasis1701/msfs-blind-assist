Visual guidance has an airspeed mode for approaches where the throttle is not yours to manage, such as the MSFS 2024 career landing lesson (which locks it) or an engine-out glide. Press M while visual guidance is on and the vertical tone holds the aircraft's approach speed with pitch instead of holding the glidepath: fast means nose up, slow means nose down. Minus and equals move the target speed 5 knots and speak it, F reads your speed error and how far above or below the glidepath you are, and the mile callouts add "on profile" or "200 high" so you still know where the runway is. Steering, the flare and touchdown are unchanged.
