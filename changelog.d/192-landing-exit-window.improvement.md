The Landing Exit window fills itself in as online taxiway names arrive, instead of
showing whatever the local navdata alone could name at the moment you opened it. At an
airport like LSZH, where the navdata names none of the 1,407 taxiway segments, that is
the difference between a runway offering a couple of exits and offering all of them —
and the list used to be stuck as you first saw it for the rest of the session.

The window also tells you how many of the exits on offer have no taxiway mapped clear of
the runway, and no longer picks one of those as the default selection. You learn that
while planning on the ground rather than during the rollout.
