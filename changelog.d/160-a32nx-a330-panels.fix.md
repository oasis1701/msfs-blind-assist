FlyByWire A32NX and Headwind A330: the All Landing Lights On and Off buttons now switch the
lights, and the Engine Mode selector follows the knob when it is moved in the cockpit. On the
A330, the Exterior Lighting panel shows one Landing Lights row and the real Nav and Logo state,
the Interior Lighting panel no longer offers two glareshield flood knobs the aircraft does not
have, and Passengers on Board counts all ten cabin zones.
