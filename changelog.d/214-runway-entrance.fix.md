Picking a runway as your taxi destination now routes you to a real runway entrance
instead of the nearest node to the lineup point. At runways whose taxi network does not
meet that point — typically a long displaced threshold — the route used to dead-end
abeam the runway, announce "this route does not reach Runway X" even with no taxiways
entered, and then try to steer you across the grass. An entrance further down the runway
than the one you selected is never substituted silently, so you can't be handed less
takeoff run than you asked for.
