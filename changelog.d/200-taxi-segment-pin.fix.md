Taxi guidance no longer strands you circling one spot after a wide corner. If you swung wide through a sharp turn onto a long taxiway, the steering tone could lock onto a fixed point behind you and flip left/right every time you crossed it — following it just flew you in circles, and restarting guidance rebuilt the same trap. The tone now picks up your real position on the taxiway and leads ahead down the route, including after a mid-taxi recalculate.
