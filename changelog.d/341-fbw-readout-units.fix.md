FlyByWire A380X, A320 and Headwind A330: several readouts now give the value the cockpit shows. Cross-track error is in nautical miles (it read hundreds of miles while on track), the preselected and managed speeds are in knots, and the vertical profile target is in feet. On the A320 and A330, the Vertical Deviation row now says how many feet above or below the profile you are, where it only said "active". Output Ctrl+W reads waypoint names of six or more characters in full (61N 020W was read "^^1W020"), and its bearing is right with TRUE reference selected.
