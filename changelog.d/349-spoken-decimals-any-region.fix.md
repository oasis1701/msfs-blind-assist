Numbers are now spoken and shown with a decimal point whatever your Windows regional
format is. On a PC set to German, Swedish or another region that writes a decimal comma,
Blind Assist said "COM1 active 118,300", "Mach 0,78" and "NAV 1 110,30", which a screen
reader stumbles over or reads as a different number; large figures such as "12.345 feet"
could be heard as twelve point three four five. This covered frequencies, Mach, flight
path angle, the altimeter in inches, distances, the system display rows and the flight
planning windows, on every aircraft.

On such a PC the Weather Radar window should also now load its SIGMETs, pilot reports and
winds aloft: the addresses it asked for them with were built with decimal commas.
