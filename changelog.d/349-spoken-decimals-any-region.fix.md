Numbers are now spoken and shown with a decimal point whatever your Windows regional
format is. On a PC set to German, Swedish or another region that writes a decimal comma,
Blind Assist said "COM1 active 118,300", "Mach 0,78" and "NAV 1 110,30", which a screen
reader stumbles over or reads as a different number; large figures such as "12.345 feet"
could be heard as twelve point three four five. This covered frequencies, Mach, flight
path angle, the altimeter in inches, distances, the system display rows and the flight
planning windows, on every aircraft.

On such a PC the Weather Radar window should also now load its pilot reports and winds
aloft: the addresses it asked for them with were built with decimal commas. On every PC
the pilot reports should now load at all: they were asked for with latitude and longitude
the wrong way round, so they came from another part of the world, or from nowhere.

On a PC set to Swedish, Norwegian or Finnish, a descent rate or a negative flight path
angle typed into the vertical speed window of the FlyByWire A32NX or A380 or the Headwind
A330 should now be set: the minus sign was written in a form the aircraft could not read.
