Taxi guidance: a runway hold short is no longer missed when you come up to it off to one side. BA only stopped you within 25 metres of the centre of the line, so on a wide taxiway, or after cutting a corner, you could roll past with one "Stop" callout, no hold, and the tone carrying on towards the runway. It now stops you as soon as you are level with the line, up to 75 metres either side, with the usual "Stop. Hold short of runway 12R. Press continue when cleared."

When a route cannot reach its runway at all, because the airport's data has no taxiways to it (runway 02R/20L at the new Singapore scenery), BA no longer calls the end of the route "Runway 20L", holds you short of it and offers to line you up. It counts you down to the end of the route and says "Stop. End of this route. It does not reach Runway 20L. Reprogram the taxi route, including the taxiway that connects to the runway."

The route summary no longer repeats your own taxiway after "then" when it crosses another one on the way: "via K4, then M7B, M, M13", not "via K4, then Z, K, K4, M7B, M, M13".

In the File menu, Suspend Hotkeys is now on the letter K. It shared S with Settings, so neither opened on its letter.
