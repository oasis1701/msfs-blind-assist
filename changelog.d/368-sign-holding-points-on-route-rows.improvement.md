Taxi guidance: a holding point that exists only as a sign beside the taxiway, such as KK or KG on taxiway K at Dubai, can now be chosen in any taxiway row's "Hold short of" list, for an instruction like "taxi via K, hold short of KK". Until now these were offered only in Progressive Taxi. They are listed among the taxiways and marked "(holding point, from sign, approximate)", because the stop is taken from the sign's position and may not be exactly on the painted line. Where Am I (Output Alt+Y) now also names a holding point you are near, for example "Taxiway K, near holding point KK at OMDB", and says which taxiway the point is on when it is not yours; the first press at a new airport may give the taxiway alone while BA works the points out. Both need the online names setting switched on.
