Taxi guidance: a holding point that exists only as a sign beside the taxiway, such as KK or KG on taxiway K at Dubai, can now be chosen in any taxiway row's "Hold short of" list, for an instruction like "taxi via K, hold short of KK". Until now these were offered only in Progressive Taxi. They are listed among the taxiways and marked "(holding point, from sign, approximate)", because the stop is taken from the sign and may be a few metres from the painted line. They need the online names setting switched on.
