Controls that had to be pressed twice now work on the first press. Several buttons sent an
identical command each time they were used, and the simulator discarded the repeat — so turning
something off right after turning it on, or switching the wipers off and on again, could be
ignored without any sign it had happened.

MSFS Blind Assist also now says so out loud when it cannot reach the aircraft by its preferred
route, instead of carrying on in a degraded mode where some cockpit switches quietly refuse to
move. If you hear that message, MobiFlight is not available and some controls will not respond.
