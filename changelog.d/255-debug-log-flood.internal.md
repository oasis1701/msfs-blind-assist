debug.log no longer records every unchanged one-per-second SimVar delivery (the Fenix registers about fifty of them), which had been filling the whole 20 MB log retention in roughly 35 minutes of flying. Changed and forced deliveries still log, and so does every answer to a read the app is waiting on (the read-backs behind spoken verdicts), so "the sim answered with the old value" still differs from "the sim never answered"; any other unchanged delivery logs once per variable per connection, so a full session's log now survives for support.
