Gates that the airport data leaves unconnected to the taxiway network can now be taxied to. At OMDB, stand B 18R stops 12 metres short of taxiway U in the navdata, which made taxi guidance answer "could not find a nearby taxiway node" for a gate ATC had just assigned — the only way in was to teleport. Where a stand sits within 50 metres of the network, guidance now joins the two and routes to it normally; a stand genuinely out on its own is still reported rather than guessed at.
