When a taxi route can't be built, the Taxi Assist route summary now tells you why instead of still showing your last route. Previously the reason was spoken once and then gone — if your screen reader talked over it, or you simply wanted to check it again, the summary box was still displaying the route from an earlier calculation, which read exactly like a route that had just been built for you. Now every failure — no destination selected, a stand the taxiways can't reach, a SayIntentions import that can't find your gate — leaves its reason in the box, in the same words you heard.
