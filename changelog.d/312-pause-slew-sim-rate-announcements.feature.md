The app now tells you when the sim pauses or unpauses (the Esc menu), when active pause turns on or off, when slew mode turns on or off, and when the sim rate changes ("Sim rate 4x", "Sim rate 0.5x"). Opening the Esc menu during active pause ends active pause, and the app says so: "Sim paused. Active pause off". Nothing is spoken while a flight loads, or for the state the sim is already in when the app connects.
