The A380 First Officer can now secure the aircraft. A new Securing flow and Securing the Aircraft checklist shut the jet down to cold and dark — crew oxygen, the ADIRS, the emergency exit and no smoking signs, the last exterior lights, APU bleed, ground power, the APU and finally all four batteries, in that order. Parking no longer does the power-down itself, so it leaves the APU running and the gate powered the way it always intended to: previously, finishing Parking switched the APU generators on and then removed every power source two steps later.
