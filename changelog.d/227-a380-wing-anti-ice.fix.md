Wing anti-ice on the A380 now actually switches on. Selecting it wrote a variable this aircraft doesn't use — a leftover from the A320, which wires the same button differently — so the real switch never moved and the wings were never selected for anti-ice, with nothing to tell you. It now operates the switch the cockpit pushbutton operates, and reports the switch's true position even when you move it in the flight deck. Note that FlyByWire has not yet modelled the A380's wing anti-ice air supply, so the separate "Wing Anti-Ice Flowing" readout will still show no flow — that part is the aircraft, not us.
