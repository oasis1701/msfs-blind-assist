Grouped ECAM cautions on the A320 no longer have a stray letter read out in the middle of
them. A circuit-breaker caution was being spoken as "C/B **m** TRIPPED ON OVHD PNL", and
the same spare letter turned up in every message with a highlighted title — "BRAKES m HOT",
"T.O m AUTO BRK". The title's closing formatting code was leaving the letter behind as a
word of its own.
