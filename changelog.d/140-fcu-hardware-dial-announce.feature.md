Turning an FCU knob on your hardware — a MobiFlight or FSUIPC dial, or the cockpit knob itself —
now speaks the new value on the FlyByWire A380, A32NX and A330, the way the PMDG 777's MCP
already did: heading, speed or Mach, altitude, vertical speed and flight path angle, so you no
longer have to press the readout hotkey after every turn to find out where the knob ended up.
Only a value the FCU window actually shows is spoken: while a window shows dashes it stays quiet,
so taxiing, the take-off roll and a managed climb don't call out your own heading, speed or climb
rate. Mute any of them from the Ctrl+M monitor manager.
