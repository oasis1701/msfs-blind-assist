The PMDG 777 now announces stabiliser trim in units rather than degrees, so it matches the
number the FMC asks for and the number on the control-stand indicator.

Trim was being read straight from the simulator, which reports it in degrees — a different scale
from the one the aircraft uses everywhere else. Setting takeoff trim therefore meant converting
in your head from the value the FMC gave you, and getting it wrong is an over-rotation. The
callout is now "Trim 5.25 units" and you can simply trim until it matches the FMC.

It steps a quarter unit at a time, matching the graduations on the indicator, so a slow trim
wheel no longer produces a stream of speech. There is no "up" or "down" in the callout — on a
scale running from zero the number says it on its own. The 737 already worked this way; only the
777 was left converting.
