On the FlyByWire A32NX, the VFE and VS speed readouts were quietly telling you the wrong number. A FlyByWire change on 11 September stopped the aircraft publishing those two speeds, and because nothing was renamed the keys kept answering — with whatever the value was before it went stale, usually zero. Both now read the speeds off the aircraft's own flight augmentation computers, the same source the speed tape on the PFD uses, and say "not available" when the computers have nothing to give rather than reading out a number that is not true.

The VS key now says "stall warning speed", because that is what it now gives you. The speed it used to read no longer exists anywhere on the aircraft, and the stall warning speed is the closest one that does — so it is named honestly instead of being passed off as the old one.

The A380 is unaffected and its readouts are unchanged.
