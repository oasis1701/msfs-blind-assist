Taxi guidance: no more "Crossing runway" call about a hold line you have already left behind. If you stopped at a runway's hold line after landing, planned your route to the gate from there and taxied away, BA said "Crossing runway 01C at Y5." about 20 metres later, although you crossed no runway. It now says nothing about that line. Once you have crossed its runway, a hold line you have passed is no longer spoken about a second time either, and you no longer hear "Passing the runway 08R at D hold line." while you are crossing that runway after a turn. A runway your route still crosses is announced as before, including "Crossing runway 28R at A6." after you press Continue at its hold line.
