More A380 controls that the same FlyByWire update had quietly disconnected now work again. The
heading knob's push and pull, the vertical speed pull, and the Standard/QNH selection on both
Captain and First Officer altimeters had all stopped reaching the aircraft — pressing them did
nothing at all, and because the readouts still showed the aircraft's true state there was no
sign anything was wrong.

Switching something off now works on the first press. Buttons that toggle — the ND display
filters, Localizer, Approach — could previously be switched on but not back off, because the
second press was being discarded before it reached the simulator.

Cruise Altitude Mode now actually appears on the PFD readout; it was being decoded but never
shown anywhere.
