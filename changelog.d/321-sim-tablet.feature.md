MSFS 2024 only: the sim's own tablet, for MSFS 2024's default aircraft, opens from Extras, In-Sim Tablet (FS2024 Native). You can read and use its Flight Planner and its Aircraft pages (mass and balance, performance, checklists) with your screen reader. On the Flight Planner, "Load latest SimBrief plan" fills in your latest SimBrief route, cruise altitude and runways with one press and tells you what loaded; sending it to the aircraft stays your own press (Send, then Send to avionics), so you can check it first. Aircraft with their own tablet and flight computer (PMDG, Fenix, FlyByWire, iFly, MD-11 and the others) keep using their own on Input Shift+T: never send a flight plan to them from the in-sim tablet.
