MSFS 2024 only: the sim's own tablet opens from Extras, In-Sim Tablet (FS2024 Native), on every aircraft, beside the aircraft's own tablet (Input Shift+T still opens that one). You can read and use its Flight Planner, Aircraft pages (mass and balance, performance, checklists), Ground Services and its other apps with your screen reader. On the Flight Planner, "Load latest SimBrief plan" fills in your latest SimBrief route, cruise altitude and runways with one press and tells you what loaded; sending it to the aircraft stays your own press (Send, then Send to avionics), so you can check it first.
