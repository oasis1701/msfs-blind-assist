Visual guidance (Output Ctrl+V) now gets you onto final and down it. When you press it, BA tells
you how it will join final: "Joining final" holds the heading you or ATC chose and counts down to
"Turn onto final"; "Vectoring to final" picks the heading for you, the gentlest cut that lines you
up at least 6 miles out, or leads you round a simple pattern from the far side of the field with
"Turn base". It allows for your speed and expects you to slow down for the turns, as you normally
would.

The pitch tone now flies a real glide path, set from the nose attitude your aircraft flies level at,
which BA learns as you fly. The path starts at the painted threshold, even where it is displaced down
the runway (KATL 27R, KJFK 22R), where before BA aimed short of it. Its angle is the ILS glideslope's,
or on a runway with no ILS the PAPI's (often 3.5 or 4 degrees), and 3 degrees when there is neither. BA warns you once if you are too high or too low to make it
("Too high, 600 feet above the path"), and the mile calls add your height against the path ("3
miles out, 80 feet high"). The flare now starts about 4 seconds before touchdown and eases your
descent to a gentle touchdown: "Prepare to flare" warns you about 4 seconds before it, and "Flare"
comes just before it starts, so you raise the nose as the tone rises. In a crosswind BA keeps your
path on the centreline all the way down; straighten the nose with the rudder in the flare. Press F
at any time to hear where you are ("Joining a 6.4 mile final"); before final it also says where the
centreline is from you ("Centreline 3.0 miles to your right", or "ahead" on a base leg). Bank
callouts are said the moment the number changes, with "Matched, level" when you are tracking the
line.
