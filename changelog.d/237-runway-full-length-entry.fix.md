A clearance to a runway's full-length holding point is no longer rejected as not reaching the runway. At OMDB, SayIntentions routes departures to runway 12R via taxiway K1, which is correct — but the sim's own data models 12R as starting 700 metres further down, past its displaced threshold, so taxi guidance called the clearance wrong and told you to reprogram it. A route that ends on the runway's own centreline, short of the threshold the data records, is now recognised as the full-length line-up point: you get a short note saying where it ends instead of a warning, and steering guidance works from there. Clearances that really do end on a taxiway alongside the runway are still flagged exactly as before.
