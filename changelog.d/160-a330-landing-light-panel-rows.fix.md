The Headwind A330's Exterior Lighting panel no longer shows the A320's left and right
landing-light switches. The A330 has a single two-position switch that works both
lights. Those two rows read variables the A330 never writes, so they were stuck on "On"
whatever the lights were doing, and their RETRACT option asked for a position this
aircraft doesn't have. A single "Landing Lights" row now shows the real state. To turn
the lights on and off, use the All Landing Lights buttons further down the same panel,
which now work on the A330 too. The A320's two switches are unchanged.
