The A330's Exterior Lighting panel no longer offers two landing-light switches the
aircraft does not have. It was showing the A320's left and right retractable switches,
each with an On, Off and RETRACT position — but the A330 has one two-position switch for
both lights, and the variables those two rows read are never written by it, so they sat
frozen on "On" whatever the lights were doing and their RETRACT option asked for a
position that does not exist. In their place is a single "Landing Lights" row that reads
the real state; turn the lights on and off with the All Landing Lights buttons further
down the same panel, as before. The A320's own two switches are unchanged.
