Fenix A320 family: a new "Fenix windows" list (input mode, then Control+Shift+F) opens five windows. Engines shows the Fenix's own engine numbers, updated every second, so they match its displays. Cabin works the cabin panel: boarding music and announcements, doors, temperature and water. Failures lets you search, fail, arm and clear the Fenix's manual failures. Fenix documents searches and reads the EFB's ECAM procedures, QRH and user guide, and Control+P opens the procedure the Fenix pinned for the latest ECAM alert. Fenix options lists every EFB option and lets you change one for this session (F2), make it permanent (Shift+F2) or reset it (Control+F2).

Anywhere in the app, the Fenix now says "Cabin ready" when the purser reports it, and announces doors opening and closing, slides armed and disarmed, and cabin cautions, each with its own row in the Monitor Manager (Control+M).
