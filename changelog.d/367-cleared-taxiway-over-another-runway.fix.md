Taxi guidance: a clearance whose last taxiway crosses another runway on the way to yours is now followed all the way. At Dubai, "taxi to Runway 30 Right via K, K7" (K7 crosses runway 12R/30L) used to stop the route at that crossing, call its hold line "Runway 30R" and start lining you up on the wrong runway; entering only "K4" could leave K4 out and cross somewhere else. BA now takes you along the taxiway you were given, stops you at the runway it crosses under that runway's own name, and after Continue carries on to your runway. The summary names the rest after "then", for example "via K, K7, then M10B, M, M13".
