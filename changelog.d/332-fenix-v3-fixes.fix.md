Fenix A320 family on Fenix v3: the EFB window reads properly again, and Alt+P, N, E, S and I frame the right displays (Fenix 2.x is no longer supported for these reads). The First Officer reads your MCDU take-off speeds again, follows the Fenix's own engine readings for engine start and shutdown, and skips the A321's missing pack flow selector. "V1", "Rotate" and "V2" are called from PERF TAKE OFF whether or not Takeoff Assist is on, and TO CONFIG waits for the Fenix's own delay before saying the configuration is normal.

Typed FCU heading, speed, altitude and vertical speed now land on the value, also with the Fenix's Encoder Acceleration option on, and a miss is said. The FCU and baro knobs no longer jump the wrong way after a restart or a turn in the cockpit, and the vertical speed is quiet during a managed climb or descent. Generator 1 switches GEN 1, buttons such as Bus Tie press once like the real ones, ADR, IR and Bus Tie read the right way round, the EFIS FD and LS lamps are named by their buttons, and the altimeter window reads both sides back. Taxiing no longer brings constant "Stop, nose-FNX, Fenix very close" warnings: the Fenix's own hidden gear objects are no longer taken for traffic.
