GSX now actually prepares the stand you pick. Automatic gate selection had never
worked on any airport: the name the app sent is not one GSX will answer to. The app
also now tells you if GSX prepared a different stand than the one you chose — at a
big airport many stands share a name, so this was previously undetectable. If the
stand still cannot be pinned down you are told once and can pick it from the GSX
menu, exactly as before.
