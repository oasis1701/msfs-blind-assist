Taxi and landing guidance is steadier from touchdown to the stand and from the stand to the runway. After landing, the exit turn is called earlier when you arrive fast, the tone holds the runway until "turn now" instead of pulling early, and you are no longer told you missed an exit you are already turning onto. An exit that needs a sharp turn back is marked "(sharp turn back)" in the planner. A backtrack after landing now names the taxiway and the side to vacate on, for example "Taxiway H ahead on the left. Vacate runway.", and steers you there.

On the way out, the lineup tone now follows the taxiway onto the runway from a set-back hold instead of cutting across the grass. Hold-short points are placed on the scenery's own hold lines more often, never on a runway. Lines you only pass by are announced as "Passing the runway 09L hold line", not "Crossing runway 09L". Clearances that return to a taxiway, such as "A, C, A", are flown as given. The stand list is ordered by taxi distance rather than straight-line distance, and two callouts that fall in the same moment are now spoken together instead of one cutting off the other.
