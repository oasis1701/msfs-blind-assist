The A380's two cockpit dimmers — main panel flood and side console — are now on the
Interior Lighting panel as sliders, and the storm light sits with them. FlyByWire recently
gave the storm light its actual lighting function, so switching it on now takes both
dimmers to full and switching it off hands them back to wherever you left the sliders.

The A320's warning list picks up FlyByWire's new `BARO VALUE DISAGREE` caution and the
seven `C/B TRIPPED` cautions that come with its new circuit-breaker system, and
`ALTI DISCREPANCY` is now read out as `ALT DISCREPANCY` to match what the aircraft
displays.
