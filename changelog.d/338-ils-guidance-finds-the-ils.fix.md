The ILS report (Output Ctrl+I) no longer says "No ILS available" for runways that have one, such as Hagfors runway 18; with an MSFS 2020 navigation database it missed most ILS runways. Its course and runway heading are now the approach chart's, the same number the runway lists and the flight bag give. A runway with only a localizer gets no made-up glideslope part and ends with "Localizer course 152" instead of a runway heading. On the wrong side of the airport the report now says how far you are, warns when you are beyond the ILS's range, and from there gives the bearing to the runway threshold instead of a heading to extend outbound.
