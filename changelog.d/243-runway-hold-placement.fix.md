Hold-short calls are placed where you actually need to stop. A route that reaches a runway crossing from beyond the runway's end used to be answered with "Stop. Hold short of runway 09" before you had moved at all — hundreds of metres from the painted line, with no hold at the place the route really meets the pavement. Measured across 1,649 routes at 300 airports, 23 of the 31 holds given before moving were this, and every one of them is now a hold at the real stop.
