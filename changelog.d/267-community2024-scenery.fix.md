On MSFS 2024, airport buildings from add-on scenery installed in the `Community2024` folder now reach Look Around and the passing callouts. The app only ever looked in `Community`, so every 2024-native airport (FlyTampa, iniBuilds, Orbx and the like) was reported as "no installed scenery package found" and its terminals, hangars and cargo sheds were missing from the surroundings readout. The same folder rule now also lets the aircraft.cfg and GSX profile lookups see aircraft installed in `Community2024` and in the 2024 official folders.
