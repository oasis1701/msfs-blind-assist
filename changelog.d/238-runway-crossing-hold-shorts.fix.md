Taxi guidance now gives you a hold-short before each runway you cross or enter wherever there is a safe place to stop, including across a displaced threshold, and never on a runway — or right away, before you move, when you are stopped at a runway hold line. The route summary and the "Route changed" announcement now name every runway you will cross or enter, and say so plainly when there is nowhere safe to stop before one of them, so a runway you must judge yourself no longer sounds like one guidance will stop you at. When your route starts held at one runway and crosses a second just beyond it, that first stop now covers both and names both, instead of the second runway losing its hold-short entirely. Runways named by compass point rather than a number — N, S, E, W and the diagonals, common at small strips and water aerodromes — are now read correctly, so their hold-shorts are spoken once instead of twice and a clearance to cross one is honoured.
