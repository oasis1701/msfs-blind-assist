Runway crossings near a displaced threshold are now recognised, so you get the hold-short you should. At OMDB the first 761 metres of runway 12R — where taxiways K5, K6, K7 and M8 cross it — were invisible to taxi guidance: a route crossing there was given no "hold short of runway 12R" callout at all, and asking for one explicitly was refused with "route does not cross it after taxiway K6", even though the taxiway was offered in the list. Runway 12L lost 496 metres the same way. Crossings are now measured against the runway surface rather than the spawn point inside it, so nothing that was already caught changes, and the crossings at the paved ends stop being missed.
