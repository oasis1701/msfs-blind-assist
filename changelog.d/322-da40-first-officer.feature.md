The COWS Diamond DA40-NG and DA40-XLS now have a First Officer (Output Shift+C). Its flows follow the aircraft's own COWS checklist pages, from Before Engine Start to Shutdown, and on the XLS there are flooded or hot and reprime starts too. The First Officer sets the switches and runs the checks those pages spell out: the flap travel test, the start, the pitot heat test, the ECU test on the NG, and the propeller cycle and magneto check on the XLS. The levers, the transponder code, the QNH and the flight plan stay yours. Its checklists are COWS's own pages, read from the aircraft, including Weights/Speeds and Tips and help. On the DA40 the automatic autopilot engagement now waits for 800 feet above the ground, the GFC 700's own limit, and the automatic gear calls, which used to send "Gear down." on this fixed-gear aeroplane, are silent.
