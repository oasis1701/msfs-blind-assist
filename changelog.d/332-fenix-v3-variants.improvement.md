Fenix A320 family: the panels follow your livery. The A321 offers Econ Flow and Calls Mid and Exit, extra centre tanks add the ACT pump, IAE engines add the N1 MODE lights, and ADIRS, DCDU, ISIS and aft cargo heat controls appear only where fitted; a panel the livery leaves empty is not listed. New controls: a Flight Control Computers panel, a Clock panel, the headset, the ISIS baro push and the aft cargo temperature. Gravity gear extension is one control that turns the crank for you, and Cockpit > Cockpit Door opens and closes the door beside both door video buttons, refusing while the jumpseat is out, as in the Fenix.
