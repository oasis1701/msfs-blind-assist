Landing exit guidance no longer mistakes a drift for the exit turn. Sliding off
the centreline while slowing down — especially away from the exit you picked —
used to hand you off early and then pan the steering tone hard at a taxiway you
could not reach without leaving the pavement. You now get a steady tone steering
you back to the centreline through the whole rollout, and the turn only counts
once you are near your exit and turning its way. If you do come off at a
different taxiway, guidance says so and follows you onto it, or tells you plainly
that it has ended — instead of quietly routing you the long way round to the exit
you skipped.
