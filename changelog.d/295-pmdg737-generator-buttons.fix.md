PMDG 737: the engine generators are now "Generator 1 On", "Generator 1 Off", "Generator 2 On" and "Generator 2 Off" buttons on the Electrical panel, like the APU generators. They used to be combo boxes that could get stuck: after the APU generator took the bus, choosing "On" again did nothing. The Gen Bus 1 and 2 Off and APU Gen Off Bus lights now follow the buttons on the panel, so you can check whether each generator is powering its bus at any time.
