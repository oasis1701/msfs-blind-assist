On the FlyByWire A32NX and the Headwind A330, the Engine Mode selector (Pedestal,
Engines) now shows where the ENG MODE knob really is. Before, the app never read the
knob, so the selector could not keep up when the knob moved some other way, such as in
the virtual cockpit. Setting the knob from the panel worked and is unchanged. The engine
mode is now also announced when it changes on its own. Your own choice in the selector
is not repeated back.
