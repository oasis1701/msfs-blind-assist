Taxi callouts no longer go missing when a route opens with a warning. A turn instruction that would have been swallowed by the pause protecting that warning is now spoken in time, the taxiway you have joined is announced once the pause ends instead of talking over the warning, and a refusal to recalculate is said once per time you go off route rather than every fifteen seconds over your hold-short callouts. A refused Calculate also leaves the route you are flying, and its written summary, exactly as they were.
