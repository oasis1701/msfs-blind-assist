Overhead controls on the PMDG 777 are now named by position rather than by
number — hydraulic pumps, generators, bus ties, IDG disconnects, packs, trim
air, the center-tank fuel pumps and the window heat INOP lights, with their
annunciator rows matching. The four hydraulic pumps on the Center system read
Center 1 and Center 2, because they are not a left/right pair at all.
