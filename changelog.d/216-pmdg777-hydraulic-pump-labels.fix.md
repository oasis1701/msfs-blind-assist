The PMDG 777's hydraulic pumps are now named after the system each one
actually serves, instead of being numbered 1 and 2.

On a two-engine aeroplane a trailing 1 and 2 reads as left and right, and
for half of these pumps that was wrong: the 777 carries both of its
electric primary pumps and both of its air demand pumps on the centre
system, so "Primary Electric Pump 1" and "Demand Air Pump 2" named centre
pumps after sides they are not on. The panel now reads Primary Engine Pump
Left and Right, Primary Electric Pump Center 1 and Center 2, Demand
Electric Pump Left and Right, and Demand Air Pump Center 1 and Center 2,
with the eight FAULT-light rows matching. Nothing moved but the words --
every switch still drives exactly what it drove before.
