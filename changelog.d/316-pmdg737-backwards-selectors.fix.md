On the PMDG 737, several knobs turned the wrong way when set from the app, so a pick
could end up on the lowest position and stay there. The bank limit, for example,
stuck at 10°. The bank limit, EFIS mode and range on both sides, wipers, position
lights, pressurization mode, outflow valve, N1 set, speed reference and the DC and
AC meters now go straight to the position you pick. The outflow valve switch stays
at OPEN or CLOSE until you pick Neutral, so pick Neutral to stop the valve.
