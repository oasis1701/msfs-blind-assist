Live testing on the A330 corrected two things the First Officer had assumed. The
seat-belt signs now always reach the position the checklist calls for: setting the
switch alone never moved the sign on this aircraft, and pressing the sign alone was
undone by the AUTO position within half a second, so the First Officer now does both.
And the landing-light item no longer reports "Landing lights: ON" when they are off —
the variable the A320 reads is frozen on the A330, so the check was passing regardless
of the actual switch.
