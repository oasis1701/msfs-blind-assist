The landing exit planner no longer falls silent when you land on a different runway from the one your exit was planned for. Plan an exit at OMDB without changing the runway box and land on 30L, and the rollout used to be abandoned the instant the wheels touched — no touchdown call, no distance callouts, no turn cue, just taxi guidance reading out taxiway names. It now tells you the plan was for another runway and counts down the runway you are actually on. If you picked the right runway but landed from the other end, it says so and guides you to the same taxiway measured from that end.
