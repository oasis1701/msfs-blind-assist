The Airport Lookup (Shift+E) now shows airport frequencies correctly: ESGG tower reads
118.605 MHz instead of 0.119 MHz. Coordinates, magnetic variation and runway headings are
rounded and read with north, south, east or west ("57.6600 N", "5.2° E") instead of long
strings of digits. The separator lines that a screen reader read as silence are gone, and
fields the navigation data leaves empty, such as country, are no longer listed.
