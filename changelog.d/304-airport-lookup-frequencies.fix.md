The Airport Lookup (Shift+E) now shows airport frequencies correctly on MSFS 2020: ESGG tower
reads 118.605 MHz instead of 0.119 MHz. Coordinates, magnetic variation, altitudes and headings,
on the airport and runway pages and in the ILS details, are rounded and read with north, south,
east or west ("57.6600 N", "5.2° E", "510 ft") instead of long strings of digits, always with a
dot whatever your Windows number format. A runway pointing close to north now gives its magnetic
heading as 353.2° instead of minus 6.8°, and the transition level reads FL180 instead of FL18000.
The separator lines that a screen reader read as silence are gone, fields the navigation data
leaves empty, such as country, are no longer listed, and other empty fields read N/A instead of
nothing.
