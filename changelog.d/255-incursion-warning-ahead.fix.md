Taxi guidance no longer warns "approaching runway, off route" at every connector while you taxi along a parallel taxiway. The runway incursion warning used to fire for any hold line within 40 metres, so at airports like KMEM, where the hold lines along taxiway M sit about 35 metres from its centreline, you heard the warning at M8, M7, M6 and so on while correctly on your cleared route. The warning now speaks only for a hold line that is actually ahead of you, on your heading or on the connector you are turning onto, and stays quiet for one beside you. Turning off the taxiway toward a hold line still warns, and the planned "Crossing runway" call is unchanged.
