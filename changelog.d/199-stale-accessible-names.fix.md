Several controls now have improved accessibility labels: the A380 OANS airport buttons name their airport, its status and runway-length lines read out in full, and the SimBrief "Show Additional Info" buttons contain their correct collapsed / expanded state. The Hand Fly test tone also stops when you switch to a feedback mode without audio, instead of playing on with its button greyed out. Also fixed on the way: a repeated Alt+X on the OANS Map tab no longer accidentally arms a BTV exit, and the SimBrief expand buttons grey out when there is no additional data to show.
