PMDG 737: a flashing annunciator now says "flashing" once instead of "on, off" on every
blink. An IRS ALIGN light with no position entered says "on", then "IRS Left ALIGN:
flashing", then nothing until it settles: "steady" or "off". Every annunciator's "off" now
comes a couple of seconds after the light goes dark, so a blink is not mistaken for it going
out.

The Warnings panel has a new Flashing lights list that shows which lights are flashing right
now, one per row, and updates while you read it.
