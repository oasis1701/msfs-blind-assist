The A330's Interior Lighting panel no longer offers two brightness knobs the aircraft
does not have. It was showing the A320's Captain and First Officer glareshield flood
knobs, each set 0 to 100 percent — but the A330 has no glareshield floods, and those two
rows drove the Captain's ceiling and map lights instead. Both are simple on/off lights,
so setting one to a percentage lit it at a brightness its own switch cannot produce and
left the switch reading off while the lamp was on, with nothing in the cockpit able to
put the two back in agreement. The First Officer already avoided them; now the panel does
too. The four brightness knobs the A330 really has — pedestal flood, main panel flood,
glareshield integral and overhead integral — are unchanged, as are the A320's own six.
