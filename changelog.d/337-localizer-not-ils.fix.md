A runway with a localizer but no glideslope is no longer called an ILS. The runway lists and the EFB's Runway Information now say "Localizer 108.70, course 152" for one, "ILS 110.10, course 171" for a real ILS, and "No localizer" when there is neither. The course is now the one on the approach chart, where the lists used to give the true heading. CAT II and CAT III are named, and a glideslope that is not the usual 3 degrees is said, for example "glideslope 5.5 degrees" at London City. An offset approach, whose course does not line up with the runway, is named LDA or IGS, for example "LDA 111.35, course 147" for runway 19 at Washington National (MSFS 2024 only).
