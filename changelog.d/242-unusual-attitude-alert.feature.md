On every aircraft, BA now interrupts in the air when the aircraft reaches an unusual attitude: more than 45 degrees of bank, more than 25 degrees nose up or more than 10 degrees nose down, the limits the FAA uses to define an upset. You hear the direction and the number, for example "Bank right 50 degrees", again if it gets 20 degrees worse or the nose swings from up to down, and the recovery: "Wings level" and "Pitch normal".
