Describe Route (Shift+E) now briefs your taxi at both ends of the flight. For the taxi out and the taxi in it gives the expected route on your own scenery, from where you are parked to the departure runway and from a landing exit you can comfortably make to your stand. You hear the taxiways in order, the turns, the distances in your ground distance unit, and every runway you cross or hold short of. It uses your SayIntentions gate and assigned runways when SayIntentions has them for this flight. A short check line then says whether the route matches real-world practice, and whether that check was made against current charts or from memory. It is followed by up to three real-world tips, such as preferred exits, size restrictions for your aircraft or current NOTAMs. When your scenery has no taxiway data, the route is planned on OpenStreetMap or X-Plane airport data where available, labelled as planning only. When something can't be planned, it says so plainly, for example a closed runway, a gate your scenery doesn't have, or a runway with no taxiway at its full-length start.
