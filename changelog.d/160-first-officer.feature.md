A First Officer for the PMDG 777 and 737, iFly 737 MAX8, Fenix A320, FlyByWire A32NX and A380,
Headwind A330-900neo and TFDi MD-11: press Shift+C in output mode, or choose First Officer in the
File menu. It replaces the old Shift+C text checklist. The Flows tab runs each phase's procedure,
from power-up to securing the aircraft, checking that each switch really moved and reminding you
of the Captain's items. On the Checklists tab a line ticks itself once the aircraft is in that
state, and ticking one yourself has the First Officer do it. It never shuts an engine down by
mistake, and Load SimBrief brings in the transition altitude and level and the takeoff flaps.

Once opened, it keeps working with the window closed, switching the landing lights at 10,000 feet
and setting the altimeters at the transition. The First Officer tab in Settings can turn those
off, and can turn on the seat belt signs, the center fuel pumps, the flaps, gear up and down and
the autopilot engage. Untick "Speak flow progress" there for quieter flows.
