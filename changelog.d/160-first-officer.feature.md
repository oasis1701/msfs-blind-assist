A First Officer for the PMDG 777 and 737, iFly 737 MAX8, Fenix A320, FlyByWire A32NX and A380,
HeadwindSim A330-900neo and TFDi MD-11: press Shift+C in output mode, or choose First Officer in
the File menu. On the Flows tab it runs each phase's procedure, from power-up to securing the
aircraft, speaking every step, checking that each switch really moved and saying "Skipping" when
one did not; the Captain's items, such as the landing autobrake, are spoken as reminders. The
Checklists tab holds the aircraft's normal checklists: a line ticks itself once the aircraft is in
that state, and ticking one yourself has the First Officer do it. Load SimBrief brings in the
transition altitude and level and the takeoff flaps. On the A32NX, A330, Fenix, A380 and iFly it
replaces the old Shift+C text checklist.

Once opened, it keeps working for the rest of the flight with the window closed: it switches the
landing lights at 10,000 feet and sets the altimeters at the transition (both can be turned off in
Settings). The new First Officer tab in Settings also offers, each off until you turn it on, the
seat belt signs, the center fuel pumps on the PMDG jets and the iFly, the flaps on the FlyByWire
jets and the A330, and, on every aircraft, gear up after takeoff, gear down on approach and the
autopilot engaged at a height you choose.
