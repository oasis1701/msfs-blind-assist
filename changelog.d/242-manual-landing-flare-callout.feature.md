The manual landing assist now says "Flare" at the moment to start the flare, timed for the aircraft you fly: 30 feet above the runway on the A320, 20 on the 737, 40 on the 777 and 15 on the DA40, spoken a fraction early so you hear it at the height. Its flare tone also follows your aircraft: on a light aircraft like the DA40 a normal approach is quiet until the round-out, instead of telling you to sink faster from 50 feet down, and the rollout tone keeps guiding you down to 20 knots.
