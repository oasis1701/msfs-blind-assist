MSFS Blind Assist now speaks through every screen reader with one library, Prism: NVDA, JAWS, ZDSR (Zhengdu
Screen Reader, which got the Windows voice before), PC-Talker, Boy PC Reader, ZoomText and more. Every
announcement also shows on your braille display for a moment, whichever screen reader you use. If your screen
reader closes while you fly, the next announcement goes to another running screen reader or to Windows speech,
and BA goes back to your screen reader within a few seconds of it starting again.

If **SAPI (Windows Speech)** is chosen on the Announcements tab of Settings, BA now uses the Windows voice even
while a screen reader is running; before, a running NVDA or JAWS took over. If you hear the Windows voice after
updating, choose **Screen Reader** there: the tab's status box says which screen reader it found and which voice
BA uses, and you can now reach it with Tab.
