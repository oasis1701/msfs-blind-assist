On the PMDG 777, the fuel pumps and their LOW PRESS lights now name their side — "Left Forward Pump", not "Forward Pump 1" — so a low-pressure warning tells you which tank it means, and the outflow valve's manual selector reads "Neutral" for its centre detent rather than "Auto". In the Ctrl+M mute list, controls that answered to one name are now distinct — the two rows both called "NAV" are now "NAV" and "Nav Lights" — and the "Altitude" and "Ground Speed" checkboxes that never silenced anything have been removed.
