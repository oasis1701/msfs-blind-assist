On the PMDG 777, the fuel pumps and their LOW PRESS lights now name their side — "Left Forward Pump", not "Forward Pump 1" — so a low-pressure warning tells you which tank it means, and the outflow valve's manual selector reads "Neutral" for its centre detent rather than "Auto". Several controls that answered to one name are now distinct, including the duplicate "Altitude" and "NAV" rows in the Ctrl+M mute list.
