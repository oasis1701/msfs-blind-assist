On the PMDG 777, the fuel pumps and their LOW PRESS lights now name their side — "Left Forward Pump", not "Forward Pump 1" — so a low-pressure warning tells you which tank it means, and the cargo-fire and jettison-nozzle lights name their compartment or side the same way. The outflow valve's manual selector reads "Neutral" for its centre detent rather than "Auto", and in the Ctrl+M mute list the two rows both called "NAV" are now "NAV" and "Nav Lights".
