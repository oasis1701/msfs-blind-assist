Online stand and gate names now come through at large airports, where the apron is mapped
as painted guidance lines rather than points — at some hubs that layer was previously
returning nothing at all, so a stand a controller named could not be matched to the one in
your scenery.

Online taxiway, stand and holding-point names also work again on Windows configured for a
language that writes decimals with a comma, such as German, French or Portuguese. The
request sent for them was malformed on those machines, so the whole online layer was
silently unavailable.
