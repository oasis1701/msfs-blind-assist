FlyByWire A380: thrust lock is now spoken. When the thrust locks, for example after switching autothrust off with the levers left in the climb detent, you hear "Autothrust Message: Thrust Lock", and "Autothrust Message: None" when you move the levers and it releases. It was never spoken before. This needs a FlyByWire development build from 6 November 2026 or later; on an older build nothing changes.
