Runways at airports whose scenery gives every taxiway the same name no longer offer a
single unusable exit. KBNA names only A to H at the whole field, so runway 20L listed
one option — a backwards-peeling 130-degree turn 1,467 feet down a 7,991-foot runway —
while its four real turnoffs were discarded as duplicates. An exit is now kept whenever
no other exit covers that stretch of runway, so the long gaps are filled while
well-named airports keep the short, clean lists they already had.
