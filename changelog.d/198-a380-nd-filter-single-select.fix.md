The A380's ND display filters are back to being one setting instead of three switches. Waypoints,
VOR/DME and NDB are alternatives on this aircraft — choosing one always replaces the last, and a
tester found that switching NDB on silently switched Waypoints off, with no way to tell from the
app that it was going to happen.

Each EFIS panel now has a single "ND Filter" control offering Off, Waypoints, VOR/DME and NDB, so
what you pick is what you get. Constraints, Airport and V/V are genuinely independent on the
aircraft and stay as separate on/off switches.
