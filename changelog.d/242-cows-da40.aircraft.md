Added the COWS Diamond DA40, both the DA40-NG and the DA40-XLS, reachable from the Aircraft menu; MSFSBA picks the right one for the airframe you load. Every panel carries the controls and circuit breakers of that airframe under the names printed in the cockpit, and each has a status display that reads like a glance at the real panel, with gauges reporting their colour arc alongside the value. The engine can be read more fully than the real instruments show it: on the XLS, the fuel primed into the cylinders against what the start needs, lean assist in words, the mag check as numbers, and a start check that names what is stopping the engine from starting. The Garmin G1000 is read and driven from its own window with softkeys, page menus, the alerts list and the autopilot modes. SimBrief plans load through the G1000's own import, and passing a waypoint is called as it happens. The aircraft's performance tables and checklists are included, and every DA40 announcement has its own line in the Ctrl+M monitor manager.
