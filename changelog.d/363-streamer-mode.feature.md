Streamer Mode: adds natural cockpit camera movement for blind creators streaming to sighted audiences. Operating controls in the main panels or running First Officer flows and checklists smoothly pans the camera toward the active section (such as the Overhead, Pedestal, Glareshield, or Cockpit Door) before acting, returning forward after a 2-second grace period. MCDU and EFB windows gain an "Alt+Shift+C" focus toggle to zoom in for detailed viewing, and "Input Shift+V" (Extras > Cockpit Views) opens a viewpoint browser with an optional hold mode. Turn it on in File, Settings, Streamer Mode.
