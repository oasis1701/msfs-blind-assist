The per-aircraft announcement manager (Ctrl+M) now has a search box and a **Show** filter, on every aircraft. Type a few letters to jump straight to the announcement you want instead of arrowing through hundreds of rows, and set **Show** to **Muted** to review everything you have silenced — or **Unmuted** to see only what still speaks. Tabbing into the list tells you how many rows the filter left, and the first match is already under your cursor, so Space mutes it straight away. Ticking a row never makes it jump away under your cursor.
