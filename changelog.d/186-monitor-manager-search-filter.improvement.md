The per-aircraft announcement manager (Ctrl+M) now has a search box and a **Show** filter, on every aircraft. Type a few letters to jump straight to the announcement you want instead of arrowing through hundreds of rows, and set **Show** to **Muted** to review everything you have silenced — or **Unmuted** to see only what still speaks. Tabbing into the list tells you how many rows the filter left. Ticking a row never makes it jump away under your cursor.
