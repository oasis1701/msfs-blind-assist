Seven A380 EFIS control panel selectors now actually do something. The navaid 1 and 2 selectors, the LS and Traffic buttons, the weather/terrain overlay, the airport-map zoom and the magnetic/true heading reference all wrote a value the aircraft overwrites several times a second, so the setting snapped straight back and nothing on the navigation display changed — silently, with no error to explain it. All of them are now operated the way the cockpit operates them.
