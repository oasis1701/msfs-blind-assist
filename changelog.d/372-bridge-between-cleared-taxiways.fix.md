Taxi guidance: a route no longer goes out along a cleared taxiway and turns straight back where two taxiways you entered do not meet in the airport's taxi data. At Newark, cleared A11, A12, A, AA to runway 04L, BA used to turn you onto taxiway F2, which ATC never gave ("Sharp right now, taxiway F2"), run you 95 metres out along A12 and bring you straight back; now you stay on A12 from the A11 link straight on to taxiway A, and the route is about 150 metres shorter, with online taxi data on or off. With it on, F2 has a name, and you hear "Crossing taxiway F2." as you pass it. Where BA has to use a named taxiway you were not cleared on to get from one of your taxiways to the next, the route summary now says so, for example "From B, taxi via L1 to reach C.", so you never meet it first as a turn call. Across the busiest airports, many such routes that used to stop short of the runway now reach it. The off-route message "Recalculated route heads backwards" now starts with a capital letter.
