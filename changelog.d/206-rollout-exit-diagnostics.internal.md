The landing rollout now records the runway's exit list itself in `landing_exit.log` — every exit by name, distance from the threshold and turn angle — on the `BeginLandingRollout entry` line and again whenever it concludes no exit remains. Previously only the count was logged, so a "missed last exit" report could not be answered from the log: nothing said whether the exit list or the scan over it was at fault, and the CYYZ investigation had to reconstruct the runway's turnoffs from navdata by hand to get anywhere.
