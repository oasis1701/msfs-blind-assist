Online airport data no longer goes silent for a whole session. One of the OpenStreetMap servers the app rotates through serves Switzerland only, and answered "nothing here" in about a second for everywhere else — fast enough to win the rotation, and believed, so at every airport outside Switzerland the surroundings readout had no buildings and the taxiway name lookup contributed nothing until the app was restarted. That server is gone, and an empty answer is now checked against the others before it is trusted.
