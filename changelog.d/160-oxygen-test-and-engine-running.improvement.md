and 777 — Captain's side then First Officer's, each a real press of the oxygen
TEST/RESET switch with the oxygen-flow sound as your confirmation. Each side is its own
flow step and its own Preflight item, so you can test one mask at a time; the read-aloud
Preflight Checklist keeps its single "Oxygen: TESTED, 100%" line. The 737 Engine Start
checklists (PMDG and iFly)
also drop the two "Engine 1/2: running" verification rows — the start-switch and
start-lever items are all that remain.
