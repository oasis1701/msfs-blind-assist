The First Officer now performs the crew oxygen test during preflight on the PMDG 737
and 777 — Captain's side then First Officer's, each a real press of the oxygen
TEST/RESET switch with the oxygen-flow sound as your confirmation. The two sides are
separate steps in the flow, under a single "Oxygen" checklist line that ticks once both
masks have been tested. The 737 Engine Start checklists (PMDG and iFly)
also drop the two "Engine 1/2: running" verification rows — the start-switch and
start-lever items are all that remain.
