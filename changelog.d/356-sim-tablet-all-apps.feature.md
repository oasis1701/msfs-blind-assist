MSFS 2024 in-sim tablet: the add-on apps installed in it now work with your screen reader, not only the sim's own: iniJetways, MK Airport, AtmosphereMod, CIVA INS, GSX, SayIntentions.AI, UTL Ride Advisor, TechLog, SimBrief Dispatch and Navigraph Charts among them. Buttons say their state ("Lower Jetway 1 (connected)"), sliders take a typed value, an app's own drop-down lists (GSX's settings) are combo boxes, GSX's open menu reads its title as a heading with a Close button, buttons with the same name say which row they are on, and long lists such as the Pilot book's logbook get Scroll up and Scroll down buttons. With an aircraft that has its own tablet, the apps keep up to date, and a new "Show the tablet in the sim" button opens the sim's tablet from its toolbar for the few things that need it on screen, GSX's page among them; closing the window hides it again.
