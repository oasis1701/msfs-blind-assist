iFly 737 MAX8: the engine start lever combos no longer act inverted — picking Idle moved the lever to Cutoff and vice versa. A full live sweep of the cockpit switches found and fixed more of the same: position lights announced Steady and Strobe-and-steady swapped, the First Officer display selector moved opposite to your pick, and landing lights could not read back the On position. Guarded switches (stabilizer trim cutouts, nose wheel steering, the GPWS inhibits, flight spoilers) previously did nothing when set from the panel — they now push through the guard correctly. The flaps combo, the DC and AC meter selectors and the EFIS navigation display range — all silently dead or misbehaving before — now work (the range is now a pair of increase/decrease buttons, matching the real knob).
