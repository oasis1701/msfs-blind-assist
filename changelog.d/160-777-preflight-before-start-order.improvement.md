The PMDG 777 First Officer's Preflight and Before Start now read in the order you fly them, following PMDG's own printed procedure. The oxygen tests were the 44th and 45th of 49 Preflight lines — after the gear lever and the FMC — while the Cockpit Prep flow ran them 7th; they now sit together with the fire and TCAS tests in both. Trim was asked for before the hydraulic pumps that move the surfaces were even switched on, and again during Before Taxi; it is now the last block of Before Start, once the hydraulics are running, and the duplicate Before Taxi reminder is gone. LNAV and VNAV are armed in Before Start (in that order, as the procedure has them), and the Before Takeoff lines now read "Verify armed" so they no longer look like a second, competing instruction. The altimeter, fuel pumps, beacon and gear lines also moved to their published positions.
