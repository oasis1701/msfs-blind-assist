The passing callouts now actually speak. At the radius they shipped with, a real taxi produced almost nothing — one callout in 5.6 km at Schiphol, two in 4.4 km at Innsbruck — because what a taxiing aircraft goes past sits just outside it: the piers at Schiphol are 141 to 223 metres from the outer parallel taxiway, and Innsbruck's hangars 106 to 137 metres from the one that serves them. The radius now clears both, so taxiing the length of a terminal names the concourses as they come abeam.
