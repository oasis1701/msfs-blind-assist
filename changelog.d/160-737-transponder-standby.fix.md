On the PMDG 737, the First Officer's "Transponder: STBY" checklist item could never be
completed — ticking it moved the knob as far as ALT RPTG OFF, stalled there for several
seconds, then un-ticked itself and announced "Unable to complete". The aircraft simply has
no selectable standby position: the mode knob stops at ALT RPTG OFF whichever way you turn
it, including clicking it yourself in the cockpit. The Preflight and Shutdown items now ask
for ALT RPTG OFF instead — the lowest position the knob actually reaches, where the
transponder stops reporting your altitude — and they complete straight away.
