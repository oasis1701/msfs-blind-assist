PMDG 737: BA now reads the engine displays, the navigation display and the primary flight display from the aircraft itself, instead of describing a picture of them with AI. Output Alt+E, Alt+N and Alt+P open a window listing what each display shows, and Output Ctrl+W says the active waypoint, its arrival time and distance. Flight mode annunciator changes are announced as they happen, starting with "FMA:", and engine alerts, navigation display messages and PFD flags as they appear and go, each kind with its own row in Ctrl+M.
