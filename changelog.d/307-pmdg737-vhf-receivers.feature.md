The PMDG 737's Calls panel now has Captain and First Officer VHF 1, 2 and 3 receiver buttons,
so you can listen to a second radio: a radio is only heard while its receiver is on. Each
press tells you the result, for example "Captain VHF 2 Receiver on". To hear COM2 (on
SayIntentions, say), turn on VHF 2. The radio your mic is set to stays audible even with its
receiver off.
