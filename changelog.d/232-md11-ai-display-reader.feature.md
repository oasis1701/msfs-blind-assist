The TFDi MD-11's PFD, ND, engine and alert display, system display and standby instrument can now be read by AI vision with Alt+P, Alt+N, Alt+E, Alt+S and Alt+I in output mode, and the app switches the simulator to the right cockpit camera view for you and puts it back afterwards. Screenshots for every AI display read and the scene description now capture the simulator itself, so a window sitting on top of it no longer ends up in the picture.
