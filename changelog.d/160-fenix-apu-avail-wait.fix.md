Ticking "APU: ON and available" on the Fenix checklist no longer says "Unable to complete" while the APU is starting perfectly well. The First Officer pressed the master and the start button and then declared the item done a few seconds later — but the APU needs the best part of a minute to spool up before the AVAIL light comes on, so the item un-ticked itself and reported a failure, then quietly re-ticked once the APU actually came up. It now stands by until the AVAIL light is on, and only reports a failure if the APU really never gets there.
