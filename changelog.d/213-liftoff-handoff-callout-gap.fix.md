When Takeoff Assist hands over to Hand Fly at liftoff, pitch callouts now resume after about a second and a half instead of three and a half, so you are no longer flying the rotation without them. The handover callout is now simply "Airborne, hand fly." — said as one phrase, which is what lets it finish before the callouts come back. (If another program is holding the H, V or Q keys, the callout also warns you about that and still holds the longer pause.)
