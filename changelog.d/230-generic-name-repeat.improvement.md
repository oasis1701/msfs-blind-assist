The passing callouts no longer repeat the same sentence about different places. Many airports carry several buildings with one name — thirty hangars share a name at Fukuoka, fifteen at Schiphol, thirteen at Keflavik, and cargo, fuel and GA areas share generic labels almost everywhere — so taxiing past a row announced the identical words over and over about buildings you could not tell apart. A sentence the app has just said is now held for five minutes. The side counts as part of it, so the same name on your left and on your right are both still announced.
