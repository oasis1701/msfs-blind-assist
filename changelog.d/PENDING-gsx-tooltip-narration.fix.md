The GSX tooltip shows the ground crew's running commentary again while a service is actually
running.

GSX narrates its work step by step — headset operator approaching, inserting the bypass pin,
raising the nose gear, disconnecting the tug, removing the pin, signalling clear. None of that
is announced automatically, so checking the tooltip was the only way to follow along. But the
tooltip only fell back to GSX's own text when no service was running, which is the exact
opposite of when there is anything to say: the commentary appeared while parked and idle, then
vanished the moment pushback or boarding started, replaced by a single line naming the service.

The tooltip now carries both — what is running, and what the crew is doing right now — so a
check during pushback answers the question you actually asked. When GSX is between steps, or
when its text only repeats the service name, the tooltip reads exactly as before rather than
saying the same thing twice.
