On the Fenix A320, pushing or pulling the FCU speed and heading knobs from the panel or
the FCU hotkeys now always registers. The app used to keep its own running count of
presses and write that count to the aircraft. If the same knob had also been pushed or
pulled another way, for example in the virtual cockpit or from a hardware panel, the
count no longer matched and the next press could be lost without a word. Each press now
works from the knob's actual value in the aircraft.
