Added the TFDi Design MD-11, reachable from Aircraft → TFDi Design MD-11. The cockpit panels read and operate with spoken state ("Battery: On", "Pack 1: Off", "unpowered" when the annunciators have no power), ordered the way you prepare the aircraft; the Flight Control Panel, all three MCDUs and the EFB each have their own window; COM radios, the squawk, the altimeters and minimums are typed and read back; and V1, Rotate and V2 are called on the take-off roll. What the aircraft announces on its own — the state changes, the take-off callouts, the frequency and squawk read-backs — each has its own line in the Ctrl+M monitor manager, so you can silence just the ones you do not want. Separately, the PFD, ND, engine and alert display, system display and standby instrument can be read on demand by AI vision, with the app switching the simulator to the right cockpit view for you.
