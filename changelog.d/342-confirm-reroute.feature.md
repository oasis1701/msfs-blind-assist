Taxi guidance can now ask before switching you to a new route. In File, Settings, Taxi Guidance, tick "Ask before switching to a new route when off route". When guidance thinks you have left your route, instead of changing it you hear "Off route. New route available via …. Press left bracket, Y to accept.", and the tone keeps guiding you along your original route. Press Input Y to take the new route, or simply follow the tone back onto your route and the offer is dropped without another word. Useful when you are following a controller's clearance and do not want a route ATC did not give you. Off by default.
