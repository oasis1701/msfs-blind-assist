On the HorizonSim 787-9, the Kuro 787-8 and the stock 787-10, the Ctrl+B altimeter window now sets
the value you type, in hectopascals (1013) or inches (29.92, or 2992), instead of always landing on
948 hPa. Its Standard (STD) button, and the EFIS panel's Baro STD button, now switch standard
pressure on and off, and switching it off puts back the setting you had before.

In the 787 FMC window, Page Down now goes to the next page and Page Up to the previous one. Before,
Page Up went forward and Page Down did nothing.

The 787's Flight Data panels now read navigation values in their real units, such as "7372.1
nautical miles", "245 degrees" or "487 knots", instead of huge raw numbers like a destination
distance of 13653434.
