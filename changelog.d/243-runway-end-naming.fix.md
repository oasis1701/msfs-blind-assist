Where Am I and takeoff assist no longer name opposite ends of the same runway. At a handful of airports whose navdata contradicts itself — the row labelled "03" physically sits at the 21 threshold — you were told one runway while the takeoff reference set up at the same spot carried the other, along with that end's threshold. Four such airports were found in a sweep of 405 runways, and the name, the threshold and the heading now always travel together.
