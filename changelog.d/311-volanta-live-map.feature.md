New Extras menu with a Volanta live map window: Volanta's map as text. The Traffic tab lists the Volanta users and VATSIM, IVAO, PilotEdge and APOC pilots around you, nearest first, with clock position, distance, altitude above or below you, speed, aircraft, who is flying it and their route. You can filter by distance, airborne or on the ground, altitude, network, or search for a callsign, user, aircraft or airport. The Friends tab lists your Volanta friends: who is flying and where they are from you, who is online in Volanta and what they are doing, and who is offline. The ATC tab lists online controllers near you with their frequency and ATIS. Everything updates every 5 seconds without moving your place in the list.

It reads the map from your own Volanta, so you need Volanta installed and logged in. Start it from the window's Connection tab, which gives the app access to Volanta's map, and keep Volanta open: it can stay behind the sim, but while it is minimized Volanta stops updating its map.
