New Extras menu with a Volanta live map window: Volanta's map as text. The Traffic tab lists the Volanta users and VATSIM, IVAO, PilotEdge and APOC pilots around you, nearest first, with clock position, distance, altitude above or below you, speed, aircraft, who is flying it and their route. You can filter by distance, from 10 to 1,000 nm or all pilots across Volanta, airborne or on the ground, network, or search for a callsign, user, aircraft or airport at any distance. The Friends tab lists your Volanta friends: who is flying and where they are from you, who is online in Volanta and what they are doing, and who is offline. The ATC tab lists online controllers near you with their frequency and ATIS, and its search looks up an airport's controllers wherever it is: type KATL or EGLL. Everything updates every 5 seconds without moving your place in the list.

Each aircraft's line ends with where it is, from the location data of Settings, GeoNames tab: the nearest town, or the sea or lake it is over. Tick "Report location to nearest major city" on the Traffic or Friends tab to hear instead how far and which way it is from the nearest major city, such as "92 miles west of Wichita, Kansas, United States". What counts as a major city follows your own Minimum Population for Major Cities and Major Cities Range on the GeoNames tab.

It reads the map from your own Volanta, so you need Volanta installed and logged in. Start it from the window's Connection tab, which gives the app access to Volanta's map, and keep Volanta open: it can stay behind the sim, but while it is minimized Volanta stops updating its map.
