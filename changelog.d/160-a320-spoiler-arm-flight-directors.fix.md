The First Officer now actually arms and disarms the ground spoilers, and actually
switches the flight directors on, on the A32NX and the Headwind A330. Neither ever
worked: both writes landed on a variable the aircraft does not have, while the steps
announced done and ticked their checklist items — so you could line up with the spoilers
unarmed, and taxi out with a flight director the checklist told you was on. The flight
director button is a toggle rather than a switch, so it is now pressed only when its
light disagrees with what the step is asking for: one that is already on is left alone
instead of being switched off.
