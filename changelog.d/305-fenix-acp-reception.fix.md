On the Fenix A320, the audio control panel now has the reception switches for VHF 2, VHF 3, HF 2 and INT, which were missing, so you can switch those radios on from the app instead of hearing COM 2 or COM 3 only when its knob was already on. Each reception switch you change is now spoken once instead of twice, the HF 2 transmit key shows its state like the other transmit keys, and the MLS volume knob, which the Fenix does not model, is gone.
