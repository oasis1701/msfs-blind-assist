Ground traffic callouts now follow your taxi route. Traffic on your route is told apart from traffic
beside it, aircraft are named the way ATC names them ("Delta A320"), and an aircraft on a converging
course is announced before it gets close. When several aircraft are lined up ahead of you on your
route, you hear about the one in front rather than every one beyond it, and an aircraft parked beside
your route no longer draws "Slow down", only "Stop" if you get close to it. While you hold short of,
cross, backtrack on, line up on or wait on a runway, you hear who is on that runway and who is on
final to it — including an aircraft already in the flare — and a runway is never called "clear", only
"no traffic seen". On the way to the runway you hear your place in the queue ("Number 3 in the
departure queue"), "Delta A320 ahead is moving" when the aircraft in front pulls away, and a "Move up"
prompt if you stay put. Only "Stop", "Slow down" and runway traffic while you are on the runway
interrupt what is being said; everything else waits its turn, and the traffic summary now includes
each aircraft's callsign.
