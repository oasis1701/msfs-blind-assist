VATSIM events from vPilot are now announced by MSFS Blind Assist itself. Turn on VATSIM
announcements in Settings and the vPilot plugin is installed for you; from then on you
hear your connection and disconnection, private messages, on-frequency radio messages and
SELCAL alerts, each with its own on/off switch. Press `]` then Alt+V to mute the chatter
for the rest of the session — it comes back unmuted next time you start the app.

This replaces the separate vPilot-to-TTS application. Its plugin is removed automatically
when the new one is installed, so there is nothing to uninstall by hand except the old
tray application itself.
