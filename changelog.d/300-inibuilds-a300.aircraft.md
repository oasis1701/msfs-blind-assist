Added the iniBuilds A300-600, passenger and freighter with either engine, reachable from Aircraft → iniBuilds Airbus A300-600. Every cockpit panel reads and operates with the position the aircraft reports, sorted by system like the other Airbuses and in the order the aircraft's own checklist prepares it, and every light that can be read is spoken as it comes on or goes out, with each system panel's ECAM values in its status box. Both MCDUs read as text (Shift+M) and the tablet reads and operates (Shift+T). The FCU windows, the altimeters, the VOR and ILS radios and the decision height take typed values with a read-back, and Ctrl+P opens the autopilot window. The flight mode annunciator is spoken as it changes, the ECAM memos have their own status box, and the PFD, ND, engine, standby and ECAM pages each have one too. V1, Rotate and V2 are called on the take-off roll, along with autopilot and autothrottle disconnects and the FCU altitude window. Where no data reaches, the PFD, ND and both ECAM screens can be read on demand by AI vision (Alt+P, Alt+N, Alt+E and Alt+S in output mode). Each announcement has its own line in the Ctrl+M monitor manager.
