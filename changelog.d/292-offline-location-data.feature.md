Output C and Location Info (Shift+L) now answer instantly and work offline, from location data
you download once: in Settings, GeoNames tab, press Download location data (about 440
megabytes; the finished data takes about 330 megabytes). The download is spoken as it goes,
keeps running when you close Settings, and picks up where it stopped if you cancel or lose the
connection. No GeoNames account is needed any more, so the GeoNames username setting is gone.
Until the data is downloaded, C tells you how to get it.

C now says the sea, bay or lake you are over and how far land is: "Over Chesapeake Bay,
nearest land 5.2 miles west. Near Dares Beach, Maryland, United States, 5.4 miles west", or
"Over Lake Michigan, shore 24 miles east". Over land it adds the coast, named by its sea, when
it is within the new Coast Range setting in the GeoNames tab (50 by default, up to 1,000):
"English Channel coast 1.2 miles south". The automatic nearest-city announcement uses the same
sentence.

Location Info opens already filled, on a new first box, Where you are: the water you are over
and the nearest land, or the state or province you are in ("In: Bavaria, Germany"), the
mountain range or desert, the named coast and the time zone. All four landmark lists now
follow the Landmarks Range setting, Water Bodies also lists gulfs, straits and coves, and the
lists say kilometers when kilometres are selected.
