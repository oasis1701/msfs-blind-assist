Taxi guidance no longer tells you to stop at a runway hold line you have already
crossed. If a route is re-planned while you are rolling over a crossing you were
cleared for, the hold behind you is dropped instead of being handed back as a
fresh "stop and hold short" — which would have stopped you on the runway. The
crossing is still announced either way, including one that falls right at the
start of a re-planned route, where it previously went unmentioned.
