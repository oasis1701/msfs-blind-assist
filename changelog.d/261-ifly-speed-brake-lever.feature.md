iFly 737 MAX: the speed brake lever can now be moved from the Control Stand panel (Down, Armed, Flight detent, Fully deployed). The lever is announced once it comes to rest rather than at every position it passes, a lever resting between positions speaks how far it is deployed (or "Speed brake down" when it is short of Armed, since it is not armed there), and the panel follows the lever to each position it stops at when the auto speed brake moves it on landing.
