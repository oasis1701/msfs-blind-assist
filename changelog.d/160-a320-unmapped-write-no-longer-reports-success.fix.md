On the A32NX and the Headwind A330, a First Officer step whose control the app cannot
actually reach now tells you so instead of announcing success. Until now such a step wrote
to a variable the aircraft does not have, reported that it had worked, and ticked its
checklist item — which is how the ENG MODE knob, the ground spoilers and both flight
directors were all silently skipped for months while the flow said "done". Those four are
fixed; this stops the next one being invisible. One known case is still outstanding and
will now be heard rather than hidden: the A32NX seat-belt signs, where the flow and
checklist steps report a failure until that write is given a working path (the A330's seat
belts are unaffected).
