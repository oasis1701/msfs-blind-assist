Added the Cessna 172 Skyhawk with the G1000, MSFS 2024's own C172SP, reachable from the Aircraft menu. Every switch in the cockpit is on a panel, from the standby battery and the magneto key to the 30 circuit breakers, the GMA 1347 audio panel, the cabin heat and the walkaround covers, and a switch moved outside MSFSBA is announced. The G1000's warnings and cautions are spoken as they appear and clear, and so are the autopilot's modes. The GFC 700 has every bezel key on its panel and in an input Ctrl+P window, with the heading, altitude, vertical speed, FLC speed and course typed. Engine readings say where they sit on the G1000's gauges, and the C172's own speeds, weight and center of gravity, fuel, and standby instruments each have a key. You can refuel each tank on the ground, set the trim in degrees, and hear "Rotate" at 55 knots on take-off.
