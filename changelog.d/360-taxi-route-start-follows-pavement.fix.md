A taxi route no longer starts hundreds of metres away with the tone pointing straight at it over the grass. This happened when the clearance began with a taxiway you were not on: after leaving the runway at a different exit from the one the clearance starts with, or when the route was loaded while still on the runway. The route now follows the pavement from where you are.

Loaded on a runway, it starts at an exit ahead on that runway and says "Join taxiway C8 in 550 metres". When the first cleared taxiway could only be reached by taxiing out to it and turning straight back, it is left out and you hear "Taxiway E7 is not on the way from here and was left out."

When the route starts almost directly behind you and every stand nearby is on one side, the opening turn cue now sends you round on that side.
