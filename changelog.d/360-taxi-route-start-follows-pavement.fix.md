A taxi route no longer starts hundreds of metres away with the tone pointing straight at it over the grass. This happened when the clearance began with a taxiway you were not on: after leaving the runway at a different exit from the one the clearance starts with, or when the route was loaded while still on the runway. The route now follows the pavement from where you are.

Loaded on a runway, it starts at an exit ahead on that runway and says "Join taxiway C8 in 550 metres". If you roll past that exit, the new route also starts at an exit along the runway, not across the grass. When the first cleared taxiway could only be reached by taxiing out to it and turning straight back, it is left out and you hear "Taxiway E7 is not on the way from here and was left out." If it has to stay, for example because you set a hold short on its row, you hear "Taxiway E7 is not on the way from here. The route goes out to it and turns back the way it came."

When the route starts almost directly behind you and every stand nearby is on one side, the opening turn cue now sends you round on that side.

If your clearance uses a runway as a taxi leg and a hold short was set on that row, you now hear "The hold short set on a skipped runway leg was dropped." instead of losing it without a word.
