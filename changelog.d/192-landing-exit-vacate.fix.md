Landing exit guidance now takes you fully clear of the runway before it tells you to
stop. It used to end the route at the first junction off the pavement, which at some
airports left the aircraft barely a wingspan from the centreline — EVRA runway 18 put
you 33 metres out, tail still in the runway strip, while the callout said "off the
runway, stop and hold position" and the tower said you had not vacated. The route now
walks on down the exit taxiway until you are past the runway holding position.

At airports where the scenery genuinely maps no taxiway past the junction, the arrival
callout says so and tells you to continue ahead until clear, instead of telling you to
hold where you are.

This also fixes exits that leave the runway at a shallow angle — LPFR taxiway F turns
off at about 23 degrees — where guidance previously ended the route with the aircraft
still on the runway.
