[Before Starting Engine]
Preflight inspection complete
Safety belts fastened, passengers briefed
Rear canopy closed and latched (Cabin, Doors and Windows)
Parking brake set (Center Console, Brakes)
Flight controls free and correct (Center Console, Flight Controls reads the surfaces)
Elevator trim centred, the take-off position (Elevator Trim, Centre Trim)
Power lever idle (Power and Levers)
Fuel valve NORMAL, safety wire intact (Fuel System)
Fuel quantity checked (output F). Above 14 US gallons the gauge reads on its cap: dip the tanks
ESS BUS off, its normal position (Electrical)
Electric master on, bus voltage checked (Electrical)
Annunciations checked (output Alt+E reads the CAS window)
Strobe lights on (Lighting Switches)

[Starting Engine]
Engine master on (Engine Start). The ECU heats the glow plugs on a cold engine by itself
Glow annunciation out before cranking. MSFSBA announces it going out
Propeller area clear
Start key START, hold until about 500 RPM, then Release Start Key (Engine Start)
Oil pressure in the green within a few seconds (output Alt+S)
No CAS warnings (output Alt+E)
Avionics master on (Electrical)
Warm-up: idle for 2 minutes, then up to 50 percent load

[Before Taxiing]
Altimeters set (input Ctrl+B sets both)
Radios set: COM and NAV standby, then swap (Radios, or the PFD window, output Alt+P)
Transponder code set (PFD window softkeys)
Flight plan loaded (MFD window: output Alt+M, or input Shift+M)
Flaps cycled UP, T/O, LDG and back to UP (Flaps)
Taxi light on (Lighting Switches)
Parking brake released, brakes checked

[Before Take-off]
Parking brake set
ECU test run, both results passed (ECU panel, about 26 seconds)
   Preconditions: power lever idle, voter AUTO, propeller below 1100 RPM, gearbox above 38 C
ECU voter AUTO (ECU)
Fuel valve NORMAL (Fuel System)
Fuel transfer pump as required (Fuel System). It stops itself above about 14 gallons
Engine instruments in the green (output Alt+S)
Flaps T/O (Flaps)
Elevator trim centred (Elevator Trim)
Canopy closed and latched, no DOOR OPEN warning on the CAS window
Engine alternate air closed (Ice and Pitot)
Alternate static valve closed (Ice and Pitot)
Pitot heat as required (Ice and Pitot)
Autopilot off, flight director as required (Autopilot)
Landing light on, strobe lights on (Lighting Switches)
Transponder altitude reporting

[Take-off]
Power lever full, load at or above the day's minimum (output E; the minimum is on Power and Levers)
Rotate 67 KIAS, pitch about 7.5 degrees nose up
Short-field rotate 60 KIAS, flaps T/O
Climb 72 KIAS with flaps T/O
Flaps UP at a safe height (Flaps)

[Climb]
Climb 88 KIAS, flaps UP
Load 92 percent or less once climbing. 100 percent is the take-off setting
Engine temperatures in the green (output Shift+O). On a hot day coolant limits climb power
Landing light off when clear of the circuit

[Cruise]
Power set (output E)
Fuel transfer when the main tank calls for it (Fuel System). Set a timer: it runs about a gallon a minute
Tank difference checked against the 9 gallon limit (output Shift+F)
Engine instruments checked (output Alt+S)

[Descent]
Altimeters set (input Ctrl+B)
Approach briefing complete, procedure loaded (MFD window, Ctrl+P for procedures)
Fuel transfer complete, transfer pump off (Fuel System)

[Approach and Landing]
Downwind: 45 percent load
Abeam the threshold: 20 percent load, flaps T/O below 110 KIAS, 85 KIAS
Final: 35 percent load, flaps LDG below 98 KIAS, 77 KIAS
Short-field approach 69 KIAS
Landing light on
Over the threshold reduce power gradually, bleed off speed in the flare
Touchdown about 65 KIAS, about 5 degrees nose up

[Go-around]
Power lever full
Flaps T/O
Climb 72 KIAS with flaps T/O, then flaps UP at a safe height

[After Landing]
Flaps UP (Flaps)
Landing light off, taxi light on
Pitot heat off (Ice and Pitot)
Transponder as required

[Engine Shutdown]
Parking brake set (Brakes)
Power lever idle
Avionics master off (Electrical)
Engine master off (Engine Start)
Lights off except as required (Lighting Switches)
Electric master off (Electrical)
ELT not transmitting (ELT)

[Tips]
Starting: cycle the engine master to run the glow plugs again
Reset failures and charge the battery: Reset Failures and Charge Battery on the ECU panel, engine master off, holds 10 seconds
Best glide 88 KIAS, flaps UP
Maximum flap speeds: T/O 110 KIAS, LDG 98 KIAS. Never exceed 172 KIAS
