[Before Starting Engine]
Preflight inspection complete
Safety belts fastened, passengers briefed
Rear canopy closed and latched (Cabin, Doors and Windows)
Parking brake set (Center Console, Brakes)
Flight controls free and correct (Center Console, Flight Controls reads the surfaces)
Elevator trim centred, the take-off position (Elevator Trim, Centre Trim)
Fuel selector on the fuller tank (Fuel System)
Fuel quantity checked (output F)
Propeller full forward, high RPM (Power and Levers)
Mixture idle cut-off (Power and Levers)
ESS BUS off, its normal position (Electrical)
Battery master and alternator master on, bus voltage checked (Electrical)
Annunciations checked (output Alt+E reads the CAS window)
Strobe lights on (Lighting Switches)

[Starting Engine - cold]
Electric fuel pump on (Fuel System)
Throttle to the priming position. Use the same position every time
Mixture full forward about 5 seconds, fuel flow above 4.5 gallons per hour, then idle cut-off
Priming state checked (Priming panel says Primed)
Throttle opened slightly
Propeller area clear
Ignition key START (Magnetos), Release Starter when the engine fires
Mixture forward as the engine fires
Not started within 10 seconds: release, prime 1 to 2 seconds more, crank again
Oil pressure in the green within 30 seconds (output Alt+S)
Ignition key BOTH (Magnetos)
Avionics master on (Electrical)
Warm-up: 1000 RPM for 2 minutes, then up to 2000

[Starting Engine - flooded or hot]
Electric fuel pump off (Fuel System)
Mixture idle cut-off, throttle half open
Crank until the engine coughs, then mixture forward
Or Clear Flooding on the Priming panel to start again from dry
Hot engine: prime only briefly, expect a long crank and a rough engine at first

[Before Taxiing]
Altimeters set (input Ctrl+B sets both)
Radios set: COM and NAV standby, then swap (Radios, or the PFD window, output Alt+P)
Transponder code set (PFD window softkeys)
Flight plan loaded (MFD window: output Alt+M, or input Shift+M)
Flaps cycled UP, T/O, LDG and back to UP (Flaps)
Ground lean: 1200 RPM, mixture back slowly until the RPM drops, then 1000 RPM
Taxi light on (Lighting Switches)
Parking brake released, brakes checked

[Before Take-off - Run-up]
Parking brake set
Mixture full rich, or leaned for a high airfield
Throttle 2000 RPM
Magneto check: ignition key RIGHT, then BOTH, then LEFT, then BOTH (Magnetos)
   Each drop at most 175 RPM, the two drops within 50 RPM of each other. MSFSBA speaks each drop
   A drop of zero is a fault: a hot magneto
Propeller cycled 2 or 3 times at 1800 to 2000 RPM (Mixture and Propeller, Cycle Propeller)
Engine instruments in the green, ammeter charging (output Alt+S)
Throttle idle, engine runs smoothly
Fuel selector on the fuller tank, electric fuel pump on (Fuel System)
Flaps T/O (Flaps)
Elevator trim centred (Elevator Trim)
Canopy closed and latched, no DOOR OPEN warning on the CAS window
Engine alternate air closed (Ice and Pitot)
Alternate static valve closed (Ice and Pitot)
Pitot heat as required (Ice and Pitot)
Autopilot off, flight director as required (Autopilot)
Landing light on, strobe lights on (Lighting Switches)
Transponder altitude reporting

[Take-off]
Throttle full, propeller full forward, mixture full rich
Engine RPM and fuel flow checked (output P and E)
Rotate 59 KIAS
Climb 67 KIAS with flaps T/O
Flaps UP at a safe height (Flaps)
Electric fuel pump off at a safe height (Fuel System)

[Climb]
Climb 73 KIAS, flaps UP
Mixture full rich. Lean only with the cylinder heads in range (Mixture and Propeller)
Cylinder head and oil temperatures in the green (output Shift+O)
Landing light off when clear of the circuit

[Cruise]
Power set from the cruise table on Power and Levers: manifold pressure, RPM, fuel flow
Mixture leaned: lean until the first EGT peaks (Mixture and Propeller, lean assist), or Set Best Mixture
   Stoichiometric only below 75 percent power. Do not exceed 1350 F EGT
Tanks kept within 10 gallons of each other: switch the fuel selector (Fuel System, output Shift+F)
Engine instruments checked (output Alt+S)

[Descent]
Altimeters set (input Ctrl+B)
Approach briefing complete, procedure loaded (MFD window, Ctrl+P for procedures)
Mixture enriched as the descent goes on
Avoid shock cooling: hold about 15 inches of manifold pressure

[Approach and Landing]
Fuel selector on the fuller tank, electric fuel pump on (Fuel System)
Mixture full rich, propeller full forward
Flaps T/O below 108 KIAS
Flaps LDG below 91 KIAS
Final approach 73 KIAS, short field 64 KIAS
Landing light on
Over the threshold reduce power gradually, bleed off speed in the flare

[Go-around]
Throttle full, propeller full forward, mixture full rich
Flaps T/O
Climb 67 KIAS with flaps T/O, then flaps UP at a safe height

[After Landing]
Flaps UP (Flaps)
Electric fuel pump off (Fuel System)
Landing light off, taxi light on
Pitot heat off (Ice and Pitot)
Transponder as required

[Engine Shutdown]
Parking brake set (Brakes)
Throttle idle, about 1000 RPM
Avionics master off (Electrical)
Mixture idle cut-off (Power and Levers)
Ignition key OFF when the engine has stopped (Magnetos)
Lights off except as required (Lighting Switches)
Alternator master and battery master off (Electrical)
Fuel selector as required (Fuel System)
ELT not transmitting (ELT)

[Tips]
Automixture on: the engine cannot be flooded and Set Best Mixture snaps to 72 percent
High airfield: for every 1000 feet about 1 percent more throttle and 5 percent less mixture to start
Starter held for long means the engine was flooded; no start at all means it was underprimed
Best glide 76 KIAS, flaps UP
Maximum flap speeds: T/O 108 KIAS, LDG 91 KIAS. Never exceed 178 KIAS
