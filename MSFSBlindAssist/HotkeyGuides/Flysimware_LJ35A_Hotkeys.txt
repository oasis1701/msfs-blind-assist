FLYSIMWARE LEARJET 35A — HOTKEY GUIDE

The Learjet answers every app-wide hotkey (altitude, heading, airspeed, wind, taxi guidance,
GSX, weather and the rest — see the general guide). This page lists what is DIFFERENT on the
Learjet: the readouts that speak in jet terms and the windows only this aircraft has.

DISPLAY WINDOWS (direct Alt keys, no output mode)
  Alt+N or Alt+M   GNS 530 window (pilot's GPS, COM 1 and NAV 1)
  Alt+P            GNS 430 window (copilot's GPS, COM 2 and NAV 2)

  Inside either window the list is read with the arrow keys and the bezel is on Ctrl and Alt:
    Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right   large right knob (page group)
    Ctrl+Up / Ctrl+Down      small right knob (page)
    Shift+Enter              push the right knob (cursor on and off)
    Ctrl+Enter               ENT
    Ctrl+D  Direct-to   Ctrl+F  FPL   Ctrl+P  PROC   Ctrl+E  MENU   Ctrl+L  CLR
    Ctrl+G  MSG         Ctrl+O  OBS   Ctrl+C  CDI    Ctrl+V  VNAV
    Ctrl+T                   type an ident into the field under the cursor (Direct-to, a
                             flight plan insert, a waypoint page); Ctrl+Enter then confirms it
    Ctrl+PageUp / Ctrl+PageDown   map range out and in
    Alt+Up / Alt+Down        radio megahertz          Alt+Left / Alt+Right   radio kilohertz
    Alt+Enter                toggle COM and NAV tuning
    Alt+Shift+Enter          COM swap                 Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Enter    NAV swap
    F5 refreshes, Escape closes.

  The first line of the list always says which page or dialog is up, and every key answers
  with what it did: the page it turned to, the row the cursor landed on, the character an
  ident entry now shows under the cursor, or the new standby frequency.

  The GNS starts on the Working Title self-test page, and on that page only the large knob and
  ENT do anything: press Ctrl+Right until the cursor is on "OK?", then Ctrl+Enter. Flight plan,
  procedures and VNAV are their own page groups; inside them the large knob turns no page, and
  Ctrl+L (or the same button again) is the way back. Waypoint idents are entered a character at
  a time: the small knob changes the character under the cursor, the large knob moves to the
  next character — or Ctrl+T types the whole ident.

  On the FLIGHT PLAN page (Ctrl+F) the knobs mean something different, and it matters: Shift+Enter
  puts the cursor on the legs, the LARGE knob (Ctrl+Left / Ctrl+Right) moves through them, the
  small knob on a leg opens a waypoint entry to INSERT before it, and Ctrl+L on a leg asks to
  DELETE it (answer with Ctrl+L again to keep it). To go direct to a leg: cursor on, Ctrl+Right
  to the leg, Ctrl+D, then Ctrl+Enter twice (ident, then Activate?). To go direct to any fix:
  Ctrl+D, Ctrl+T, type the ident, Ctrl+Enter, Ctrl+Enter.

OUTPUT MODE (press the output-mode key, then the letter)
  P          N1 and N2, both engines
  E          N1, fuel flow and thrust-lever position, both engines
  Shift+O    ITT, oil pressure and oil temperature, both engines
  Shift+A    altitude alerter (the FC-530 preselect)
  Shift+H    heading bug
  Shift+S    speed target, in knots or Mach as selected
  V          vertical speed, what the aeroplane is doing
  Shift+V    vertical speed target
  D          distance and time to the destination, from the GNS flight plan
  Shift+D    estimated top of descent: a three-degree path to the GNS VNAV target, or to
             1500 feet when no target is set (the readout says which)
  Ctrl+W     the waypoint the GNS is flying to: ident, distance, bearing and time

  Speeds (the Airbus green-dot keys carry the 35A's speeds instead):
  Shift+1    takeoff at current weight, flaps 8: rotate and V2 (estimated from the stall speeds)
  Shift+2    Vmo 300 knots and Mmo 0.81
  Shift+3    flap limits: 200 knots for 8 and 20 degrees, 150 for 40
  Shift+4    Vref flaps 40 at current weight, with a flaps-20 figure (computed from the stall
             speed at weight; matches the 35A chart within a knot or two)
  Shift+5    stall speeds at current weight, clean and flaps 40, and the AFM figures at max gross
  Shift+6    gear: operate below 200 knots, extended limit 260
  B          both altimeter settings, in hectopascals and inches
  F          flaps, with asymmetry if the two sides differ
  G          gear: three green, up, or in transit with the percentages
  Shift+F    fuel by tank in pounds and gallons, with the manual's transfer advice

INPUT MODE (press the input-mode key, then the letter)
  Ctrl+A     altitude alerter, with Altitude Select (arm) and Altitude Hold buttons
  Ctrl+H     heading bug, with Heading, NAV and Approach buttons
  Ctrl+S     speed target, with Speed mode and Mach units buttons
  Ctrl+V     vertical speed target, with V/S and Altitude Hold buttons
  Ctrl+P     every FC-530 mode button in one dialog
  Ctrl+B     both altimeters (type 1013 or 29.92), with a Both to Standard button
  Ctrl+M     Learjet Monitor Manager: tick and un-tick which switch and lamp changes speak

THE FC-530 IN A SENTENCE
  Power the Autopilot Master Circuit on the FC-530 panel, set the alerter with Ctrl+A and arm
  Altitude Select, engage with Ctrl+P, then Heading or NAV. Vertical Speed captures the rate it
  is engaged at; Altitude Hold holds the altitude it is engaged at; Speed holds airspeed with
  pitch. With the GNS 530 fit the vendor provides no go-around mode and no half bank.

ENGINE START, FROM THE PANELS
  Start Panel > Engine Start: Battery 1 and 2 on, Ground Power Unit connected, both inverters
  on. Anti-Ice and Fuel Computer Panel: both fuel computers on. Throttle Quadrant: both fuel
  cut-offs at Cut-off. Engine Start: Left Starter Generator to Start; when N2 passes about
  8 percent set the Left Fuel Cut-off to Run. Light-off follows; the starter drops out itself,
  then move the Left Starter Generator to Generator. Repeat on the right. The GPU disconnects
  itself when a generator comes on line.

Airport surroundings (app-wide, named here so every guide carries its own chords):
  Alt+L      Look Around (where you are, the apron or concourse you are in, and the nearest buildings with direction and distance)
  Ctrl+Shift+L  Surroundings window (everything nearby as a list, with the airport's fuel and frequencies)
