SKYWARD CITATION SOVEREIGN+ (C680) HOTKEY REFERENCE

Application Navigation:
  Ctrl+1     Focus Sections List
  Ctrl+2     Focus Panels List
  Ctrl+3     Focus Status Display field of the current panel (refreshes it if empty)

Tip: Press the same bracket key again to cancel a mode you activated by accident.
Tip: The Crew Seat (Simulation section) decides which touchscreens open and which altimeter
     the readouts use. Both seats are always reachable: the "other seat" keys below.

DISPLAY WINDOWS - Input mode (left bracket), unless marked Output

  Shift+M         MFD touchscreen (GTC) for your seat
  Ctrl+Shift+M    MFD touchscreen for the OTHER seat
  Ctrl+Shift+R    PFD touchscreen (GTC) for your seat
  Alt+Shift+R     PFD touchscreen for the OTHER seat
  Shift+T         Vendor EFB (Home, Services, Flight, Checklists, Settings)
  Ctrl+P          Autopilot buttons window
  Alt+E           CAS messages and engine indications (Output mode)
  Alt+S           MFD synoptic and checklist reader (Output mode)
  Ctrl+Shift+C    MFD touchscreen opened on its Checklist page (Output mode)

Inside a touchscreen window:
  Up/Down         Move through the page's text and buttons
  Enter           Press the selected button
  Letters/digits  Press the on-screen keys while a keyboard or keypad page is up
  Backspace/Space Backspace / space on a keyboard page
  Ctrl+Left/Right Small (inner) knob decrease / increase
  Ctrl+Up/Down    Large (outer) knob increase / decrease
  Ctrl+Enter      Push the knob; Ctrl+Shift+Enter push and hold
  Alt+Up/Down     Middle knob increase / decrease; Alt+Enter push it
  Ctrl+Shift+Arrows  Joystick nudge; Ctrl+Shift+Space joystick push
  Ctrl+Home       Home page; Ctrl+Backspace Back; Ctrl+G MSG
  Ctrl+R          Hide or show the radio bar and the bottom bar (XPDR, Back, Home, MSG)
                  that every page carries; they are listed after the page's own buttons
  A               On an Active Flight Plan leg row: open that leg's altitude constraint
  S               On an Active Flight Plan leg row: open that leg's speed and flight path angle
  Side combo      Switch the window to the other seat's unit
  F2              Speak the page title and its text (not the buttons)
  F5              Re-read the screen; Escape closes
  Note: on MFD Home, Map / Traffic / Weather select what the touchscreen's half of the MFD
  shows (press again for that display's settings). On PFD Home, Nav Source cycles the
  autopilot's lateral source (FMS, LOC1, ...) on every press - read the label first.
  Reading the rows: toggle buttons say "on" or "off", tabs say "tab" and "selected", an empty
  entry field says "blank". Choice buttons say "selected" on the chosen one and carry their group
  name ("XPDR/TCAS Mode: Auto, selected", "Navigation: On, selected"). While a popup is open (Audio & Radios, a keypad, VNAV Constraint)
  only the popup and the two bars are listed, and the page title is the popup's.
  Active Flight Plan: one row per leg, for example "ABEGI, at or below 4000 feet, angle -3.00
  degrees, at 220 knots". "predicted" is VNAV's estimate, not a constraint; "not designated" is
  a constraint VNAV will not fly. Enter on a leg opens its waypoint options.
  Flight plans from SimBrief: MFD Home > Services > ACARS > Flight Plan Request. Each plan is
  one row: Request fetches it, then Import (when it reads "Ready for Import") loads it.
  From Utilities > Initialization, Weight and Fuel and Takeoff Data show Next instead of Home
  in the bottom bar: use Back (Ctrl+Backspace) to leave.

Inside the EFB window:
  Page / Tab / Section combos   Change page, tab and (Checklists) the checklist to read
  Enter           Toggle the card, door or setting on the selected row, or press its button
  F5 / Escape     Re-read / close
  Home lists the cover alerts first, each with Remove and Dismiss buttons: remove the pitot,
  AOA and wheel chock covers there (or on Services > Ground) before flight.

Inside the CAS window and the synoptic reader:
  Up/Down         Read the rows; the list refreshes in place
  Page combo      (synoptic reader) select a synoptic or the checklist
  F5 / Escape     Re-read / close

OUTPUT MODE - Press right bracket to activate

Autopilot targets:
  Shift+A    Altitude preselect
  Shift+H    Heading bug
  Shift+S    Speed target (knots, or Mach when the units are Mach)
  Shift+V    Vertical speed target

Speeds:
  Shift+1    Takeoff V1 / Vr / V2 / Vfto (scaled to gross weight, estimated)
  Shift+2    Vmo / Mmo
  Shift+3    Flap and gear limits
  Shift+4    Vref / Vapp (scaled, estimated)
  Shift+5    Stall speeds (flight model, scaled)
  Shift+6    Vfto and gear limits

Engines:
  P          N1 and N2, both engines
  E          N1, fuel flow and thrust lever positions
  Shift+O    ITT, oil pressure and oil temperature

Aircraft:
  B          Altimeter setting for your seat's PFD (hPa and inches; "standard" at 1013)
  L          Flap position
  Shift+G    Gear handle, three gear positions and locks
  F          Fuel left, right, total (pounds)
  Shift+F    Fuel in kilograms
  Shift+W    Gross weight and the margin to the limits
  X          Squawk and transponder mode
  D          Distance and time to destination
  Shift+D    Top of descent (on the MFD touchscreen's VNAV page)
  Ctrl+E     CAS call-outs on / off
  Ctrl+M     Monitor manager (mute individual announcements, including the CAS severities)

Standard readouts (altitude, speeds, heading, position, wind, weather, time, navigation
radios, taxi guidance, GSX) work as on every aircraft; see the general guide.

INPUT MODE - Press left bracket to activate

  Ctrl+A     Set altitude preselect (100 to 47000, rounded to 100)
  Ctrl+H     Set heading bug
  Ctrl+S     Set speed target (knots, or 0.xx for Mach). Switches the G3000's speed source
             from FMS to Manual first - in FMS mode the aircraft ignores typed targets
  Ctrl+V     Set vertical speed target (negative for down)
  Ctrl+B     Set both PFD altimeters (hPa or inches; "std" for standard)
  Ctrl+N     NAV radios dialog
  Shift+A    AP on / off
  Shift+P    APR mode
  Ctrl+J     Autothrottle arm / disconnect
  Ctrl+L     NAV mode

COLD AND DARK TO APU, IN THE CHECKLIST'S ORDER (Left Tilt Panel section)
  Electrical: STBY PWR on, Left and Right BATT on, Left and Right AVN on.
  APU: APU knob On, then Start (it springs back to On); watch RPM to 100 percent; APU GEN on;
  APU BLEED AIR on. Electrical: INTERIOR on. The bus tie closes itself on the ground.
  The touchscreens and the CAS come alive once the avionics have power.
