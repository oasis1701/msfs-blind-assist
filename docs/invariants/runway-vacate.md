# Vacate stop after a landing exit — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/runway-vacate.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md).
These rules came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. They sit on top of main's own designs (`RunwayVacateResolver`, `RolloutExitGate`), which win wherever the two overlap; the rollout's own rules, including the derived-constant tripwire that covers `RunwayVacateResolver`'s tolerances, are in [landing-rollout.md](landing-rollout.md). Their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and `tools/LandingExitDestinationSweep` is not in the repository, so re-measure with VirtualPilot before touching one.

## VAC-1

- **The vacate stop goes past the LANDED runway's own hold line even where the exit curves** (`RunwayVacateResolver.ExtendPastOwnHoldAhead`, after the greedy walk): EGLL 09L A1 stopped 22 m short of "A1, Runway 27R" because A1 runs 120 → 123 → 120 m out and the walk needs every step further out. Every gate is measured (whole-DB sweep, 2026-09-25): the line must NAME the landed runway, be ≤ 180 m out, within 80 m of taxiing, never more than 10 m back toward the runway, with a ≤ 60 m hop past it (else the old stop — KCVG 09 C), and `LandingExitDestination.Resolve` drops it for an exit whose path turns back (RJAA 34L A8, CYYC 11 A). 497 exits at 229 airports move, all outward. Same round: the handoff anchor switches to the exit's own path only where that path carries no node of the exit's name and the exit is not a turn-back (`ExitPathStartAnchor`, KCLT 18 D6 → R), and the post-handoff miss retarget uses the 11 ft/kt lead with a close-exit fallback. → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## VAC-2

- The vacate walk never steps onto/past a hold line that genuinely guards ANOTHER runway — but only when NAME and GEOMETRY agree (`HoldGuardsAnotherRunway`): hold names mis-bind between close parallels (nearest-centerline naming, 12TS), and trusting the name alone stalled 2,400+ walks. Off-pavement arbitration backstops BOTH this and `LandingExitDestination`'s chained-continuation cap (`RET_CONTINUATION_MAX_STEP_FT`): a new rule whose result cannot even clear the pavement degrades to the legacy answer. Re-measure with `tools/LandingExitDestinationSweep` (width-relative off-pavement scoring; beware duplicate-ident airports producing join artifacts) before touching any of it. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
