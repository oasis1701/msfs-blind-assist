# Runway hold-shorts, crossings and runway shape — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/runway-holds.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md).
The text is verbatim from CLAUDE.md as of `1f37801a`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. Cross-references such as "the bullet below", "above" or "under Core" point at CLAUDE.md's old single list, whose rules now live in several files: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.
HLD-12 to HLD-22 came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; HLD-20 to HLD-22 were the branch's HLD-9 to HLD-11, renumbered when it merged main, which had taken those numbers. They sit on top of main's own designs, which win wherever the two overlap; their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and of the sweeps they name only `tools/VirtualPilot` and `tools/ExitConsistencySweep` are in the repository, so re-measure with VirtualPilot ([virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)) before touching one.

## HLD-3

- Runway-incursion warnings on the route-less frames are gated by `Services/RunwayIncursionWatch`, on HAVING THE GRAPH — never on one hand-listed state. `UpdatePosition` early-returns without a route, and the rescue used to name `Arrived` alone; PR #236 added three more ways to finish on the airfield still taxiing with no route (the countdown's "Runway vacated" close-out and both backtrack endings), all of which land in `Taxiing`, and all three taxied to the stand with the warnings off. The "Warning: approaching runway X at Y, off route." half of that guard judges "approaching" against the AIRCRAFT'S OWN MOTION AND THE MAP (`RunwayIncursionWatch.IsApproaching`: the node in front, within 12 m of the heading line and no further across than ahead; OR `IsApproachingAlongAPath`: the aircraft inside the pavement corridor of a path segment leading into the node — walked back up to 40 m through the micro-bends navdata draws a fillet as, since only the last one is incident to the node and a mid-turn aircraft is on an earlier one — with the nose within a STRICT 20° of that segment: the heading line alone is blind to a turn onto a connector whose hold line is closer than the turn radius, the pavement corridor is what keeps an aircraft rolling at a crossing 25 m off a wide taxiway's centreline inside the warning, and 20° strict because the ICAO rapid exit leaves its parallel at exactly 30° and a 30° tolerance re-created the KMEM warning at every RET junction), and speaks about the NEAREST in-range node that qualifies (`Pick`), never simply the nearest; an aircraft LEAVING a runway through a hold line is not approaching it — but only the runway THAT line guards (`RunwayIncursionWatch.GuardedRunway`, the same nearest-centreline rule the line's spoken name comes from) and only from its runway side (`IsLeavingThroughHoldLine`: on that runway's pavement AND deeper into it than the node), never "on ANY runway's pavement": that first form, measured on fs2024, silenced another runway's hold line at 420 airports (90 dead ahead — EGER on 24/06 toward runway 28's line) and, since 3,020 of 80,864 hold nodes are drawn ON pavement, never warned 35 approaches from the taxiway at all (EHAM 18C at W5); a path chain that runs onto pavement (the runway side of a hold line) is never walked. A turn onto a connector whose hold line is close to its junction is seen by those two tests only once the nose is within 20° of it (one frame cannot tell it from taxiing straight past a rapid-exit junction), so a third test uses HEADING HISTORY: `IsApproachingWhileTurning` judges an aircraft yawing at least `TurnMinRateDegSec` (4°/s, the "Straighten." episode's own rate) ALSO at the pose `PredictPose` puts it in `TurnLookaheadSeconds` (3 s) along the arc it is flying, from taxi guidance's own filtered `_yawRateDegSec` — and only when that pose is NEARER the node (a turn ending farther away is going somewhere else; that rule cut 279 false warnings and delayed no approach). Measured on fs2024 with a turning motion model (10 kt, ~25 m fillets, 30 Hz, the manager's yaw filter): approaches first warned inside 20 m fall from 11,408 to 5,491 of 512,210, at the cost of 3,556 more false warnings on passes through junctions near a hold line (on top of 37,237, out of 266,003); judging the predicted pose by the path test alone would halve that cost but leave 7,790 late. Never fix a late warning by proximity alone and NEVER by borrowing the route or the off-route detector's verdict; the warned-node latch and the withheld-log set are per GRAPH and reset TOGETHER, only through `ResetIncursionNodeMemory` (new graph, LoadRoute, the accepted recalculation, StopGuidance — the recalculation once re-armed the latch alone; pinned by `IncursionGuardResetTests`), since node ids restart per airport and the rollout entries install a graph without LoadRoute; the per-node paths and guarded runway are built once per graph, never per frame: the guard names the nearest hold-short node within 40 m, and at KMEM every hold line along taxiway M sits 34-38 m from M's centreline (measured 2026-09-28), so by proximity a pilot on the cleared route N, M, M1 heard it at M8, M7, M6 … all the way to 36L. An on-route gate was tried first and reviewed out: after `Arrived` the route object outlives the taxi so the route-less warning went silent to the gate, the detector's turn windows and post-advance grace muted the guard exactly at junctions, and `PavementTolerance` (up to 60 m) can exceed the radius. Nothing is latched when a node is not ahead (logged once per node as `Incursion warning withheld`), so turning toward that hold line warns from the full radius. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-4

- Never disable the auto-inserted runway-crossing hold-shorts — VATSIM controllers expect a hold-short at every crossed runway, and silently rolling across an active runway is a runway-incursion risk (FAA AIM 4-3-18 / ICAO Doc 4444). The pass, its log line and the Progressive Taxi cleared-crossing strip are owned by ONE method, `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses`, and that method is reached from ONE place: `AdoptRoute`, which runs it and then assigns `_route`. **Adopt a route there and nowhere else** (clearing `_route` to null, as `StopGuidance`, ROL-4, ROL-15 and the landing-exit handoff's discards do, adopts none) — a recalculation that skipped the pass silently produced a route with no crossing hold-shorts at all (PHNL 2026-09-03: a route to 04R via D tagged 26R/04L/04R, a recalc 88 s later dropped all three, and the aircraft crossed all three at 13-19 kt with no callout), and consolidating the pass's BODY did not stop its CALL going missing, which is the half that actually does. The recalc adopts BELOW its no-op guard, so a discarded recalc neither re-tags a route nobody loads nor logs a crossings line claiming it did. The pass holds before every runway ENTRY as well as every crossing, judged per runway by `RunwayRouteClassifier` — which side of the runway the route is on before and after, never a per-edge intersection, whose centimetre verdicts lost P19's crossing and invented ESMX's and KORD W5's — and REPORTS every entry and crossing on `TaxiRoute.RunwayEvents`, from which the summary and the "Route changed" clause are built — and a runway that could NOT be held is named again in a trailing warning ("…, with no hold short point for runway 06L"), because an unheld crossing worded exactly like a held one left the pilot waiting for a "Stop. Hold short" that never comes; `Held` reached only the `unheld=` log field. `StripClearedCrossing` removes the cleared runway's EVENTS as well as its stops, or a Progressive leg would announce that warning over a crossing the pilot is deliberately cleared for. The designator pattern (`RunwayToken`) matches compass-point ends (N/S/E/W/NE/NW/SE/SW — 204 of them in fs2024, at 21 airports with taxi paths) as well as digits, numeric branch first: digits-only it read none of them, so live 3KS4 and RJSSE spoke "Stop. Hold short of runway N at runway N" and no Progressive clearance could ever strip one; `Reciprocal` carries the compass pairs. A stop goes only where one can: the scenery's own hold line within 150 m, else the nearest node clear of the runway (half-width + 10 m), never on the pavement of ANY runway (a walk that meets another runway's pavement may only share that runway's stop — LEBL 20's stop once landed on 06L/24R). CLEAR is EXTENT-AWARE (`RunwayShape.IsClearOfAt`, the exact complement of `ContainsAlongLateral`): a node beyond a runway's along-track extent but near its axis was neither ON it nor CLEAR of it, so the walk stepped over it and every node behind it and fell through to a START hold hundreds of metres from the real hold line — measured, 23 of 31 start holds over 1,649 fs2024 routes. The two walks keep DIFFERENT lateral margins ON PURPOSE (owner ruling): the SCENERY hold line uses the BARE half-width, because a painted line hugging the pavement edge must stay usable (SC99's is 7.2 m out on a 4.0 m half-width), while a stop this code invents for itself uses half-width + 10 m. Never collapse them, and keep the code, the method doc and docs/taxi-guidance.md saying the same thing — a scenery hold line MAY sit inside the 10 m margin. **A START HOLD counts as an already-placed stop at node 0** (`ResolveHoldStop`'s `startHoldPlaced`): it tags no segment, so `IsExistingStop` — which reads `segments[nodeIndex - 1]` — is false there by construction, and without the flag a route that started held for runway A and then crossed B beyond it latched `crossedOther` on A's pavement, found nothing to share and returned -1, leaving B with NO hold at all; the pilot heard only "hold short of runway A", pressed Continue and crossed both. The start hold sits before every node, so sharing is always safe and the label names both. A stop that guards MORE THAN ONE runway takes ONE CONTINUE PER RUNWAY (`Navigation/RunwayHoldStages`): the stages are read from the stop's own LABEL, the hold sentence ends "Press continue when cleared for runway 09", that press answers "Runway 09 cleared. Still holding. Press continue when cleared for runway 01" WITHOUT moving (state stays HoldShort, tone stays paused), and the last press resumes as before. A single-runway stop is byte-identical (`From` returns empty), reciprocal designators are one runway and one clearance, and the DESTINATION hold is deliberately never staged — a Continue there is a lineup/takeoff clearance, not a crossing clearance. The same-runway guard is untouched — a second passage whose walk meets the runway's own pavement still returns -1. a stop the aircraft has already rolled more than 10 m past, measured ALONG THE ROUTE from its position (`RouteRunwayCrossings.RouteProgressMeters`, which counts only within 30 m of the route: an aircraft further away has not joined it), is recorded, not placed — never judge "passed" by projecting onto a hold segment's own axis, which dropped holds more than a kilometre ahead (EHAM 18L at E10 from stand 284). When the only stop is the route's start node the route STARTS HELD (`TaxiRoute.StartHoldRunway`), decided by the pass from the AIRCRAFT'S OWN STATE and nothing else: never while it stands on or within the CLEAR MARGIN of any runway (`RouteRunwayCrossings.RunwayWithinClearMargin` — half-width + `RunwayClearMarginM`, along the axis as well, the SAME line walk 2 demands of a stop it invents; a zero-margin containment let an aircraft stopped 4 m outside the edge, tail still over the runway, be told to hold short there), never once it has rolled more than 10 m past the start node, and never on a RECALCULATION (`AircraftPosition.MayStartHeld`, false from `TryRecalculateRoute` only — that route is built from an aircraft already committed to where it is going; a landing-rollout route needs no flag, the clear margin and the 10 m rule refuse it). A GROUND-SPEED gate was tried in that flag's place and withdrawn: the manager's speed is 0 on every fresh Calculate and live only mid-guidance, and 3 kt is the "stopped" line, not "committed" — a pilot re-importing a clearance at 8 kt thirty metres short of a runway can still stop and lost the hold that told them to, while a recalc at 2-3 kt (above `OFF_ROUTE_MIN_GS_KTS`, below the gate) could still start held. There is NO `allowStartHold` flag: it was a second, differently-shaped answer to "is the aircraft on the runway" (`!IsWithinRolloutRunwayLaterally`: lateral-only, along-track unbounded, +10 m margin, 200 ft default) handed to a pass that asks the same question extent-bounded, zero-margin and 75 ft default, and every disagreement DROPPED a legitimate start hold — an aircraft off the far END of the runway, one 5 m outside the pavement edge, a width-less centreline's 7.6 m band. `LoadRoute`'s `landingRolloutRoute` only labels the log line `phase=touchdown`; never re-derive a start-hold decision from that string. The aircraft's position counts as the route's first point until it has rolled more than 10 m along the route, so a route whose first node is already on the runway still starts held (KORD 22R at D); no start hold is ever set while the aircraft stands on any runway's pavement. Every caller of `StartGuidance` that leaves guidance in `Taxiing` or `HoldShort` must take the start-hold sentence with `ConsumeStartHoldCue()` and speak it: MainForm feeds guidance no position frames while it holds, so no later frame will (`TaxiAssistForm` does, on Calculate and for a Progressive leg; a landing-exit re-route, which no caller starts, enters the hold in `UpdatePosition` and speaks it in that frame). `LandingExitPlanner` calls `StartGuidance` and enters the landing rollout at once, where the clear margin refuses a start hold anyway. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

Evidence: `TaxiGuidanceManager.AdoptRoute` in `TaxiGuidanceManager.Routing.cs` holds the one non-null assignment; `StopGuidance`, `BeginLandingRolloutNoGraph`, `TryEarlyExitHandoff` and `EnterRunwayEndCountdown` set it to null.

## HLD-5

- `ApplyUserRunwayHoldShorts` must bind a pick to the FIRST run of segments tagged with the named taxiway, not the LAST (counting repeats in the sequence), and judge the route against the resolved `RunwayCenterline` (reciprocal-aware) — or a same-named taxiway continuing past a crossing (KSFO D) silently rejects the user's correct hold-short pick. The pick is placed by the same resolver as the automatic pass (`RouteRunwayCrossings.ApplyUserRunwayHold`), so one crossing never gets two stop points. An honoured pick also RECORDS its own `TaxiRouteRunwayEvent`, merged back in by `AdoptRoute` (`MergeUserPickEvents`, de-duplicated by runway and kind) after the automatic pass resets the list — without it a pick on the DESTINATION STRIP, whose arrival that pass skips, was named nowhere: the summary said nothing and the pilot was stopped by a hold they were never told about. Never widen the destination-strip skip to fix this; it must keep skipping only the route's own final arrival. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-6

- **MEASURED AND DELIBERATELY LEFT ALONE — do not "fix" either.** (1) The NARROW LATERAL BAND is correct: the nearest off-runway graph node sits 3.2-4.2 m from a runway centreline at p0 and 5.1-5.8 m at p1, and at SC99 a taxiway node is 4.2 m from the centreline of a runway whose half-width is 4.0 m — there is no headroom to widen it without claiming the adjacent taxiway, and 42,661 of 48,321 runways are narrower than 150 ft. `RunwayShape.MaxPlausibleHalfWidthMeters` only NARROWS a malformed >400 ft width and never widens a band. (2) `RouteRunwayCrossings.RouteProgressMeters` returning **0.0 for both "at the route start" and "not near this route"** reads like a bug and is not: the 30 m `RouteJoinMaxCrossTrackMetres` bound is what keeps an aircraft stopped at the KORD 04L hold line, 90 m beside a route that starts along the runway, treated as the route's first point so it keeps its start hold. The "fabricated start holds" once measured came from displacing the aircraft perpendicular to its own route by up to 1,500 m, which production cannot produce. If you touch it, keep the prepend behaviour identical and change only the naming. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-7

- The route classifier, hold placement, Where-Am-I, takeoff detection, the vacate resolver, the reach walk and the END a hold node is named after all read the runway through the ONE `RunwayShape.For`: the runway table's PAVEMENT ends and half-width when they are a sound line for that centerline (finite, not (0, 0), at least 1 m, and both start rows within half-width + 10 m of its axis — EDVQ's mis-paired centerlines fail it), otherwise the `start` rows, with an extent enveloping both. A start row more than `TaxiGraph.MaxOutboardStartRowMetres` (100 m) OUTBOARD of the pavement is slid back onto the nearer pavement end as it enters the graph (`PullOutboardStartRowOntoPavement`) — measured, 3 of 812 rows are outboard at all and only the 29.5 m one is real, while LIMC 17L's 800 m band claimed three taxiway-AB nodes as runway. Repair the ROW, never cap the extent: lineup anchors on the `start` table and at LIMC 17L the lineup target IS that row. Never widen `SnapStartToRunwayCenterline` into an along-track relocator — that is the separate, lateral-only repair, and relocating name-swapped rows cost AYCH its centerline. Never read `Pavement*` directly, and never test membership against `Lat1..Lon2` alone: the start rows are repaired only laterally, so at a displaced threshold they hide real runway (OMDB 12R: 761 m, with K5/K6/K7/M8 crossing inside it). Hold-node MATCHING (`MatchHoldShortRunwayName`, nearest start-row centerline within 150 m) deliberately stays on the start rows — at EGKK the nodes between 26L and 26R are 26L's lines. Takeoff assist takes its END from the shape as well (`RunwayShape.DepartureEndFor`): designator, threshold and heading out of ONE frame. Picking the end from `HeadingDeg1`/`Lat1`/`Lat2` while Where-Am-I named it from the shape made the two answer OPPOSITE ENDS at the same point on a name-swapped centerline (measured: AYCH, OIII, URWW, EDVQ, 4 of 405). The threshold stays a `start` row — lineup anchors on that table — but the row paired with that end BY POSITION, never by name index. The Progressive Taxi "Cross at" list (`GetTaxiwaysCrossingRunway`), `FindFarSideRunwayNode`, the rollout's lateral tests and `AnotherRunwayClaimsPoint` keep their own geometry on purpose. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

Corrected 2026-10-08: the one-line form now names the exemptions this text gives, which its "the ONE `RunwayShape.For`" contradicted. Evidence: `TaxiGraph.MatchHoldShortRunwayName` (matches on `Lat1..Lon2`, names the end through the shape), `TaxiGraph.AnotherRunwayClaimsPoint`, and `TaxiAssistForm.GetTaxiwaysCrossingRunway` and `FindFarSideRunwayNode`, which read no `RunwayShape`.

## HLD-9

- `TaxiGuidanceManager.CheckRunwayIncursion` builds what it knows about each hold-short node from the map alone, the path segments leading into it (`CollectPathsIntoNode`) and the runway it guards (`RunwayIncursionWatch.GuardedRunway`), ONCE per graph, when its hold-short cache is rebuilt for a new graph instance, and never per frame: the guard runs on every position frame inside `_stateLock`, and on the frames with nothing in range (over 99 %) it allocates nothing and does no runway geometry. `CollectPathsIntoNode` walks back from each incident edge through degree-two nodes for up to `RunwayIncursionWatch.PathLookBackMetres`, on the TAXIWAY side only: a chain whose first node beyond the hold line is on runway pavement is the runway side, and an aircraft on it is leaving the runway, not approaching it, so never walk it (this is load-bearing wherever `IsLeavingThroughHoldLine` is false). A NEW graph also resets the per-node memory through `ResetIncursionNodeMemory`: node ids restart per airport, so the departure airport's node 17 must not silence the arrival airport's node 17, and the rollout entries install a graph without passing through `LoadRoute` or `StopGuidance`. Only the node actually spoken about is latched (`_lastIncursionWarnedNodeId`); a node withheld as not ahead is logged once (`Incursion warning withheld`) and judged again every frame, so turning toward that hold line warns from the full radius. `IncursionGuardResetTests` pin how the reset clears both; nothing pins that a new graph triggers it, the once-per-graph build or the runway-side skip. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-10

- Whether a route STARTS HELD (`TaxiRoute.StartHoldRunway`) is decided by the automatic pass (`ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses`, through `RouteRunwayCrossings`) from the aircraft's own state, `RouteRunwayCrossings.AircraftPosition`, and nothing else: not while it stands on or within the clear margin of any runway (`RunwayWithinClearMargin`: half-width + `RolloutExitGate.RunwayClearMarginM`, along the axis as well), not once it has rolled more than 10 m past the start node, and not on a recalculation (`AircraftPosition.MayStartHeld`, false from `TryRecalculateRoute` alone, through `recalculation: true`: that route is built from an aircraft already committed to where it is going). Never put a ground-speed gate in its place: one was tried (PR #243) and withdrawn, because the manager's speed is 0 on every fresh Calculate and live only mid-guidance, and 3 kt is the codebase's "stopped" line, not a "committed" one. Never bring back an `allowStartHold` flag, a second, differently-shaped answer to "is the aircraft on the runway" that dropped a legitimate start hold wherever the two answers disagreed. Never re-derive the decision from `LoadRoute`'s `landingRolloutRoute`, which only labels the crossings log line `phase=touchdown`. A landing-rollout route needs no flag: the clear margin refuses the hold on or beside the pavement, and the 10 m rule once the aircraft has rolled on. The tests build `AircraftPosition` themselves, so the `TryRecalculateRoute` wiring is unpinned. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-11

- A stop that guards MORE THAN ONE runway takes ONE Continue per runway (`Navigation/RunwayHoldStages`, read from the stop's own LABEL; `TaxiGuidanceManager.ContinuePastHoldShort`). A press with a runway still outstanding answers `RunwayHoldStages.ComposeAdvance` ("Runway 09 cleared. Still holding. Press continue when cleared for runway 01.") WITHOUT moving the aircraft: the state stays `HoldShort` and the tone stays paused, which is why it says "Still holding" and not "Continuing". The last press resumes as before. Never stage the DESTINATION hold (`_holdShortAtDestination`): a Continue there is a lineup or takeoff clearance handed to the lineup state machine, not a crossing clearance, so a destination stop that also guards another runway keeps its single press, a deliberate limit recorded rather than guessed at. Only the "Still holding" wording is tested; neither the standstill nor the destination exemption is. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## HLD-12

- **A runway-destination hold node found ON runway pavement is moved back** (`TruncateToHoldShort` Pass 1.25): to the latest route node clear of every runway (half-width + `RunwayShape.RunwayClearMarginM`) within 200 m; none → unchanged. ## HLD-13

- **The destination hold is a hold that can BE this runway's line** (`TruncateToHoldShort` Pass 1, `CanBeDestinationHold`): within `DEST_HOLD_NEAR_M` (200 m of route) of the runway entry whatever it is labelled, or within `DEST_HOLD_MAX_BACK_M` (1,000 m) when it names this runway (either end) or no runway; otherwise the synthetic back-off places the stop. The latest hold ANYWHERE on the route used to win: WIII 06 held at "runway 07L at N8M" 612-868 m out, RPLL 31 at the 13-end hold 2.5 km out, OTBH 16L 6 km out (26 of ~2,000 simulated departures). ## HLD-15

- **Measured and REJECTED for the off-route warning (do not rebuild without a new idea):** voiding the tracking silence when a hold line is dead ahead (≤ 25°) and the route's steering target points elsewhere — 9 silent wrong turns fixed, 204 false warnings on correctly flown taxis; even gated on the aircraft already ≥ 6 m off the route and the line within 40 m, 148 false warnings for 11 fixes. ## HLD-16

- The automatic runway hold pass, its `Route crossings:` log line and the Progressive Taxi cleared-crossing strip are ONE method, `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses`, called by BOTH `LoadRoute` (phase `load`, or `touchdown` when `allowStartHold` is false) and `TryRecalculateRoute` (phase `recalc`), with the remapped user picks re-applied on both — a recalculated route is built with every `IsHoldShortPoint` false, so a LoadRoute-only pass left an off-route recalc rolling across active runways (2026-08-28 fix). Picks run BEFORE the pass; a pick that meets its runway but finds no stop is RETRIED after it (`RetryDeferredRunwayPicks`) and only then warned about — warning early said "no safe place to hold short" for a pick the finished route holds at a stop shared with a runway just in front (review finding on PR #238). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md) (Main's HLD-4 has since made `AdoptRoute` the one caller of `ApplyAutoHoldShortPasses`, and HLD-10 replaced the `allowStartHold` flag; what this rule adds is the pick ordering and the deferred-pick retry.)

## HLD-17

- A runway the route enters or crosses with NO safe stop must be SAID — "no hold short point for runway X" (`DescribeUnheldRunways`) — and it, the route-start warning and any hold-short pick that could not be set are published as `LastRouteStartWarning` and spoken in the form's ONE standstill utterance after `StartGuidance` (and a Progressive leg's opening utterance), never only in the summary: StartGuidance's first callout interrupts the summary (the reach warning's 2026-06-13 lesson, which the route-start warning had silently repeated). A stop the aircraft has already PASSED (`TaxiRouteRunwayEvent.Passed`) is NOT announced as missing — "no hold short point for runway 04L" to a pilot already crossing 04L invites a stop on the runway.
