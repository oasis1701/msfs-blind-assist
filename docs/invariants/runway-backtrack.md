# Runway backtrack after landing — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/runway-backtrack.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md).
These rules came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. They sit on top of main's own designs (the runway-end countdown, `RolloutExitGate`), which win wherever the two overlap. Their figures were measured on the owner's local build; re-measure with VirtualPilot before touching one. The landing-rollout rules about the runway-end countdown are in [landing-rollout.md](landing-rollout.md) and [landing-exits.md](landing-exits.md).

## BTK-1

- **"(backtrack required)" and the guided backtrack share ONE rule** (`LandingExitBacktrack`): End exit + does not vacate + NO way off this runway's pavement without standing on it > 100 m behind the junction + an earlier exit that vacates. Never drop the forward-way-off gate (the naive rule was wrong for 124 of 308 exits DB-wide) and never widen it to "clear of every runway" (mislabels 24 crossing-runway fields) — and it must never be able to engage on a RET. The rollout intercept (`TryStartPlannedBacktrack`) runs BEFORE the normal handoff and keys on the 150 ft DISTANCE, not the turn-now latch; the backtrack target is where the route to the via exit leaves the runway, not the via junction. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## BTK-2

- "Runway vacated." (backtrack handoff) is a SAFETY CLAIM: the backtrack connection node must be clear of every runway corridor (`IsClearOfAllRunwayCorridors`), and the phrase is spoken only when the aircraft itself is genuinely clear — otherwise hand into the `_arrivedRunwayClearing` phase. The old unfiltered node scan preferred on-centerline junction nodes and announced "vacated" dead-centre on the pavement. **But a corridor-clear node is passed ABEAM, never driven over, so the hand-off must never again need the aircraft within 25 m of it** (CYYZ 05, 2026-10-03: the node sat on H 70-88 m off the centreline, the tone steered the centreline, the pilot passed it at 69 m and stopped on the runway with nothing more said). The connection-node leg is `Navigation/BacktrackConnectionHandoff`: inside 200 m, once come round onto the backtrack heading, the callout names the taxiway AND the side ("Taxiway H ahead on the left. Vacate runway." — name via `TaxiGraph.PreferredTaxiwayNameAt`, the hold-node ranking; side from the bearing to the node with the exit turn direction's 10° floor), the tone swings onto the bearing to the node, and the hand-off is clear-of-every-corridor (vacated, anywhere) OR within 25 m (clearing phase when still on pavement). Before the approach is announced nothing changes, or a pilot backtracking on the grass beside the runway (LGZA) is told "vacated". Both entries into the clearing phase raise `PositionStreamRequired` AFTER `SetState(Arrived)` — MainForm stops the stream on `Arrived` inside `SetState`, and the phase is driven from that stream, so it used to receive no frame at all. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
