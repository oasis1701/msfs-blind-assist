# Landing assist, touchdown and go-around — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/landing-touchdown.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md).
The text is verbatim from CLAUDE.md as of `1f37801a`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. Cross-references such as "the bullet below", "above" or "under Core" point at CLAUDE.md's old single list, whose rules now live in several files: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.

## ROL-1

- The manual landing assist hands its rollout tone to taxi guidance SILENTLY on the frame taxi guidance leaves a landing rollout for anything but a route reload or a stop (`LandingFlareAssistManager.StepTaxiHandover`, fed from `StateChanged` and yielded after the taxi position update) — never by widening `IsLandingExitTaxiSteering` to count backtracking, which ended the two-tone overlap but let the assist's interrupting "Rollout guidance complete" cut "End of runway … Turn around" off within a frame. A Taxi Stop is not a takeover (the assist is independent of taxi guidance), and the assist's own speed or turn end is QUEUED whenever taxi guidance ran during the rollout, so it never clips a countdown, "Runway vacated…" or closure sentence. Since 2026-10-15: during a landing rollout the assist ends only at a stop, silently, or by the takeover, taking turns on the tone with exit guidance by ownership ([ROL-32]); with no landing rollout it ends below 40 kt or once laterally clear of the runway, and the 20° heading turn-off end is retired. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-7

- A go-around or touch-and-go after touchdown ENDS landing-exit guidance and keeps the plan (`Services/LandingExitGoAround`): while KNOWN airborne the rollout is held (`HoldsRollout`, unknown counts as the ground, never the other way); MainForm arms a one-shot check on the liftoff edge while landing-exit guidance runs (`Arms`: `LandingRollout` or landing-exit `Taxiing`), a touchdown stops it as a bounce, and after `ConfirmMs` (5 s) a FRESH position read decides (`Ends`), never the 1 Hz cache — the liftoff handoff's pattern, token-guarded against a response landing after a touchdown, disconnect or aircraft switch. It stops guidance as `StopGuidance` does, re-arms the planner (`RearmAfterGoAround`) and speaks ONE sentence ("Exit guidance off, plan kept."). Before it, nothing ended the rollout at liftoff: exit callouts spoke into the climb-out and the next approach had no exit guidance. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

Moved 2026-10-10 from landing-rollout.md (split for the rule budget).

## ROL-32

- The landing assist's rollout centerline cue (`LandingFlareAssistManager`, Takeoff Assist's intercept law and tone settings) and exit guidance's steering tone take turns by OWNERSHIP and never sound together. Exit guidance selects `RolloutToneMode.Centerline` — pausing its own tone — only while the cue is engaged (`IsRolloutEngaged`, through `TaxiGuidanceManager.CenterlineCueEngagedProvider` into `RolloutExitGate.SelectToneMode`): above 50 kt (except a turn toward a known exit side inside the exit's own turn window, which stays Silent), beyond the exit's turn window at or below 50 kt, and on every too-fast frame. The cue reads `ExitGuidanceOwnsRolloutTone` (`RolloutExitGate.ExitGuidanceOwnsTone`: a rollout with an exit whose last tone mode is not Centerline) every frame and stays engaged at volume 0 while it is true, so it plays with no exit guidance, in the runway-end countdown and in Centerline, and comes back when exit guidance retargets downfield; a mode switch overlaps or gaps by at most one frame. From a rollout's entry and every retarget until exit guidance's first tone frame the mode holds `RolloutExitGate.ToneModeAtRolloutEntry` (Centerline), never Silent, so ownership fails toward the cue sounding, never toward both tones silent. During a landing rollout only a stop (below `RolloutExitGate.NoExitStoppedGroundSpeedKts`, silently: the rollout's own speech covers it) or the takeover ([ROL-1]) ends the cue (`DecideRolloutEnd`'s `inLandingRollout`); with no landing rollout running it ends below 40 kt or once laterally clear of the runway (`RolloutExitGate.IsLaterallyClearOfRunway`), turn-off and excursion alike. Never restore the old 55 kt handover: it left 55 to 50 kt with no tone and only a heading cue below, with no centerline term. Never restore the 20°/90 kt heading turn-off end either: it ended the cue mid-veer and said "Rollout guidance complete" there, where a heading swing past 20° at speed is an excursion far more often than an exit, and exit guidance's own handoffs (`turnBegun`, `exitedLaterally`) already end it at a real exit. Motivating case, KSEA 16R 2026-10-15: an A300 autoland held the centerline to 96 kt, then yawed right at 2-3°/s with the autopilot engaged and left the pavement at 70 kt, 1,303 ft short of exit Q, with no cue at all — the cue was armed only by the destination dialog's manual-landing checkbox, and exit guidance's tone was silent above 50 kt. The cue is now also armed for the rollout alone by a destination runway or a landing exit plan (`Navigation.RolloutCueArming`, setting `RolloutCenterlineToneEnabled`). Their touchdown speech does not overlap either: with an exit plan the rollout-only cue holds "Rollout guidance" for 1.5 s (`DecideRolloutGuidanceSpeech`, `ResolveDeferredRolloutGuidance`), longer than the planner takes to hear the touchdown from the 1 Hz `SIM_ON_GROUND` sample, then drops it if exit guidance started (the planner's touchdown sentence covered it, runway correction included) and otherwise speaks it without interrupting. Never speak it at once while a plan exists: the planner's sentence, up to a second later, interrupts it, and a predicted "the plan will speak" is wrong whenever the planner does not take the touchdown. The flare mode keeps its immediate sentence (EXIT-3). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
