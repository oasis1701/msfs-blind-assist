## ROL-1

- The manual landing assist hands its rollout tone to taxi guidance SILENTLY on the frame taxi guidance leaves a landing rollout for anything but a route reload or a stop (`LandingFlareAssistManager.StepTaxiHandover`, fed from `StateChanged` and yielded after the taxi position update) — never by widening `IsLandingExitTaxiSteering` to count backtracking, which ended the two-tone overlap but let the assist's interrupting "Rollout guidance complete" cut "End of runway … Turn around" off within a frame. A Taxi Stop is not a takeover (the assist is independent of taxi guidance), and the assist's own speed or turn end is QUEUED whenever taxi guidance ran during the rollout, so it never clips a countdown, "Runway vacated…" or closure sentence. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-7

- A go-around or touch-and-go after touchdown ENDS landing-exit guidance and keeps the plan (`Services/LandingExitGoAround`): while KNOWN airborne the rollout is held (`HoldsRollout`, unknown counts as the ground, never the other way); MainForm arms a one-shot check on the liftoff edge while landing-exit guidance runs (`Arms`: `LandingRollout` or landing-exit `Taxiing`), a touchdown stops it as a bounce, and after `ConfirmMs` (5 s) a FRESH position read decides (`Ends`), never the 1 Hz cache — the liftoff handoff's pattern, token-guarded against a response landing after a touchdown, disconnect or aircraft switch. It stops guidance as `StopGuidance` does, re-arms the planner (`RearmAfterGoAround`) and speaks ONE sentence ("Exit guidance off, plan kept."). Before it, nothing ended the rollout at liftoff: exit callouts spoke into the climb-out and the next approach had no exit guidance. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-32

- **"Flare" is the one word the manual landing assist adds to its tones, and it belongs to the aircraft.** The assist engages at 50 ft gear height ("Flare guidance, runway 28R."), but the moment a pilot starts the flare is lower and differs by type: A320 FCTM 30 ft, the MD-11 35 ft, the A330 FCTM and the PMDG 777's autoland 40 ft, a light single 10-20 ft (FAA Airplane Flying Handbook). So the call fires at `VisualGuidanceProfile.FlareTriggerWheelHeightFt`, the height visual guidance already flares at, 0.3 s early at the current sink rate (`LandingGuidanceLaws.FlareCalloutDue`, `FLARE_CALLOUT_LEAD_SEC`) so the word is heard at the height, not a screen reader's latency below it. It interrupts, once per engagement, except behind the engage sentence: on the 777 and A330 (40 ft) the call is due about half a second after the 50 ft engage, and interrupting would cut off "Flare guidance, runway 28R, not 28L.", the one sentence naming the runway actually being landed on, so a call due inside the engage sentence's ESTIMATED speaking time (1 s, 3 s with a correction; not measured) is queued behind it (`DecideFlareCallout`). A bounce back into the flare does not repeat it; under a silent flare visual guidance speaks its own "Flare" at the same height, and taking the tones over from visual guidance below that height counts it as said. A call that would come more than 8 ft below the height (a float that drifted down) is dropped as too late to act on. The flare's sink-rate law (`FlareSinkTauSec`, `FlareTouchdownSinkFpm`, `FlareHoldsEntrySink`) and the rollout tone's end speed (`RolloutGuidanceEndKnots`) come from the same profile: as constants they were a jet's (6 s, 120 fpm, 40 kt), which told a DA40 on a normal 380 fpm approach that it was floating from 50 ft down and ended its rollout tone a few seconds after a 55 kt touchdown. The jets' defaults are unchanged (added 2026-10-12, PR #242). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
