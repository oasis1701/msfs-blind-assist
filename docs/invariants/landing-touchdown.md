# Landing assist, touchdown and go-around — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/landing-touchdown.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md).
The text is verbatim from CLAUDE.md as of `1f37801a`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. Cross-references such as "the bullet below", "above" or "under Core" point at CLAUDE.md's old single list, whose rules now live in several files: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.

## ROL-1

- The manual landing assist hands its rollout tone to taxi guidance SILENTLY on the frame taxi guidance leaves a landing rollout for anything but a route reload or a stop (`LandingFlareAssistManager.StepTaxiHandover`, fed from `StateChanged` and yielded after the taxi position update) — never by widening `IsLandingExitTaxiSteering` to count backtracking, which ended the two-tone overlap but let the assist's interrupting "Rollout guidance complete" cut "End of runway … Turn around" off within a frame. A Taxi Stop is not a takeover (the assist is independent of taxi guidance), and the assist's own speed or turn end is QUEUED whenever taxi guidance ran during the rollout, so it never clips a countdown, "Runway vacated…" or closure sentence. Since 2026-10-15: during a landing rollout the assist ends only at a stop, silently, or by the takeover, taking turns on the tone with exit guidance by ownership ([ROL-32]); with no landing rollout it ends below the aircraft's `RolloutGuidanceEndKnots` (40 kt by default, [ROL-43]) or once laterally clear of the runway, and the 20° heading turn-off end is retired. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-7

- A go-around or touch-and-go after touchdown ENDS landing-exit guidance and keeps the plan (`Services/LandingExitGoAround`): while KNOWN airborne the rollout is held (`HoldsRollout`, unknown counts as the ground, never the other way); MainForm arms a one-shot check on the liftoff edge while landing-exit guidance runs (`Arms`: `LandingRollout` or landing-exit `Taxiing`), a touchdown stops it as a bounce, and after `ConfirmMs` (5 s) a FRESH position read decides (`Ends`), never the 1 Hz cache — the liftoff handoff's pattern, token-guarded against a response landing after a touchdown, disconnect or aircraft switch. It stops guidance as `StopGuidance` does, re-arms the planner (`RearmAfterGoAround`) and speaks ONE sentence ("Exit guidance off, plan kept."). Before it, nothing ended the rollout at liftoff: exit callouts spoke into the climb-out and the next approach had no exit guidance. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

Moved 2026-10-10 from landing-rollout.md (split for the rule budget).

## ROL-32

- The landing assist's rollout centerline cue (`LandingFlareAssistManager`, Takeoff Assist's intercept law and tone settings) and exit guidance's steering tone take turns by OWNERSHIP and never sound together. Exit guidance selects `RolloutToneMode.Centerline` — pausing its own tone — only while the cue is engaged (`IsRolloutEngaged`, through `TaxiGuidanceManager.CenterlineCueEngagedProvider` into `RolloutExitGate.SelectToneMode`): above 50 kt (except a turn toward a known exit side inside the exit's own turn window, which stays Silent), beyond the exit's turn window at or below 50 kt, and on every too-fast frame. The cue reads `ExitGuidanceOwnsRolloutTone` (`RolloutExitGate.ExitGuidanceOwnsTone`: a rollout with an exit whose last tone mode is not Centerline) every frame, on the one-lock `TaxiGuidanceManager.RolloutCueSnapshot`, and stays engaged at volume 0 while it is true, so it plays with no exit guidance, in the runway-end countdown and in Centerline, and comes back when exit guidance retargets downfield; a mode switch overlaps or gaps by at most one frame. From a rollout's entry and every retarget until exit guidance's first tone frame the mode holds `RolloutExitGate.ToneModeAtRolloutEntry` (Centerline), never Silent, so ownership fails toward the cue sounding, never toward both tones silent. During a landing rollout only a stop (below `RolloutExitGate.NoExitStoppedGroundSpeedKts`, silently: the rollout's own speech covers it) or the takeover ([ROL-1]) ends the cue (`DecideRolloutEnd`'s `inLandingRollout`); with no landing rollout running it ends below the aircraft's `RolloutGuidanceEndKnots` (40 kt by default, [ROL-43]) or once laterally clear of the runway (`RolloutExitGate.IsLaterallyClearOfRunway`), turn-off and excursion alike. Where nothing else owns a turn off the runway (no landing rollout, or the runway-end countdown) the cue MUTES, still engaged, while the heading is at least `RolloutExitGate.TurnBegunHeadingDeg` (15°) off the runway at or below 60 kt, the fastest any exit is taken (`IsTurningOffRunway`, owner `turn` in `landing_assist.log`): without it the cue panned hard against every turn onto the countdown's end taxiway, every turnaround and every rapid exit taken with only a destination set, until the aircraft was clear. It is a mute, not an end, and above 60 kt a heading swing is a veer and stays steered. Never restore the old 55 kt handover: it left 55 to 50 kt with no tone and only a heading cue below, with no centerline term. Never restore the 20°/90 kt heading turn-off end either: it ended the cue mid-veer and said "Rollout guidance complete" there, where a heading swing past 20° at speed is an excursion far more often than an exit, and exit guidance's own handoffs (`turnBegun`, `exitedLaterally`) already end it at a real exit. Motivating case, KSEA 16R 2026-10-15: an A300 autoland held the centerline to 96 kt, then yawed right at 2-3°/s with the autopilot engaged and left the pavement at 70 kt, 1,303 ft short of exit Q, with no cue at all — the cue was armed only by the destination dialog's manual-landing checkbox, and exit guidance's tone was silent above 50 kt. The cue is now also armed for the rollout alone by a destination runway or a landing exit plan (`Navigation.RolloutCueArming`, setting `RolloutCenterlineToneEnabled`). Their touchdown speech does not overlap either: with an exit plan the rollout-only cue holds "Rollout guidance" (`DecideRolloutGuidanceSpeech`, `ResolveDeferredRolloutGuidance`) while the planner still has to decide the touchdown (`LandingExitPlanner.IsAwaitingTouchdown`: its 1 Hz `SIM_ON_GROUND` sample, a fresh position read, the route or re-plan; 5 s backstop for a touchdown it never takes), then drops it if exit guidance started (the planner's touchdown sentence covered it, runway correction included) and otherwise speaks it without interrupting. Never speak it at once while a plan exists, and never replace the planner's own signal with a fixed delay: a 1.5 s hold lost the race to a slow re-plan and doubled the runway correction. A predicted "the plan will speak" is wrong whenever the planner does not take the touchdown. The flare mode keeps its immediate sentence (EXIT-3) when the plan starts exit guidance here; for a plan made for another airport or already used it defers the same way, since the planner's "Runway not identified" would cut it off; and it defers whenever visual guidance is still on (ROL-33). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-33

- The landing assist's rollout cue (`LandingFlareAssistManager`) holds its tone and its "Rollout guidance" sentence while visual guidance is still on after touchdown. Visual guidance switches itself off on the airborne-to-on-ground edge of `SIM_ON_GROUND` ([VG-11], in `MainForm.Announcers.cs`), which arrives on the 1 Hz continuous batch, while the cue engages on its first SIM_FRAME ground frame: up to a second apart. Starting at once played visual guidance's two tones and the cue's together, and the cue's interrupting "Rollout guidance" could cut off visual guidance's "Touchdown". `DecideRolloutToneOwner` gives the tone to visual guidance first (owner `vg` in `landing_assist.log`), then to exit guidance (ROL-32), else to the cue; `DecideRolloutGuidanceSpeech` defers the sentence in every mode while visual guidance is on, and `ResolveDeferredRolloutGuidance` waits until it is off and the planner has decided the touchdown (ROL-32), then drops the sentence if exit guidance started or speaks it without interrupting, after "Visual guidance off". Visual guidance keeps tracking the centerline during that second, so the pilot is never without a cue. The hold ends only because visual guidance does switch itself off: VG-11 keeps that edge ungated. The manual landing assist's flare mode had the same overlap from the start (its flare stays silent under visual guidance and its rollout tone started at touchdown); every visual guidance landing gained it on 2026-10-15, when a destination runway began arming the rollout cue, since visual guidance always has one. `LandingFlareRolloutOnlyTests` pin the decisions; the wiring in `UpdateRolloutTone` and `ServiceDeferredRolloutGuidance` is unpinned. → [visual-guidance.md](../visual-guidance.md)

## ROL-34

- A change that takes the manual landing assist's flare tones away or brings them back, made anywhere but the destination dialog (a landing exit plan set or cleared, through `PlanChanged`, or the rollout centerline setting switched, on Settings OK), is spoken: `MainForm.RefreshRolloutCueArming` compares the arming before and after through `RolloutCueArming.ManualLandingChange`, which says "Manual landing assist off" or "Manual landing assist armed for {ICAO} runway {id}", queued after the planner's own "Landing exit planned…" or "Landing exit plan cleared.". The typical case: the checkbox ticked for one airport, then an exit plan set for another, which wins as the latest choice (`RolloutCueArming.Decide`) and arms the rollout cue there without flare tones. In flare mode silence means "on profile", so flare tones the pilot armed must never vanish unheard (the reason a database switch says "Manual landing assist off", EXIT-11), nor come back unannounced. The destination dialog (`force`) speaks its own confirmation and adds nothing; a database switch disarms with its own announcement before it clears the plan, so that `PlanChanged` finds nothing to say. `RolloutCueArmingTests` pin the phrase decision, not the wiring. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-43

- **"Flare" is the one word the manual landing assist adds to its tones, and it belongs to the aircraft.** The assist engages at 50 ft gear height ("Flare guidance, runway 28R."), but the moment a pilot starts the flare is lower and differs by type: A320 FCTM 30 ft, the MD-11 35 ft, the A330 FCTM and the PMDG 777's autoland 40 ft, a light single 10-20 ft (FAA Airplane Flying Handbook). So the call fires at `VisualGuidanceProfile.FlareTriggerWheelHeightFt`, the height visual guidance already flares at, 0.3 s early at the current sink rate (`LandingGuidanceLaws.FlareCalloutDue`, `FLARE_CALLOUT_LEAD_SEC`) so the word is heard at the height, not a screen reader's latency below it. It interrupts, once per engagement, except behind the engage sentence: on the 777 and A330 (40 ft) the call is due about half a second after the 50 ft engage, and interrupting would cut off "Flare guidance, runway 28R, not 28L.", the one sentence naming the runway actually being landed on, so a call due inside the engage sentence's ESTIMATED speaking time (1 s, 3 s with a correction; not measured) is queued behind it (`DecideFlareCallout`). A bounce back into the flare does not repeat it; under a silent flare visual guidance speaks its own "Flare" at the same height, and taking the tones over from visual guidance below that height counts it as said. A call that would come more than 8 ft below the height (a float that drifted down) is dropped as too late to act on. The flare's sink-rate law (`FlareSinkTauSec`, `FlareTouchdownSinkFpm`, `FlareHoldsEntrySink`) and the rollout tone's end speed (`RolloutGuidanceEndKnots`) come from the same profile: as constants they were a jet's (6 s, 120 fpm, 40 kt), which told a DA40 on a normal 380 fpm approach that it was floating from 50 ft down and ended its rollout tone a few seconds after a 55 kt touchdown. The jets' defaults are unchanged (added 2026-10-12, PR #242). Since main's #308 the profile's end speed applies only with no landing rollout running in taxi guidance ([ROL-32]); the rollout-only cue snapshots the profile at its touchdown, as it has no flare engage. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
