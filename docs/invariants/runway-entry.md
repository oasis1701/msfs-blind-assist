# Departure runway entry and lineup point — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/runway-entry.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md).
These rules came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. They sit on top of main's own designs (`RunwayLineupTarget`, `RunwayShape`, `PullOutboardStartRowOntoPavement`), which win wherever the two overlap. Their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and of the sweeps they name only `tools/VirtualPilot` and `tools/ExitConsistencySweep` are in the repository (`LineupPointSweep` and `SignHoldProbe` are not), so re-measure with VirtualPilot before touching one. Cross-references such as "the rule below" point at that branch's CLAUDE.md list: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.

## ENT-1

- **The departure lineup point comes from ONE place, `TaxiGraph.DepartureLineupPoint`** (PickFullLengthStart + SnapStartToRunwayCenterline + the rule below) — main's `RunwayLineupTarget.Resolve` (the taxi planner's destination list and the route briefing) and VirtualPilot call it; never re-inline the pick/snap pair (two VirtualPilot copies had drifted). A snapped point more than `LINEUP_BEHIND_EDGE_TOLERANCE_M` (10 m) BEHIND the runway_end pavement edge is kept only when a taxi node sits on the extended centreline (half-width + 5 m) between 10 and 500 m behind the edge — a starter extension (EGKK 26L, iniBuilds EGLL 09L) — and otherwise moves forward to the edge, never further. Taxi2Gate LFPG 09L's start row is 136 m to the side and 43 m behind the edge with nothing behind it: the lineup point sat on the grass, the Z1 hold node became the route DESTINATION, no entry stub survived and the lineup tone cut 14 m across the grass. Whole fs2020 DB (2026-09-24, `tools/LineupPointSweep`): 17 of 54,032 runway ends move, 9 route destinations change, every one from a node 59-276 m off the centreline to one on or beside it (KSAW 01, VRMM 36, WSSS 02L/20C, LFPG 08L/09L/09R); EGKK and EGLL unchanged. Pinned by `DepartureLineupPointTests`. → CLAUDE.md

## ENT-2

- **An intersection's listed distance is quoted from where the taxiway crosses onto the pavement on the AIRCRAFT's side** (`GetRunwayIntersections` + aircraft position; `RunwayIntersection.PavementEntryAlongLeft/Right`), only ever FURTHER down the runway, only within 300 m, and not at all without a position. On an entry angled back against the takeoff direction the node nearest the centreline is up to 250 m before where the aircraft turns on (EGLL 09L AB11, KORD 04R Y4) — overstating remaining runway is the unsafe direction. Never move it back (EGLL 09L A8 angles forward: it would promise runway the lineup turn uses up) and never pick the far edge of a taxiway that crosses the whole runway (A8, again) — the side is the aircraft's. `FindFullLengthEntrance` shares the scan and is deliberately untouched. → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## ENT-3

- **The departure-entry holding-point picker skips DESCRIPTIVE OSM hold labels** (`TaxiGraph.IsDescriptiveHoldLabel`: all words hold-kind words, optionally with a runway designator — "28C APCH", "GP HOLD LINE", "ILS", "RWY 27 CAT II"; a designator beside anything else, "B 12", is a ref and stays). KORD's "28R APCH" (a hold for taxiways crossing 28R's approach) became a "partway" entry to runway 04R and stopped the pilot on its pavement. Progressive Taxi's hold-at-named-point list is NOT filtered — ATC can say "hold short of the 28C approach". A holding-point PIN that cannot be made (no path through the point, or one gone wild) is now SPOKEN ("Holding point P8 is not on a usable route to Runway 18. Holding at the runway entry instead.") — it used to be log-only, so the pilot who picked P8 was held at another line in silence (EDDF 18). → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## ENT-4

- **A full-length runway route that ARRIVES on its runway facing the reciprocal of the takeoff heading is re-routed to the same runway's nearest node (≤ 4 m off the centreline, within 400 m either way, ≤ 400 m extra taxi, same clearance honoured, no U-turn in its last 300 m) whose arrival faces the takeoff direction** (`TaxiGuidanceManager.TryForwardFacingRunwayEntry`, `Navigation/RunwayEntryDirection`: reversed = within 35° of the reciprocal, over ≥ 10 m). EGLL 09R via NB10 (A220, 2026-09-24): NB10 splits into a west curve (the 27L entry, ending facing 270° beside the lineup point) and an east curve (the 09R entry, 117 m down); the route took the west curve, the entry-stub tone turned the pilot right, the intercept then demanded ~100° left at the pavement edge and the aircraft ran 94 m off the far side of the runway. The note ("Joining Runway 09R where NB10 faces the takeoff direction, about 120 metres down the runway." / "at the runway end, about N before the usual start point" for a displaced-threshold end — KABQ 08, PANC 33) leads the summary. NEVER for a picked intersection (`intersectionDeparture`), a named holding point, a backtrack or a cross-component route — unscoped it swapped 135 intersection departures onto a neighbouring taxiway (LTFM A4 → A3B). VirtualPilot A/B (`VP_LEGACY_REVENTRY=1`): 10 findings fixed, 3 new, all explained. **It and the named holding-point pin (`ApplyHoldingPointPin`) each REPLACE the route object, so every pilot pick — `ApplyUserHoldShorts`, `ApplyUserRunwayHoldShorts`, `ApplyUserTaxiwayHoldShorts` — runs AFTER both in `LoadRoute`**, never before: a pick made on the earlier object was silently gone from the adopted route while the `TaxiRouteRunwayEvent` it recorded still reached the summary, so the pilot was told about a hold that was not there (main already ran the pin first; the port had inverted it, and the re-route is new). → CLAUDE.md

## ENT-5

- When an airport paints NO holding-point names, the full-length call-out falls back to the NAVDATA ENTRANCE (`TaxiGraph.FindFullLengthEntrance` → "Full length entry via K16.") — the named taxiway meeting the runway NEAREST THE LINEUP POINT, never nearest the threshold (OMDB 30R is N9 at +81 m, not N10 at −28 m). The window is ASYMMETRIC by design — 150 m ahead (every metre past the lineup point is runway the pilot loses; also wider than `GetRunwayIntersections`' 50 m margin, which is tuned for the different job of keeping a full-length entrance OUT of the intersection list — OMDB 12R's K5 is 72 m past its start row) against 400 m behind (costs nothing, and is where starter extensions live — iniBuilds EGLL 09L's AB13 is ~345 m back; measured, widening 150→400 names 172 otherwise-silent runway ends over 1,952 airports). Nothing inside the window is SILENCE — never the nearest named taxiway at any distance: OMDB 30L's next named entrance is M18, 695 m down a 3,606 m runway, and calling that "full length" describes an intersection departure as a full-length one. Only the NAMED-taxiway index is scanned, because the runway's own pavement is itself a chain of UNNAMED navdata nodes (there is no `type='R'`), so an all-nodes scan returns the centreline instead of the entrances onto it. Painted names still win wherever OSM has them — this fires only when `namedPoints` is empty (OMDB: 235 holding_position nodes, all typed, not one with a `ref`). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ENT-6

- "(backtrack required)" is part of the runway combo ITEM LABEL (`TaxiGraph.RunwayNeedsBacktrack` + `BacktrackRequiredSuffix`) so the pilot hears it while arrowing — never a separate announcement on selection (the screen-reader rule). The suffixed label keys every `_destination*Map`; parse/speak consumers go through `StripBacktrackSuffix`; the SI import survives via `CleanRunway`'s regex extraction. Predicate changes must be re-measured (TRUE: LICR/LGSK/EGPB/EGNM; FALSE: every EGKK/EGLL/KJFK/LEBL direction) — a TRUE label must always mean the ticked backtrack works. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ENT-7

- **A start row on a STARTER EXTENSION is not pulled back onto the pavement** (`TaxiGraph.StarterExtensionReachesRow`, gating main's `PullOutboardStartRowOntoPavement` in `Build`): when taxi pavement runs out along the runway's own axis (inside half-width + 5 m, each segment within 20° of the axis, parking lead-ins excluded) to within 60 m of an outboard row, that row is a real departure point (EGKK 26L, 406 m behind its runway_end). Pulled back, EGKK's lineups left the pavement and its holds named no runway (VirtualPilot: 16 hold findings, 9 lineups off pavement, 6 wrong lineup places, 3 taxis off pavement fixed, 0 new). A taxiway CROSSING the extended centreline (LIMC 17L's AB, the case main's pull exists for) is never axis-aligned and never counts. → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)
