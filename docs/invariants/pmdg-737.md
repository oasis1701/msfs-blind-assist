## P737-1

- Two CDUs (no observer), no FPA mode, annunciator names differ from the 777 (LVL_CHG/HDG_SEL/VOR_LOC), DU selectors reverse sequence for the F/O, and fire handles need an active fire to test — see docs/pmdg-737.md for the full gotcha list. → [pmdg-737.md](../pmdg-737.md)

## P737-2

- The PMDG 737 CDU form sends every CDU key (letters, LSKs, function keys, CLR/DEL/EXEC) as a `TransmitClientEvent` carrying `MOUSE_FLAG_LEFTSINGLE` (0x20000000), a self-contained press-and-release click, and NEVER as the CDA `{eventId, 1}` write the 777 form uses for most keys. The NG3 FMC ignores the CDA write for CDU keypad events: the click sound plays but nothing registers (the same momentary-button behavior proven for the MCP buttons). A copy of the 777 form's CDA path, or a cleanup that "harmonizes" the two forms, brings back keys that do nothing. The 777's own FMCCOMM and HOLD keys use the same transmit path for a different reason ([P777-6]). A single `LEFTSINGLE` is a complete click; no separate `LEFTRELEASE` is needed. Live-verified against the NG3 (CLR and letter entry) with `tools/CDUTest`. Evidence: `PMDG737CDUForm.SendCDUKey` (`Forms/PMDG737/PMDG737CDUForm.cs`), which calls `SendEventViaTransmitWithTarget`, and "CDU keys must use TransmitClientEvent, not the CDA write" in `docs/pmdg-737.md`. Added 2026-10-09.

## P737-3

- `PMDG737Definition.HandleUIVariableSet` falls back to a click-walk for any 3+ position selector with no special handling: one `TransmitClientEvent` per detent, LEFTSINGLE to step up and RIGHTSINGLE to step down. That direction is right for only some NG3 knobs. On 2026-10-18 a live sweep with `tools/PMDGDispatchTester` (its `watch` command reads the struct field back) sent one LEFTSINGLE and one RIGHTSINGLE to every selector on that path. On 14 of them LEFTSINGLE stepped DOWN: the MCP bank limit, EFIS mode and range on both sides, both wipers, the position lights, pressurization mode, the outflow valve, N1 set, speed reference and the DC and AC meters. A walk on those drives every pick toward position 0 and pins it there; the pilot report was "the bank limit is stuck at 10°", and the sweep left the wipers running. Each of the 14 now dispatches by absolute target (`TransmitClientEvent` with the position as the parameter), which was read back across non-adjacent positions in one send. The position-light event counts from the other end: parameter 2 lands STEADY (struct 0), 1 OFF, 0 STROBE & STEADY, hence `_reversedAbsolutePositionSet` (`AbsoluteSelectorParameter` takes the top position from the selector's own detent count). The outflow valve switch, spring-loaded in the real cockpit, latches when set from outside (an absolute send and a CDA write of OPEN or CLOSE both held 3+ s until Neutral was sent), so the pilot picks Neutral to stop the valve. Knobs confirmed to step the right way and left on the walk: dome, fasten belts, window heat test, packs, isolation valve, landing light retract, the four VOR/ADF pointers, disengage test, lights test, fuel flow. No smoking passed that sweep too, but one click each way cannot show a missing detent: the NG3 models it with two positions, OFF and AUTO, not the header's three. Swept 2026-10-20, either click toggles OFF and AUTO, and ON is refused by the CDA write, the absolute transmit and the cockpit's own `K:ROTOR_BRAKE` click, wheel and drag codes, so a pick of ON walked one click from AUTO and landed OFF. It is now a two-position control on the CDA write (`Pmdg737NoSmokingSwitchTests`). When a sweep leaves a selector on the walk, also confirm its top position is reachable. The temperature source selector moved for neither click and is unresolved. The same bug was fixed earlier for the overheat-detect, cargo-detect, DU and NAV/DISPLAY source selectors, the engine start and ignition selectors, and (by a closed-loop walk) the transponder mode. Evidence: `_absolutePositionSelectorSet` and its comment in `PMDG737Definition.cs`. Added 2026-10-18; the No Smoking measurement and the top-position check added 2026-10-20.
