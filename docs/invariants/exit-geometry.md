# Landing exits: side, bank and turn-point geometry — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/exit-geometry.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads `TaxiGraph.cs`. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md).
These rules came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; a trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's. They sit on top of main's own designs (`ExitBranch`/`RefineExitByBranch`, `RolloutExitGate`), which win wherever the two overlap. Their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and of the sweeps they name only `tools/VirtualPilot` and `tools/ExitConsistencySweep` are in the repository (`LandingExitSideSweep` and `AmdbExitSweep` are not), so re-measure with VirtualPilot before touching one. Cross-references such as "the bullet below" point at that branch's CLAUDE.md list: search `docs/invariants/` for the rule's key name to find it.

## EXG-1

- **A crossing exit's NAME and `ExitBearingTrue` must sit on the bank its own `ApronNodeId` is on** (`TaxiGraph.ReconcileExitSideWithHandoff`). Where a turnoff CROSSES the runway, one centreline node carries a named edge on each bank under DIFFERENT names (HESH 04L: F/J, E/K, G/H), both fold to the same off-axis angle, and the "most off-axis edge" tie-break picks between them on a few hundredths of a degree — independently of the corridor walk that sets `ApronNodeId`. Live, that announced "taxiway J" at 132.6° (right bank) while routing to node 232 on F, 91 m LEFT; `ExitBearingTrue` drives `ResolveExitTurnDirection`, the post-turn-now tone, `TryEarlyExitHandoff`'s pan floor and `alignedWithExitPH`, so a wrong bank is a confident instruction to turn the wrong way across an active runway. The reconcile needs ALL of its gates — junction ON the pavement, a named off-axis edge on BOTH banks, apron node clear of the pavement, and the bank taken from the apron node's own lateral OFFSET, not the bearing to it (a curve's endpoint bearing is not its side). Ungated on those it moved 2,008 exits at 1,176 airports and re-typed 755 by "fixing" ordinary curved single-sided exits; gated, it moves 883 at 507 airports, every one of which already had its bearing on the opposite bank from its own `ApronNodeId`, and none of which changes `ApronNodeId`. Re-measure with `tools/LandingExitSideSweep` before touching a gate. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-2

- **An exit's corridor walk must not come back on ANOTHER exit's pavement** (`TaxiGraph.RewalkCorridorOnExitBank`). `ExitPathLeavesCorridor` is seeded from EVERY named edge at the junction, so where two taxiways leave the runway at one point it returns whichever branch clears in fewer hops — a coin flip that decides `ApronNodeId`, i.e. the route. YPPH 21 node 849 carries C9 at 180.0° (13.9° LEFT of a 193.9° runway) and A9 at 197.1° (3.2°, along the runway); the walk took A9, so **picking C9 gave you A9's apron node, A9's vacate destination, A9's bearing (225.4°, adopted by the shallow-angle override) and "Right" for a turnoff that goes left**. `ReconcileExitSideWithHandoff` cannot see it — A9's 3.2° stub is below `SIDE_MIN_LATERAL`, so the junction never reads as a crossing, and by then bearing and apron agree. ALL FOUR gates are load-bearing: gated on the bank disagreement alone it moved 6,115 exits and re-typed ~400 between End/Normal/High-speed (`ExitType` gates `TryEarlyExitHandoff`) — (1) the exit's own edge must name a bank, (2) the walked node must be clear of the pavement and on the other bank by its own lateral OFFSET not the bearing to it, (3) NOT at a genuine both-banks-named crossing, where the reconcile already owns the disagreement and settles it the OTHER way round (two rules pulling opposite ways on one population is worse than either), (4) the walk must have escaped down a DIFFERENT, NAMED taxiway (an unnamed first step is this exit's own connector). Applied at all four handoff-node sites — fixing one alone fixes nothing at a marker-rich airport (the KDTW lesson). Measured: 160 exits at 138 airports, no name/angle/type change anywhere, 0 removed, 1 added. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-3

- **Where the edge bearing cannot name a bank, the exit's OWN NAMED PAVEMENT does** (`TaxiGraph.RewalkOnOwnNamedPavement` + `FollowOwnNamedPavement`, the fallback for every exit `RewalkCorridorOnExitBank`'s four gates decline). EGLL 27L "S5W": the junction carries ONE edge, a 9.5 m stub 2.3° off a 269.7° runway, so gate (1) has no bank to compare; the all-branch walk goes 31 m down S5W's own link, cannot clear the ~40 m corridor in small hops, backtracks and takes neighbouring N5E's single 113 m leg to a node 109 m on the OTHER bank — so S5W (72 edges of pavement south, none north) got N5E's apron node, N5E's vacate destination and, via the shallow-angle override, N5E's 351.8° bearing: the south turnoff panned RIGHT across an active runway. Gate (4) cannot catch it either — it tests the walk's FIRST step, and here the first six steps are S5W's own pavement. Its gates: the walked node clear of the pavement AND carrying no edge of this exit's name (ending on our own taxiway is not ending on somebody else's), not at a both-banks-named crossing (the reconcile's territory), the own-name walk (named edges only, never unnamed connectors, 120-node cap) reaching clear pavement on exactly ONE bank, and that bank opposite the walked node's. It must clear the CORRIDOR (`lateralToleranceM`), not the pavement — at half-width it stopped on nodes barely off the edge and nearly abeam, and since the apron override recomputes the bearing as junction→apron that gave 70 Normal exits a tone target within 15° of runway heading (TONE_STRAIGHT 2075→2145) — and the node must be ≥ `EXIT_TURN_DIRECTION_MIN_DEG` (10°) off the axis or the correction is DECLINED, because below that the turn direction is not spoken and the tone falls back to runway heading (TONETGT_RWYHOLD 24→28). A correct bank is not worth a dead cue. Measured whole-DB: 351 records at 218 airports, only side/apron/bearing moved (no name, angle, type or distance anywhere), exits whose route ends on foreign pavement 591→269 at 309→168 airports, every `ExitConsistencySweep` count improved or held (SIDE_VS_APRON 2928→2902, WORD_VS_TONE 2708→2678, DEST_BEHIND 4577→4559, TONE_STRAIGHT 2075→2060, ON_PAVEMENT/RWYHOLD unchanged). Net +2 exits: one duplicate High-speed "D" MERGED at EGXT 07 once both halves agreed on a bank, three same-named High-speed siblings SURFACED (KDAB 07L/25R "A", KDLH 27 "A") that the name dedup had been collapsing only because the wrong bank made them look identical. Re-measure all three sweeps, diffed OCCURRENCE-KEYED, before touching any gate. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md) (The "DECLINED" sentence was later amended by EXG-6: with no own-pavement node 10° off the axis, the first clear one is returned.)

## EXG-4

- **`ExitSide` must never come from a bearing whose lateral component is NOISE** (`TaxiGraph.ResolveExitSide`). YPPH 21 "P" is a right-angle turnoff to the LEFT whose first modelled segment is a 9.8 m stub 3.2° RIGHT of the axis, and nothing fixed the side, so the list said "Normal, right, 88°" for an exit that goes left. Below `SIDE_MIN_LATERAL` the side comes from the apron node's own lateral OFFSET — the same authority the reconcile uses and the same node `ResolveExitTurnDirection` speaks from, so label and word agree — and with no usable node it is left EMPTY, never guessed. `SIDE_MIN_LATERAL` (0.20, ~11.5°) is ONE shared constant across the crossing test, the re-pick, the bank check and the side resolver. Re-measure BOTH sweeps (`tools/LandingExitSideSweep` + `tools/ExitConsistencySweep`), diffed OCCURRENCE-KEYED, before touching any of it: every consistency count must improve or hold (SIDE_VS_APRON 5759→2927, ON_PAVEMENT and DEST_OTHER_RWY unchanged). → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-5

- **A bearing ABOVE the noise floor can still be a STUB's** (`ResolveExitSide` + `OwnNamedPavementBank`): the floor judges direction, not length, and a 2-8 m junction segment's direction is node placement (KORL 25 A6: 2.1 m, 67° left, for a turnoff that leaves right). The label is overruled ONLY when the apron node is clear of the pavement on the other bank AND the pavement carrying the exit's own name reaches only that bank — both gates required; a same-name crossing reaches both banks and keeps the bearing side. `ExitBearingTrue` is NOT changed (it feeds `ExitType` and the rollout tests; `ResolveTurnToneTarget` already refuses a bearing whose side disagrees with the word). Measured 2026-09-23: only `ExitSide` moved (2,853 exits), SIDE_VS_APRON 2,902→387, every other consistency count unchanged; the X-Plane painted lines (`tools/AmdbExitSweep`) side with the apron 253/281 on this population. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-6

- **`RewalkOnOwnNamedPavement` stands aside at a both-banks crossing ONLY where the reconcile can act** (gate (c) also requires the exit's own bearing ≥ `SIDE_MIN_LATERAL`). Below the floor the reconcile declines too, and with both rules standing aside KDFW 36R F4 — a shallow right exit leaving from WM's crossing node — was routed onto WM to the left. Measured 2026-09-23: 3 exits, WORD_VS_TONE 2,678→2,675, nothing worse. When no own-pavement node reaches 10° off the axis, `FollowOwnNamedPavement` returns the FIRST CLEAR one rather than decline (KLAX 25L A7 routed onto H3 across the runway; KPHX 07L F9): the owner ruled that tones following the geometry outrank the TONETGT_RWYHOLD count (24→28 — exits that had been pulling toward the wrong bank). Re-run both sweeps and VirtualPilot before touching either rule. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-7

- **A taxiway drawn ALONG the runway centreline is not a turnoff where its name starts** (`TaxiGraph.OnAxisDepartureShiftM`, walk-only junctions, both build sites). A forward on-centreline run ≥ 20 m becomes `LandingExit.TurnPointOffsetFeet` — NORMAL exits only, read by the rollout's along-track gates and the post-handoff overshoot monitor — and never relocates the exit (relocating changed 2,661 runway directions / 461 re-types). A backward run ≥ 150 m drops the candidate as a backtrack phantom; 60 m hits 1,194 directions because crossing taxiways routinely jog 20-70 m along the centreline. LROP 08R live 2026-09-13: D's junction sat 45 m short of its curve (turn cue ~300 ft early, aircraft rolled past), and the miss retargeted to a centreline "D" whose only way off was 245 m back down the runway. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## EXG-8

- **A "High-speed" exit must agree with the route its handoff steers** (`GetLandingExits`' `CorroborateHighSpeed`, after main's `RefineForPlanner` at both build sites). Main's branch measurement reads only along the exit's own NAME, while `ApronNodeId` comes from the corridor walk, which can leave by another taxiway. Two steps: (1) when the junction→`ApronNodeId` chord turns more than `HIGH_SPEED_MAX_DEG` (50°) and the exit's own taxiway clears the runway as a rapid exit (name-filtered `ExitBranch.Analyze`, measured, turn ≤ 50°), the ROUTE moves onto it (`ApronNodeId` = that branch's corridor/clear node — KMIA 08R M7, whose junction M6 crosses at 90°); (2) when the shortest route junction→`ApronNodeId` then turns more than 50° (and at most 110°) off the landing heading on any leg of 5 m or more, the exit is demoted to Normal at that angle (KPIT 32 R1: 70° onto Q within 100 m; KDFW 17C "M6" via EL; LPFR 28 F) — the local build's corroboration rule, read along the route the handoff actually steers rather than the all-branch corridor walk, which wanders onto crossing taxiways and demoted KMIA M7. VirtualPilot vs the unchecked port: 37 off-pavement landings, 23 exit-route hairpins and 13 backwards handoff routes fixed. → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)
