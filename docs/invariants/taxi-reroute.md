## RTE-40

- **"Ask before switching to a new route when off route" (`UserSettings.TaxiGuidanceConfirmReroute`, default off) OFFERS a recalculated route, never adopts it unasked.** `TryRecalculateRoute` runs as usual through every refusal, the sanity gate (RTE-18) and the no-op guard (RTE-22); a route that would then be adopted is offered instead (`Navigation.RerouteOffer.Compose`, "Off route. New route available via …. Press left bracket, Y to accept."), placed so that nothing after it has run: no `AdoptRoute`, no hold passes, no latch resets, no tone re-slew, so the tone keeps steering to the route being flown. A Turn pick the aircraft has MISSED is said in the offer, before the key hint (`TurnPickMessages.Missed`): it is true whether or not the offer is taken, and the offer can name the very taxiways of the route being flown (TURN-3: a missed pick is never a no-op); "could not be kept" waits for "Route changed". Only the pilot's Continue (`ContinuePastHoldShort`, Taxiing, and not while a paired-crossing offer still STANDS: `RerouteOffer.KeyIsFree`, which frees the key as soon as that offer is taken, never only when its window ends) adopts, calling `TryRecalculateRoute(pilotAccepted: true)` from the LIVE position with the cooldown cleared, so the "Route changed" callout (RTE-8) names the route actually taken and its crossings; a press that adopts nothing and hears nothing gets `RerouteOffer.NothingToAccept`, collected like a frame's speech (STR-22). One offer per excursion (`_rerouteOffered`): a later recalculation that reaches the offer point while it stands ends there silently (its refusals and sanity-gate rejections still speak, as without the option); it is withdrawn, unspoken, once the aircraft is back on the route line (`RerouteOffer.BackOnRoute`: inside the route tolerance AND neither `farBehindStart` nor `goingBackward`), on leaving Taxiing (at a hold the key means "cross"), in `AdoptRoute` and in `StopGuidance`. Never withdraw on the tolerance alone, as the reachability latch beside it clears: `goingBackward` fires from 10 m behind the segment's start (`OFF_ROUTE_BEHIND_START_M`), inside the 25 m tolerance, so an offer raised by a slow wrong-way roll was withdrawn on the next frame, the key did nothing and the offer was spoken again after the cooldown. Every end goes through `EndRerouteOffer`, which also puts Repeat-last back to the instruction the offer replaced when it still holds the offer (`RerouteOffer.InstructionAfterOffer`): the key hint is never replayed for an offer that is gone. Why: LFPG 2026-11-05 (B, QB9, Q, E, TB2, G3 to B13), a slow hairpin left the aircraft outside the route line for the 3 s persistence window; the detector recalculated just as the aircraft finished the turn the tone asked for and the adopted route opened at 265° against a heading of 086°, while the pilot was following a VATSIM controller's clearance correctly. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
