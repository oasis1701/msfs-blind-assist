# Cessna 172 trim and throttle — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/c172-flight-controls.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [c172.md](../c172.md).

Measured on 2026-10-18 on MSFS 2024's stock "C172SP G1000 Passengers" (wheels), standing on the runway with the parking brake set and the engine idling, over SimConnect (the scratch harness running MSFSBA's own `SimConnectManager`) and a 20 ms Coherent recorder in the VLogic view; the trim, the throttle and the disconnect were put back as found (1.67 degrees nose up, idle, connected).

## C172F-1

- **One write sets the trim: `ELEVATOR_TRIM_SET`.** 0 gave 0 degrees, 8192 gave 11.0 nose up, -8192 (sent as its 32-bit two's complement, as the VS target is) 11.0 nose down, 16383 the 22.0 nose-up limit and -16383 stopped at the 19.0 nose-down limit (`ELEVATOR TRIM UP LIMIT` 22, `DOWN LIMIT` 19). So the scale is 22 degrees per 16383 both ways, and `ELEVATOR TRIM PCT` is a percentage of 22 both ways (-86.4 at the nose-down stop). `TrimSetValue` converts degrees, the Elevator Trim panel's typed field writes it, and the Nose Up and Nose Down buttons step 0.5 degree from the cached `MON_ElevatorTrim` through it.
- **The cockpit's two trim inputs do not set a position.** `HANDLING_ELEVATORTRIM_WHEEL` reads in percent over SimConnect (50.41 at 1.67 degrees; 0 degrees is 46.35, the Coherent `B:` read showing the same as a fraction, C172-9) and only FOLLOWS the trim: written 60 and 70, it left the trim where it was or nudged it a fraction of a degree. `HANDLING_ELEVATORTRIM_YOKE` is the yoke's electric trim switch, the one a pilot's hardware trim drives (Coherent `B:` 0 nose down, 0.5 at rest, 1 nose up, running the trim at 3.6 degrees a second while held); over SimConnect each write was one step of about 0.11 degree, and its direction did not follow the value written (100, 50 and 75 each stepped nose up; 0 and -1 nose down). The stock `ELEV_TRIM_UP` and `ELEV_TRIM_DN` step 0.029 degree each. None is a way to place the trim.
- **The take-off trim is 3.0 degrees nose up** (`TakeoffTrimDeg`). The POH's take-off trim is a mark on the wheel with no number. A flight restarted on the runway, the trim untouched, had `ELEVATOR TRIM POSITION` at exactly 3.0 and the flaps at 10 degrees (2026-10-18), and the flight model's `ELEVATOR TRIM NEUTRAL` reads the same 3. The Takeoff Trim button sets it, and the trim's row adds "takeoff position" while it sits there. → [c172.md](../c172.md)

## C172F-2

- **The trim disconnect is a direct set and blocks every trim.** `HANDLING_TRIM_DISCONNECT` set to 1 made `ELEVATOR TRIM DISABLED` 1, and a second 1 left it at 1, so it is a switch, not a toggle; 0 made it 0. Its own input-event read stayed 0 throughout, over SimConnect and Coherent alike, so it is read from `ELEVATOR TRIM DISABLED` (the panel's Trim Disconnect, Connected or Disconnected, announced as every switch is, C172-20). While it was 1, `ELEVATOR_TRIM_SET` 4000, two `ELEV_TRIM_UP` and a yoke-switch write all left the trim where it was; at 0 again each worked. On the real aeroplane the manual wheel still turns with the electric trim disconnected; in the sim nothing does, a pilot's hardware trim included.
- **So every trim write asks it first** (`HandleTrimSet`, from the cache, the switch being Continuous and announced) and says "The trim is disconnected." rather than sending a write that will do nothing. Whether the disconnect also drops the autopilot was not measured (it was off). → [c172.md](../c172.md)

## C172F-3

- **The throttle lever's input event has two scales.** `ENGINE_THROTTLE_1` written 20 put the lever at 100 percent and the engine at 2,157 rpm for a second (the parking brake held); written 0.1 it gave 10.0 percent. It takes a 0-1 FRACTION, yet SimConnect reads it back in PERCENT (9.998 after the 0.1). The cabin levers are percent both ways (C172-9), so neither scale can be assumed for an input event.
- **The throttle combo writes `THROTTLE1_SET`**, 0-16383, as the mixture combo writes `MIXTURE1_SET`: 1638 gave exactly 10.0 percent, and the cockpit lever followed (`ENGINE_THROTTLE_1` read 9.998). The combo (`C172_THROTTLE`, Idle, 1 to 99 percent, Full) is silent on a pick, its pick being its confirmation (CORE-7), and a hardware throttle moves it again on its next movement. → [c172.md](../c172.md)
