# COWS DA40-XLS engine rules — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/da40-xls-engine.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [da40.md](../da40.md).
The text is verbatim from the CLAUDE.md invariants PR #242 added; where two bullets told one rule they sit together under one ID. A trailing "→ doc" pointer is the original's.

## DA40X-1

- **The DA40-XLS has its OWN engine instrumentation now, all of it measured on the live XLS** — six panels (Magnetos, Power and Levers, Priming, Fuel System, Engine Start, Mixture and Propeller) built one at a time against the aircraft, with every unit and encoding recorded in `docs/da40-xls-variables.md`. The rules that came out of it: `STARTER_SWITCH` 0 OFF/1 R/2 L/3 BOTH and 4 is momentary and does NOT crank - `STARTER_SPAD:1` does; `THROTTLE_LEVER` is a read-only mirror, drive `K:THROTTLE1_SET` and measure, never compute (36 % gave 2212 rpm, 38 % gave 2190 on a different engine); `ENG_FUEL_PRESS` is bar and the G1000 draws it x 14.5; `FUEL_SELECTOR` 0 LEFT/1 RIGHT/2 OFF; `INPUT_START` = 1 (percent) runs COWS's own eleven-step auto-start and the K-event route is unreliable; the red box, fouling and cylinder damage are per-cylinder INDEXED variables (the bare names are phantoms) and are recomputed from their inputs because `DAMAGE_REDBOX_ITS:n` holds its last value when the box closes; with the master OFF the fuel logic does not tick at all. ⚠️ With the Engine Damage option off none of the damage accumulators run, and detonation is commented out of this build's model entirely (Logic 1857-1897) - never add a detonation call-out. → [da40.md](../da40.md)

## DA40X-2

- **Cycling the propeller is a DA40-XLS run-up item and does not exist on the NG** — the XLS's Lycoming has a blue pitch lever and a governor to exercise (and cold oil to push out of the hub); the NG's Austro FADEC governs the propeller through the single power lever, and its run-up item in that place is the ECU test. → [da40.md](../da40.md)

## DA40X-3

- **⚠️ THE XLS's MIXTURE HAS TWO ENTIRELY DIFFERENT REGIMES AND THE PILOT CANNOT SEE WHICH ONE THEY ARE IN.** `L:AUTOMIXTURE` is DETECTED, never set: the model watches for the simulator's own mixture assistance (`(A:RECIP MIXTURE RATIO:1, ratio) 100 * 9 ==`, or the package's `AUTOMIXTURE_FORCE`) and ramps the variable 0 → 1 in FIFTHS, dropping it to 0 in one step (XLS Logic 1805ff) — so the intermediate values are the ramp and only the ends are states, and the call-out must be spoken on the settled EDGE, never from the number. With it set the mixture VALVE is bypassed (the lever picks an air/fuel TARGET instead), **every cylinder's charge is clamped at 2 g so the engine CANNOT be flooded** — the whole hot-start problem the Priming panel exists for cannot arise — and Set Best Mixture snaps to 72 % rather than walking to 12.5 to 1. Verified live both ways. It is READ-ONLY: the setting lives in the simulator's assistance options, which MSFSBA does not own. → [da40.md](../da40.md)

## DA40X-4

- **⚠️ THE XLS SPELLS BLOCK AND OIL DAMAGE WITHOUT AN INDEX, and inheriting the NG's spelling reads a phantom.** `DAMAGE_BLOCK`, `DAMAGE_OIL` and `HEALTH_OIL` are unindexed on the XLS; the NG's `DAMAGE_BLOCK:1` / `DAMAGE_OIL:1` / `HEALTH_OIL:1` do not exist on that airframe at all, and there is no `HEALTH_BLOCK`, `HEALTH_FUEL`, `DAMAGE_FUEL` or `DAMAGE_TURBO` either — so a row bound the NG way reads 0, which is indistinguishable from an undamaged engine. The XLS's own failure set is BIGGER than the NG's (per-cylinder, per-PLUG magneto, magneto GROUNDING, three levers that can each come adrift), which is why `GetPanelStructure` now drops only "FADEC and Sensors" for the XLS and keeps Engine Failures, Fuel Failures and Engine Damage — removing all four made every one of those unreachable. `DAMAGE_REDBOX_{FAC,ITS,LOP,ROP}:n` stay UNSHOWN: `ITS:n` holds its last value once the box closes (19.7 with the engine stopped), so MSFSBA recomputes the box from its inputs. → [da40-xls-variables.md](../da40-xls-variables.md)

## DA40X-5

- **⚠️ THE XLS's PERFORMANCE-VARIATION SET IS THE ONLY CHANNEL FOR AN AEROPLANE THAT WILL NOT START WITH EVERYTHING SET CORRECTLY, and it now has a panel.** Fuel pressure is a PRODUCT of `FUEL_SPREAD_PRESSURE` and the idle jet is multiplied by `SPREAD_INJ_TRIM`, so an all-zero spread is zero fuel — structurally, for ever, with no CAS message and no failure flag — while `SPREAD_SET`/`CYL_SPREAD_SET` both read 1, "already done". Simulation → Engine Variation carries the whole set read-only. The sweep that found it is FS Copilot's `COWS_DA40XLS.yaml` diffed against `GetVariables()` READ FROM THE BUILT ASSEMBLY, with every survivor re-checked against the installed package by a BINARY-inclusive scan (a var used only by a WASM gauge reads as absent from a text grep). ⚠️ A first pass of that scan reported `OC_TEMPERATURE`, `ENG_MAG_FOUL_PWR` and `ENG_FUEL_LINE_GRAM` as "written nowhere in any XML, so the WASM writes them" and that was WRONG — a grep that silently matched nothing; all three have Logic.xml writers, and `ENG_MAG_FOUL_PWR:nX` is `sqrt(DAMAGE_MAG_FOUL:nX / 100) × 0.8`, so its resting 0 is a CLEAN PLUG. Five listed names are deliberately NOT bound under the DISP_ rule (`CHT_C:n`, `CHT_PROBE:n`, `EGT_PROBE:n`, `EGT_DELTA:n`, `OT_PROBE` — each sits behind an indication MSFSBA already reads, and the XLS models `FAILURES_DISP_CHT`/`_EGT`), and sixteen more are absent from the package exactly as they are from the NG's. → [da40-xls-variables.md](../da40-xls-variables.md)

## DA40X-6

- **⚠️ THE XLS'S SIMULATED OIL-TEMPERATURE ARC IS 45 °F WIDER THAN THE REAL AFM'S, AND MSFSBA FOLLOWS THE GAUGE.** The genuine Diamond AFM ships in the package (`Documentation/XLS/60101e-r10-complete.pdf`, WITH a text layer, unlike the COWS POH) and its section 2.5 gives oil temperature green 149-230 °F, caution to 245, red above; the aircraft's own `panel/panel.xml` draws green 122-275, caution to 285, red above. Every other gauge agrees to within rounding. **The GAUGE wins**: a band annotation exists to give a blind pilot the glance a sighted pilot gets, and the glance is the arc the G1000 DRAWS - annotating the AFM's caution against a needle sitting in green would invent a warning nobody else sees. ⚠️ The one place the two are NOT reconciled the same way is oil PRESSURE, and deliberately: `panel.xml`'s red zone there begins at 90, overlapping its own green (55-95) and caution (95-97) zones - a COWS typo - so `DA40InstrumentBands` takes the AFM's 97. → [da40.md](../da40.md)

## DA40X-7

- **⚠️ THE DA40's INDICATION FAILURES ARE PER-AIRFRAME AND THE XLS CARRIED THE AUSTRO's SET.** Grepped from each model's own XML: the two share six (oil pressure, oil temperature, ammeter, voltmeter, both tank quantities), the NG adds four (fuel temperature per tank, gearbox, coolant) and the XLS adds ELEVEN that had no row anywhere - tachometer, manifold pressure, fuel flow, fuel pressure, and CHT and EGT PER CYLINDER. This is the sharpest case of the `DISP_` rule in the aeroplane: a failed indication ZEROES the variable MSFSBA reads while the engine runs on perfectly (verified live - `FAILURES_DISP_CHT:1 = 1` took `DISP_CHT:1` from 351 °F to 0 while cylinder 2 read 353), so with no row the pilot sees a cylinder at zero and has nothing to tell it from a real reading. ⚠️ The four it WAS carrying are the phantom trap: a Lycoming has no gearbox, no coolant and no fuel-temperature indication, and none of those variables is in its package, so the rows read "No failure" for ever about systems nothing watches. ⚠️ The READING side was already correct - `DA40_XLS_RPM`/`_MAP` bind `DISP_*` on purpose - which is exactly how the gap survived: the half that was thought about was right and the half nobody looked at was silent. → [da40.md](../da40.md)
