# Synaptic A220-300 panels — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/a220-panels.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [a220.md](../a220.md). The other A220 rules are in [a220.md](a220.md), [a220-fms.md](a220-fms.md) and [a220-systems.md](a220-systems.md).

## A220-40

- The speedbrake lever is moved the way its cockpit lever moves itself. `FCTL_SPD_BRK_LEVER` (Pedestal/FlightControls.xml, template `A220_Lever`) reads `(L:A22X Spoiler Lever)` as a 0-1 travel and writes `<travel> 0.5 - 32768 * (>K:SPOILERS_SET)`, stepping 0.1 per wheel click. The `A22X_SPOILER_LEVER` combo sends exactly that string (`A220Panels.SpeedbrakeCalc`, seq-prefixed, invariant fixed-point per [VAR-5]) and shows the read-back as the nearest detent (`A220Panels.SpeedbrakeDetentForTravel`). Its six positions are the cockpit's RET, quarter, half, three-quarter, FULL and MAX — the pilot guide describes friction detents at the quarter positions and a lift gate between FULL and MAX — and the ECL's sensed SPOILER_LEVER_0..5, so the ECL maps all six. The detent travels 0, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 and 1.0 were confirmed live on 2026-10-16 (Synaptic A220 v1.0.10) by a sweep of the travel in 0.1 steps against the aircraft's own sensed bits: each of the six lit its own bit (RET to MAX = SPOILER_LEVER_0..5), no odd tenth lit any, and the read-back `L:A22X Spoiler Lever` equalled the written travel to about 1.5e-5 (the stock `SPOILERS HANDLE POSITION` stayed 0 throughout). A finer sweep (0.025 steps, edges refined to 0.005 for RET, quarter, FULL and MAX) lit each bit within about ±0.04 of its detent and none between the bands: RET 0-0.035, quarter 0.16-0.24, half 0.375-0.425 and three-quarter 0.575-0.625 (edges not refined), FULL 0.76-0.84, MAX 0.965-1.0. SPOILER_LEVER_5 is MAX travel, never FULL, so the pack's forced-landing "SPOILER lever — FULL" item, which senses _5, completes only with the lever at MAX. Change the travels only from such a sweep. Never use the stock `SPOILERS_ON`/`SPOILERS_OFF` events (the old Retract/Full buttons, which bypassed the lever) or a 0-16383 `SPOILERS_SET` value: the lever's own write is centred on half travel. → [a220.md](../a220.md)

## A220-41

- An A220 control's "(guarded)" label is the cockpit's guard, read from the behaviour XML (`IS_GUARDED`, carried into the `guarded` column of `tools/a220-gen/reference/cockpit-controls.csv`), never a habit carried over from another Airbus. `A220PanelStructureTests.GuardLabels_FollowTheCockpitXml` checks it both ways for every definition whose L:var is a cockpit control. The labels it corrected on 2026-10-12: gravity transfer was marked guarded and is not; cabin power, evac, PFCC 1-3, both hydraulic SOVs, aural warning inhibit, TAWS gear/terrain/flap inhibit, gear aural cancel, alternate brake and alternate flap are guarded and were not marked. The fire bottles carry no guard in the XML. Regenerate the CSV with `tools/a220-gen/cockpit_inventory.js` after an aircraft update; the test follows it. → [a220.md](../a220.md)

## A220-42

- The cockpit's pushbutton template decides the write. `A220_ButtonPBA` without `IS_LATCHED` — probe heat (ground test), both engine fire pushes, EXT PWR, master caution — sets `SET_STATE_EXTERNAL` to `1 (>VAR)`: the cockpit writes 1 and nothing else. What happens next is the aircraft's, measured live. EXT PWR and probe heat (2026-10-16) are consumed and cleared: probe heat's var read 0 again 8 s and 10 s after the write, its lamp `L:A22X Probe Heat Lamp` lit with the status message ADS PROBE HEAT GND ON, a second write 2 minutes later did not turn the test off, and the lamp went out on its own within 20 minutes (exact length not measured); the ECL's PROBE_HEAT_ON bit never lit, and no item of the installed checklist pack senses it. The engine fire pushes are NOT cleared (2026-10-16, Synaptic A220 v1.0.10, left push): written 1 the var stayed 1, L_ENG_FIRE_PRESSED and the CAS messages L ENG FUEL SOV CLSD and HYD 1 SOV CLSD appeared and stayed, and a second write of 1 changed nothing — one-way in the sim; only a flight reload resets it (a write of 0 was not tested). MSFSBA models probe heat and both engine fire pushes as write-1 buttons (`LPress`, `IsMomentary` false). They used to be level combos ("Probe Heat: Auto/On", "Left Engine Fire Push: Normal/Pushed") whose 0 wrote nothing the aircraft reads and whose shown state never matched. EXT PWR and master caution are the same template but are never turned into `LPress`: each is written 1 through its own case in `HandleUIVariableSet`. EXT PWR stays an Off/On combo on the in-use state (`A22X External Power In Use`); its case writes the toggle (`A22X External Power Toggle`) 1 only when the cached state differs from the pick, and retries once if the press is lost. Master caution is a button (`A22X Master Caution Warning`) written 1, and the lamps say whether it took. `A220_ButtonMomentary` and `A220_ButtonMomentaryClicky` pushes (chrono, nav source, crosstune, FD, CVR, mech call, transmit select) and `IS_HELD` pushes (gear aural cancel) keep the 1-then-0 pulse (`LMomentary`, `IsMomentary` true) as two calc strings, and so does the standby baro STD push (the ISI knob's push, `L:A22X ISI Altimeter STD`, pulsed in its own case and then read back from `KOHLSMAN SETTING STD:3`, never "pressed", [A220-43]). Latched korrys (`IS_LATCHED`) toggle a level the WASM reads, so they are two-position combos written by the generic L:var path. `A220PanelStructureTests.Pushbuttons_WriteTheWayTheCockpitDoes` derives all three from the inventory's `kind` column. → [a220.md](../a220.md)

## A220-43

- The standby instrument (ISI) has no set event and no CommBus action. Its baro knob (`A220_KnobContinuous`) writes a per-click delta, `p0 (>L:A22X ISI Altimeter Set)` with p0 = ±1, which the WASM applies and clears; its push writes `1 (>L:A22X ISI Altimeter STD)`, which the WASM also clears. Both are read back from the stock altimeter 3, which the aircraft's systems keep for the ISI (the systems WASM references `KOHLSMAN SETTING STD:3`).
- Measured live on 2026-10-16 (Synaptic A220 v1.0.10, MSFS 2024): one click is the ISI's 0.01-inch step (about 0.34 hPa; +1 raises). `KOHLSMAN SETTING MB:3` follows every click within about 150 ms, on a 1/16-hPa grid, so successive clicks read 0.3125 or 0.375 hPa apart (1013.25 on STD, then 1013.5625, 1013.875, 1014.25). `KOHLSMAN SETTING STD:3` reads 1 after an STD press (MB:3 then 1013.25) and 0 after the next knob click, which steps on from 1013.25; a second STD press leaves it 1. STD is a one-shot set to 29.92 inches, not a toggle, so a walk started on STD simply leaves it with its first click. No click was lost at a 600 ms pace (the walk's own 60 ms pace is left to the in-sim test plan), but the first clicks right after the ISI is powered are: two clicks within about 10 s of power-up did nothing, and later ones all landed.
- The ISI's own `A22X.ISI Data` store, which the displays agent exposes as `isi()`, is never the read-back. Its `qnh_hpa` is a whole number (the ISI prints it with no decimals), so it cannot see one click two times in three: the old walk read "moved 0" and said the knob was not responding while it worked. It has no STD field either: on STD it reads 1013, as a dialled 1013 does. The walk asks it only whether the ISI is powered (`powered:false` refuses with "Standby altimeter not powered."); a missing store or display link never blocks the walk.
- So the "Standby Baro" entry (940-1090 hPa or 27.00-32.00 inches) is a walk under the FCP walks' rules ([A220-7], [A220-9], [A220-10]): one ±1 write per calc string, paced so two never land in one WASM tick, a delivered read of MB:3 after each burst, the step and the direction measured on the first click of this walk, `round(|delta|/step)` clicks after that. A sign change after ONE click is a straddle and ends on the nearer of that click's two values; after a burst of several clicks it is an overshoot, and the walk aims back (`A220Panels.IsiWalkDecision`): a step measured on one click is up to 8 % short or 11 % long on the 1/16-hPa grid, and on a model that fits every measured click (`A220PanelsTests`) a walk from STD to 1030 that stopped at the first sign change would end on 1031.5. With no read-back, or a knob that does not move (as in the first seconds after power-up), the walk refuses aloud and never clicks blind.
- The STD push stops a walk in flight, is pulsed, and is read back once about 800 ms later (`A220Panels.IsiStdReadBackText`): "Standby altimeter STD" when STD:3 is set, the setting from MB:3 in hectopascals when it is not (the press did not land), and "Standby altimeter reading not available" when no read came back — never "pressed", never a guessed state (CORE-7). → [a220.md](../a220.md)

## A220-44

- The audio control panel's only functional receive controls are the three VHF volume knobs (`A220_ACP_Knob` → `A220_KnobPotentiometer`, `Pedestal/ACP.xml`, `Lib/Knob.xml`). The rotation writes `(O:SwitchState) 1 28 / * (>L:A22X ACP VHFn Volume)`: 29 detents, the level 0 to 1 in 1/28 steps, and the knob follows a direct write of the L:var. The push (`BTN_TOGGLE True`) runs `(L:A22X ACP VHFn Rx) ! (>L:A22X ACP VHFn Rx)`, a 0/1 toggle drawn as the knob pulled out. The aircraft's `systems.wasm` reads all three names and composes `COMn_RECEIVE_SELECT` and `COMn_VOLUME_SET`, so they drive the stock COM receivers (string scan 2026-10-15). Measured live on 2026-10-16 (Synaptic A220 v1.0.10, VHF 2): Rx 1 = receiving (`COM RECEIVE:2` read 1 with the L:var at 1 and 0 with it at 0, 2 s and 5 s after each write); the volume written as `step 28 /` drives `COM VOLUME:2` (14/28 gave 50 %, 28/28 gave 100 %) and the L:var holds the written level (the WASM never rewrites it). `L:A22X ACP VHF1 Volume` reads 100 in a fresh cold-and-dark flight, off the knob's 0-1 scale, with `COM VOLUME:1` at 100 % once powered: the aircraft's start value, which the volume combo shows as 100%, not an error. The knob's own position cannot be read: it lives in the component-local `O:SwitchState`, which no L: or A: var exposes.
- MSFSBA writes the TARGET receiver state, 0 or 1, never the toggle: a toggle would undo a pick the aircraft had already taken (main's PMDG 737 VHF receivers, #307, send the target state for the same reason). It writes the volume as `step 28 /` (`A220Panels.AcpVolumeCalc`), so the sim computes the exact level and the knob lands on its detent; never the combo's step index, which the generic L:var path would write (14 into a 0-1 L:var), and never a four-decimal level.
- Each write is read back once, after the pilot has stopped on a value (700 ms with no newer write to that control): silent when it landed (the screen reader read the pick, CORE-7), "did not change — still …" / "reads …%" when the aircraft kept another value, "not confirmed" when nothing came back. A write the pilot has arrowed past is never read back, so the 29-position volume combo neither reports a false failure nor snaps back mid-arrow.
- HF 1-2, SAT, CAB, INT, PA, SPKR, NAV and DME have no variable on this aircraft (their cockpit nodes only animate), so they are not offered. → [a220.md](../a220.md)
