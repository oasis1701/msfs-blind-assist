# Fenix display channel rules: each display's reader — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/fenix-channel-displays.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads the code of a display's window, keys or announcer, on top of [fenix-channel.md](fenix-channel.md). Everything here was measured on Fenix build 3.0.0.7613 unless it says otherwise. Background, with each display's rows and measurements: [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md), "Fenix display channel".

## FNX-27

- `FenixChannelPfdRows` has a flag row only for a flag whose drawing a capture in the sim showed. Measured and drawn on 2026-11-03 (A321 IAE): 46.1.1 ATT, 46.1.2 HDG, 46.2.1 SPD, 46.2.2 ALT, 46.8 V/S; 122.3 Metric (exactly while "-40 M" is drawn; `Efis.Meters` 1.5 is never set); 205 `dhMessage` (amber "DH" from a radio altitude of 200, never at BARO); 122.6 and 122.7 follow STD. 122.8 `AltAmber` (the altitude readout amber, 12:49:07.4 to 12:49:59.3, and alone from a BARO minimum down, 17:33:04.1 at 700 ft) is the altitude row's ", amber" (the owner's word, 2026-11-05). 206 `dhMessageFlashing` is measured but has no words, so no row.
- Left out as unmeasured: never seen set: 93, 94, 96, 115, 122.4, 122.5 and 110.10. Seen set with no capture of what they draw: 114.1, 118.1 and 118.2, 120.1, 120.2, 120.4 to 120.6, 120.8 and 120.9, 122.1 and 122.2 (times in [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md), "Flags left out as unmeasured").
- Why. The real A320's flags are a poor guide to what the Fenix draws, and a row naming the wrong flag tells a blind pilot an instrument is lost, or fine, on no evidence. The same rule held the box and flash letters unspoken ([FNX-24]). A capture of the PFD is the evidence; a future flag joins with its capture cited here. That a capture exists cannot be pinned by a test. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-28

- Field 155 is a fixed64, absent until a minimum is set. It read 700.0 with BARO 700; with RADIO 200 it read -701.76 at FL150 and -124.87 at a radio altitude of 200 and a baro altitude of 518, moving with the aircraft, and it is absent with none set (2026-11-03). So it is not the setting, though one reading looks like it. The Fenix's own slot 13 of the FMA reads `BAROdc⠀  700` or `RADIOdc⠀⠀ 200`, drawn "RADIO 200" in white and cyan, and the window speaks that through `FenixChannelSpeech`. The plan once expected a minimums row from 155; it was dropped, and the field number is not in `FenixChannelFields`. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-29

- protobuf leaves out a member at its default. The Fenix's valve enum has Open as 0, so a cruise push carried the closed valves and none of the open ones (2026-11-03), and every false or 0 elsewhere is left out too. A composer that read a missing nested message through its defaults would therefore say every valve open and every bus not powered, with no sign anything is wrong. So `FenixChannelSdReader.Message` makes the page not available when a message the rows read is absent; a member left out of a present message is its default, as measured. Pinned per page by `A_missing_valve_message_is_not_available_never_open`, `A_missing_part_is_not_available_with_its_path` and the like. A new page's composer takes the same reader. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-31

- `FenixA320Definition.Variant.Engine`, read from the Fenix's own config L:vars, decides the block: IAE V2500 reads EPR, FF, EGT, N2, N1 (the order on the screen, confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-03); CFM56, LEAP and PW GTF read N1 first. Channel field 6.5 looks like the same fact and is not usable: its CFM56 value is 0, which protobuf-net leaves out, so a CFM56 cannot be told from a missing field. An unread engine type shows no block rather than a guessed order. Measured on both families; pinned by `Each_family_has_its_block` and `An_unread_engine_type_shows_no_block`. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-33

- `PressDcdu` calls the definition's own `HandleUIVariableSet` branches for `S_DCDU1_*`, a full press and release ([FNX-6], [A320-3]), and the window presses once per key press: with no guard a held key auto-repeats, and an answer is two separate presses on one slot, WILCO then SEND. `FenixChannelDcduKeys.Decide` judges a soft key on a snapshot taken at the press: a blank label sends nothing and says "No action on that key.", and an untrusted DCDU sends nothing and says so. So a press is never reported for a key the screen does not offer. Tested on real PDCs under SayIntentions on 2026-11-03: a PDC comes back as two messages, a status and the clearance, and CLOSE clears it. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-34

- On the DCDU the message counter rises with `S_DCDU1_MSGDWN` although that part is named Minus in the cockpit (measured 2026-11-03; the first build had them swapped to match the names, and Ctrl+PageDown went backwards). [A320-17] needs DOWN to go forward everywhere: answer keys stay inactive until the pilot reaches the end. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-35

- A value's presence says nothing about whether the airframe has the part: an A321 cruise push reported every slide armed, the overwing ones the A321 lacks included (2026-11-03). The same holds for the cabin's data ([FNX-15]). So a page's parts come from `FenixChannelSdPageContext.Variant` and the page's own layout flags, among them 179.26 (`HasDoubleOverwingExit`), 179.27 (`HasBulkCargoDoor`) and 137.19 (`TpisFitted`). Pinned by `Only_the_airframes_own_doors_are_listed`, `Bulk_shows_only_on_an_airframe_with_one` and `The_a321_fuel_page_has_no_outer_tanks_or_center_pumps`; an unknown airframe is not available (`An_unknown_airframe_is_not_available`). → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-36

- An enum on these pages has only the values someone saw on the Fenix's own SD: `GearUnit`, `GearDoorPosition`, `HotAirStates`, the surfaces' signs, the PTU's states, the valve words. Any other value reads "not available" and goes to the log once ([FNX-22]'s `ReportFeatureProblem`), because a word guessed from its neighbor would tell the pilot a state nobody has seen. Pinned by `An_unknown_valve_value_reads_not_available_and_is_reported`, `An_unmeasured_ptu_state_reads_not_available_and_is_reported`, `Each_measured_hot_air_value_reads_its_word` and `Surface_directions_follow_the_measured_signs`. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-37

- The owner's ruling of 2026-11-03: every SD page says the colors and pulses the Fenix sends ("NORM BRK, amber", "ΔP: 8.2 PSI, pulsing"). So a row speaks a color only from a field named for it, and a `...Pulse` member as "pulsing". A replay of the flight to CYYZ found the case the second half of the line guards: the A321's flow amber is set all flight with no flow drawn, so the flag alone would say amber about something the SD does not draw. Pinned by the pages' color tests (`Bleed_values_speak_the_side_drawn_amber`, `Cabin_values_speak_their_colors_and_pulses`). → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-38

- SimConnect can no longer stand in for a check ([FNX-22]), so what makes the ISIS trustworthy is the build, the live flag (182's `Powered`, since the ISIS is on no display unit), the shapes and the content condition here: 182 must carry a QNH or a phase length. A round-gauge Fenix (`FenixVariant.HasIsis` false) has no ISIS to read, so Alt+I keeps the AI read and the list entry says "This Fenix has no ISIS."; that check stays ahead of `OpenChannelWindow`, or a round-gauge Fenix would get "display off".
- 182.16 and 182.17 hold "INIT 90s" and "ATT 10s": measured constant on the wire through a whole alignment, so they are phase lengths, read as drawn, and never counted down. While either shows the ISIS draws no attitude. Rows matched the ISIS's captures on 2026-11-04: speed within 0.1 kt, Mach within 0.001, pitch and bank within 0.5 degrees, altitude within 14 ft level. The altitude can be stale for minutes after power-up (367.7 for 4.4 minutes) with no sign on the wire; it is read as sent. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-40

- A GetMapData reply is not part of the snapshot's one instant ([FNX-22]), so `Check` ties it to the snapshot it is listed with. In sitting 1 at KATL (2026-11-03) the reply carried the EFIS's mode (2, 3, 4) and option, its range was the ND's reach (the range in ARC, half of it in ROSE NAV and PLAN), and a smaller range did not renew it. In the flight that evening the reply renewed each time the aircraft had moved a tenth of its range (2.00 to 2.08 NM at 20, 16.01 to 16.14 NM at 160), its center 0.11 to 0.14 NM from the aircraft at a renewal. The poller confirms the version once a second, so 3 s (`MaxAgeMs`, chosen) is about three missed confirmations. Pinned by `A_disagreeing_or_stale_reply_is_not_used`, `The_check_agrees_within_its_bounds` and `The_center_may_trail_by_a_tenth_of_the_replys_range`. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-41

- The window is the glass in words: the owner's and Hadi's rulings of 2026-11-03 dropped the rows the PMDG 737's window has and its ND does not draw ("Captain ND: <mode>, range <n>", "Messages: none"), and put each marker where it lies along the route and say where the route line ends and starts again. Pinned by `No_row_of_the_apps_own` and `A_gap_in_the_line_reads_where_it_ends_and_starts_again`.
- The color rule is [FNX-24]'s ND exception. A symbol or fixed color that does not come from the channel is an entry of the look table citing the capture that showed it, and an item with no entry says no color. Pinned by `An_item_with_no_measured_look_speaks_no_color`, `Every_look_cites_its_evidence` (the names are the record's, see [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)) and `Only_the_looks_that_tell_items_apart_or_are_red_speak_their_color`. → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-42

- The FMA's slots were recorded through one flight on 2026-11-04 (A319 IAE, take-off to an ILS autoland at KDFW 18R): slots 0 to 14 at row x 5 + column, slot 15 never held text, LAND, FLARE and ROLL OUT in slot 1 alone with field 112 set, the minimum in slot 13, every armed mode active in one push, and a change box drawn 10 s after a column's last change. Comparing raw slots would speak the box and the flash letters as changes, so the announcer compares `FenixChannelFmaSpeech.Read`, the spoken form. The settle is the PMDG 737's: a change that reverts inside 300 ms says nothing. It is `FenixChannelSettle`'s, shared with the DCDU and the ND messages: a reading that moves on inside the settle starts it over, so only a reading that held the whole 300 ms is spoken (before the review of PR 343 it ran from the first changed sample). A reconnect is a trust gap (a new connection number) and a mute keeps tracking ([FNX-26]). Column 5 is the FCU lights' to say, so it never speaks. The Lateral column stays silent under LAND, FLARE and ROLL OUT (the owner's ruling, field 112). Pinned by `FenixChannelFmaSpeechTests` and `FenixChannelFmaAnnouncerTests` (`A_recorded_flight_is_announced_as_expected`, `A_change_box_or_flash_alone_says_nothing`, `A_change_is_spoken_once_it_has_held_300_ms`, `A_reading_that_passes_inside_the_settle_is_never_spoken`). → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)

## FNX-43

- The ECAM entries stream, measured on 2026-11-04 (A321 IAE, in cruise, descent, landing and at the gate): the list is sampled on a 3.84 s beat, and a heartbeat with no entries is not a list that emptied. An entry can reach the list before or after its line reaches the E/WD (`LineWaitMs`, 500 ms): a failure with no line by then is spoken with no color rather than held. During a power transfer ENG 1 FIRE dropped from one list with the fire still set and the E/WD trusted, which would have said "cleared" over a real fire: `LeaveHoldMs` (4.5 s, one more beat) is why an item counts as gone only after staying out (the owner's ruling, 2026-11-04).
- The owner's rulings of 2026-11-04: LAND ASAP, whose entry is named as a memo, is heard by its color under the warnings or cautions row, after the failure that caused it. A memo whose entry words differ from the E/WD's ("MEMO-FUEL < 3 T" draws "FOB BELOW 3 T") speaks the E/WD's words with its color, from a table of the measured pairs. After a blackout the list remembered when the E/WD is trusted stays the baseline, so the start-up failures on the first new list are spoken, as a sighted pilot sees them on the display: never wait for that list to take it silently. The displays only go dark in a serious power failure. The memos row starts on, like every row. A blackout does not reconnect the ECAM entries call; the stream breaking or the Fenix restarting does, and the list of the connection before is then dropped (`FenixChannelSnapshot.EcamConnection`) so the new connection's first list is a silent baseline: kept, a list from a Fenix that has since restarted would have said every failure of the earlier session "cleared" (review of PR 343, 2026-11-06).
- Pinned by `FenixChannelEwdAlertAnnouncerTests` (`A_new_warning_interrupts_with_its_color`, `Only_the_first_red_title_of_a_tick_interrupts`, `A_failure_gone_for_the_hold_is_cleared_once`, `A_color_change_is_never_spoken`, `A_memo_speaks_the_words_the_ewd_draws`, `A_new_ecam_connection_forgets_the_remembered_list`). → [a32nx.md](../a32nx.md)
