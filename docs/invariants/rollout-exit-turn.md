# Rollout exit turn, hand-off and missed-exit — rules in full

Each section is the complete text of one rule. Its one-line form, under the same ID, is in `.claude/rules/rollout-exit-turn.md`, which Claude Code loads when it reads matching code. Background: [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md), [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md).
These rules came with the taxi/landing port (`feat/taxi-landing-port`) and are verbatim from that branch's CLAUDE.md as of `4854ec3d`; ROL-38 to ROL-42 were the branch's ROL-30 to ROL-34, renumbered when it merged main, which had taken those numbers, and the group was split out of landing-rollout.md for the rule budget. They sit on top of main's own designs, which win wherever the two overlap; their figures were measured on the owner's local build, and of the sweeps they name only `tools/VirtualPilot` and `tools/ExitConsistencySweep` are in the repository, so re-measure with VirtualPilot ([virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)) before touching one. "The failure above" in ROL-38 is ROL-42's.

## ROL-35

- **Neither missed-exit detector may call a miss while the aircraft is TURNING ONTO the chosen exit's own path** (`LandingExitPathFollow.HoldsOffMiss`, fed by `GetRolloutExitPath` = graph route junction → `LandingExitDestination.Resolve`). ALL gates are load-bearing: within 10 m of the path, the path stretch there ≥ 5° off the runway, and the aircraft turned ≥ 5° to the SAME side and within 20° of that stretch; bounded to margin + 400 ft. Both detectors judge from the RUNWAY (along-past, heading < 15°, post-handoff lateral < 30 ft), which cannot tell "rolled straight past" from a shallow exit: YPPH 21 C9 leaves at 14° and is 20 ft off the centreline 100 ft past its junction, so the pilot flying it 12° into the turn with the tone centred was told "Missed C9. Retargeting C11" (live 2026-09-18). NEVER make it position-only: many sceneries (EGLL, LFPG) draw exits ALONG the centreline for 60-75 m, and a position-only hold-off (measured, 500 airports) delayed 6,350 straight-roll miss calls, 1,390 of them far enough that the retarget skipped the next exit. The heading gate keeps a straight roll (runway heading) exactly as before. → CLAUDE.md

## ROL-36

- **Both missed-exit detectors also wait out the exit's on-centreline STUB** (`LandingExitPathFollow.OnAxisRunMetres` on the `GetRolloutExitPath` path, less any `TurnPointOffsetFeet`; capped 200 m; 6 m lateral): many sceneries draw the exit taxiway down the runway centreline first (KORD 27L M 80 m, ZSPD 35R B7 99 m, EGLL 27L N7 40 m after a 3 m jog), so the route tone says "continue" while the junction + 100 ft miss check said "Missed". Measured with `tools/VirtualPilot` (100 busiest airports): 996 → 194 false misses. The price, accepted: a straight roll past a stub exit is called later (median 59 m). The two refusals are load-bearing — a path that turns BACK down the runway WHILE STILL WITHIN 30 m OF THE CENTRELINE (`OnAxisHairpinLateralMetres`; KPHL 35 E4, a 160° hairpin) or comes back onto the centreline after leaving it (KSDF 29 F) returns 0, or those landings lose the miss that currently rescues them. The hairpin refusal is scoped to near the runway: unscoped, any exit that loops back once out on the apron (KSTL 30L V, YBBN 01L T1, CYYC 29 C, 50-80 m out) lost its whole stub and heard "Missed" 31 m past the junction (44 false misses). Do NOT widen the 6 m band: 7.5 m and 9 m were measured and each fixes offset-stub misses (OOMS 26R A runs 7 m off the centreline) only by calling 11-24 straight rolls "missed" > 200 m late — the two positions are indistinguishable. Re-run VirtualPilot and `compare_codes.py` before touching any constant. Both detectors also hold a miss off while the aircraft is within 15 m of the exit's own path (`StillOnExitPath`, bounded to 800 ft past the margin) — an owner ruling (2026-09-23) that a false "Missed" to a tone-following pilot outranks a later call to one rolling straight past: false misses 68→22, STRAIGHT_MISS_LATE 14→101 (median 224 m). Do not remove it to speed up straight-roll calls. Both detectors take ONE margin, `MissMarginFeet()` = Max(main's `RolloutExitGate.OvershootMarginFor` read at `DivergenceAngleDegrees`, the same rule read at `ExitAngleDegrees` + `OnAxisMissExtensionFeet()`), and pass that same margin to `HoldsOffMiss`/`StillOnExitPath` — measured from the junction, `HoldsOffMiss`'s 400 ft window was used up by a long stub before the exit curved (KPHX 07L E12, 2026-09-23). Never ADD the stub to main's divergence margin: both describe the same stub, and summed a straight roll past LTFM 34L A7A was called 202 m past the junction (153 m divergence, 117 m stub). → [virtual-pilot.md](../virtual-pilot.md)

## ROL-37

- `exitedLaterally`'s heading branch (≥ 8°) is bounded to `ROLLOUT_NEAR_EXIT_FT` (500 ft) unless the aircraft is on mapped taxi pavement (`IsOnTaxiPavement` — a shallow earlier taxiway taken deliberately keeps the old handoff) (drifting onto the grass short of the exit is main's `OffPavementAlert` + DriftCorrection tone, not a handoff). Unbounded, 40 m onto the grass 2,367 ft short of C11 read as "took the exit". The rollout handoff re-route anchors its start on the exit's taxiway only within `ROLLOUT_STOPPED_HANDOFF_MAX_DIST_FT` (1000 ft) — far out the anchor started the route at a junction 700 m ahead. → CLAUDE.md

## ROL-38

- **The rollout "turn now" trigger (`ROLLOUT_TURN_NOW_FT`, 150 ft) is GEOMETRIC and must stay so** — it also swings the Normal-exit tone onto `ExitBearingTrue`, so speed-scaling it hard-pans the tone hundreds of feet early at 40 kt, which is the failure above reintroduced through a different door. The LEAD is genuinely speed-dependent (rapid-exit guidance arms at 300 ft against this 150; the undershoot scan already uses `ROLLOUT_UNDERSHOOT_LEAD_PER_KT_FT` = 11 ft/kt ≈ 6.5 s; 150 ft is 4.4 s at 20 kt and 2.2 s at 40 kt), so the two jobs are SPLIT: a separate speed-scaled **preparatory** call on the same 11 ft/kt law fires first (`_rolloutTurnPrepAnnounced`, Normal exits only, capped at `ROLLOUT_TURN_PREP_MAX_FT` 400 ft so it can never land on the 500 ft milestone, skipped entirely when it would fall within `ROLLOUT_TURN_PREP_MIN_GAP_FT` of the turn point). Arrive fast → warned earlier, never pulled earlier. If the turn point reads late, move `ROLLOUT_TURN_NOW_FT` — which moves the verbal and the tone together — never scale it with speed. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-39

- **The Normal-exit speed handoff must wait for the turn-now callout, never fire at `nearExit` (500 ft).** `speedNearExitHandoff` is gated on `_rolloutTurnNowAnnounced`, because everything the KDTW 22L fix built lives in `UpdateLandingRollout` and dies the moment the state leaves `LandingRollout`: the Normal-exit tone that holds RUNWAY HEADING until the turn point, the speed-scaled prep call, and the 150 ft "Turn left now" verbal. On a normal decelerating landing the aircraft is below 30 kt a long way out, so the old gate fired dead-centre on the runway (HESH 04L, live 2026-08-26: hdgDelta 0.4°, lateral 36 ft, 480 ft short) and handed the pilot to `UpdatePosition`'s `_rolloutHandoffActive` branch, which snaps the tone to the exit SEGMENT bearing — a full 90° hard pan 152 m before the junction, held 18 s with no verbal at all. Following it, the turn began 443 ft early and cut the corner, passing 59 m from the junction node; the 25 m arrival radius still reported a clean vacate. Keying on the LATCH rather than a 150 ft distance test is required: this block runs BEFORE the turn-now callout in the same method and returns, so a distance gate swallows the verbal one frame later. `turnBegun` (15°), `trulyStopped` (<3 kt) and the in-rollout overshoot detector still cover every other exit path. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-40

- **Both post-handoff tone sources carry a bounded cross-track pull** (`exitCrossTrackPull`: 1°/m, ±20°, alongside the current segment, segment within 30° of the runway, never into a sharp turn or on a turn-back exit) — without it an aircraft parallel to a shallow exit but 16 m wide read zero error (KPDX 28R A6, KATL 08L B11). VirtualPilot: 53 off-pavement + 11 false misses fixed, 0 new. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md) (Split 2026-10 from one bullet, words unchanged: its detour-retry sentence is now RTE-36; the VirtualPilot figure covered both.)

## ROL-41

- **Never derive the spoken exit turn direction from the bearing AIRCRAFT→junction** — use `ResolveExitTurnDirection` (junction→`ApronNodeId` bearing, then `ExitBearingTrue`, both vs runway heading, each needing `EXIT_TURN_DIRECTION_MIN_DEG` = 10° to be spoken; below that the direction word is DROPPED — "Turn now, taxiway X" — never guessed). Where a scenery models the junction ON the centreline the old rule's sign is decided by the aircraft's own tracking error, not by the exit: 5 of KDTW 22L's 13 junctions sit within a metre of the centreline (Y3 0.4 m, R 0.1 m, V 0.4 m, Z7 0.7 m, Z5 0.9 m), so a LEFT exit announces "Turn right now" for any aircraft tracking a metre left of centre. A confident wrong side is far worse for a blind pilot than no side. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)

## ROL-42

- **The rollout tone must never pull while the correct action is "go straight"** — "follow the tone" is this app's one contract, and a tone that has to be ignored until a verbal arrives destroys it. On a **Normal** (right-angle) exit the pre-turn desired heading is RUNWAY HEADING, not bearing-to-junction, switching to `ExitBearingTrue` only once the turn-now callout fires (so the tone then means "how much turn is LEFT" and decays to silence as the pilot aligns). Bearing-to-a-point amplifies ANY lateral offset — the aircraft's or the node's — without bound as it closes: measured on KDTW 22L against a 15° full-pan, a 10 m offset reads 6° at 300 ft but 47° at 30 ft, a 25 m offset 16° → 70°, so the tone saturates hard-over from parallax in the last seconds before an 81° turn the pilot has not started. This is the behaviour the block already CLAIMED ("≈ runway heading when the junction is on the centreline → tone stays silent"), made true when neither the aircraft nor the node is exactly on the centreline. **High-speed and End exits keep bearing-to-junction** — on a RET the drift onto the arc IS the manoeuvre, and `ExitBearingTrue` at that range is the EIDW S5 premature-hard-pan failure. → [taxi-guidance.md](../taxi-guidance.md)
