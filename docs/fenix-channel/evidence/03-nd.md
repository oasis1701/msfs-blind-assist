# Plan 03 evidence: the ND

## Sitting 1, Part A: the header, the EFIS and the route's structure

Measured on 2026-11-03 on Fenix build 3.0.0.7613 (the app's log: "build 3.0.0.7613: checked"), MSFS 2024 1.9.21.0, an A320
with CFM engines, at KATL: first on a runway (heading 095, engines running, no flight plan), then after a restart at a gate
(heading 278, engines off) with Hadi's route KATL 08R to KCVG 18C. Claude set the captain's EFIS through the sim's Coherent
debugger (knobs and selectors written directly, buttons as a full press and release), read and paged the captain's MCDU
through the Fenix data service (the app's own key writes), and made one temporary flight plan and erased it; Hadi pressed CLR.
Raw fields came from a local recorder of the display data stream; the ND from captures of the captain's ND popped out of the
cockpit (a 752 x 729 window). The verdicts came from `fenix_channel.log` of a running app (the shared branch's build): the ND
stayed trusted throughout.

### The view (Step 1)

The captain's ND popped out into its own window, captured with PrintWindow. It is the captain's: a change of the captain's
range changed it and left the first officer's range field alone. Every later crop uses this window.

## Sitting 1, Part B: how the ND draws the map

Same sitting, at the KATL gate with the route loaded (heading 278.12 magnetic, variation -5.60), from 11:09. The captain's EFIS
was set by Claude; each capture was checked against positions: the route's own (2.4), the reply of GetMapData (Part C) and,
for the first VOR checks, the app's navdata database. A local tool predicted each point's pixel from the model below and
measured the drawn symbol's centroid beside it.

### The projection (Steps 9 and 10)

- ROSE NAV and PLAN: the center of the screen is pixel (375.5, 357) of the 752 x 729 window; the compass ring's radius is
  241.5 px and stands for half the range at every range, so a nautical mile is 241.5 / (range / 2) px (48.3 at 10, 24.15
  at 20, 12.08 at 40, 6.04 at 80, 3.02 at 160).
- ARC: the aircraft symbol is at about (375.5, 597); the compass arc stands for the whole range at 473 px (2.956 px per NM
  at 160).
- ARC and ROSE NAV are heading-up on the TRUE heading (194.1 plus 194.3: 278.12 - 5.60 = 272.53), from the ND's own
  position (56). Every route waypoint drawn (INTCPT, RONII, HOYYT, GRABA, PADGT at 10 to 160) and every VOR checked (DOB,
  RMG, LGC, CSG, GAD in ARC 160) sat within 1 to 3 px of its predicted pixel; bearings within 1 degree except within a
  few pixels of the center.
- PLAN is MAGNETIC-north-up: a true-north model put every point about 5.6 degrees off; with north rotated by the variation
  (194.3) the VORs and waypoints sat within 1 to 3 px. Its center is 84, the second waypoint row of the captain's F-PLN
  page (the top row was PADGT and the center SMTTH; a discontinuity row is skipped).
- 84 carries more in PLAN: 84.3 (varint 1), 84.4 a course ('337') and 84.5 a distance ('7NM') of the center waypoint, not
  seen drawn.

### Declutter (Step 14)

At 160 and 320 the route elements with leg member 5 = 1 (RONII, HOYYT, GRABA, NAVPE, OPAVE, FIDEK, UBEDE, LEWIN, WUTVU) lose
their symbol, label and constraint, and the line stays; the elements without it (KATL08R, PADGT, SMTTH, ARTUR, JAKIE) keep
theirs, and JAKIE's constraint (its marker has no member 7) stays. At 80 and below everything is drawn. So leg member 5 and
marker member 7 both mean "removed when decluttered", and the ND declutters at 160 and above.

## Sitting 1, Part C: what GetMapData returns

From 11:24, with Hadi's go: the first calls of `GetMapDataVersion` and `GetMapData`, from a local read-only tool, each with a
MapDataRequest of 1 = true (the captain's).

- `GetMapDataVersion` answers `{1: <version>}` (2 bytes). It held still for 20 s at rest and went up by one at each mode
  change, option change and range increase.
- `GetMapData` answers 16 to 20.5 KB in 60 to 72 ms (once 590 ms, at ARC 320). No effect on the sim or on the app's
  channel was seen.
- The reply (MapData): 1 airports, 2 navaids, 3 fixes, 4 NDBs (each a repeated point), 5 and 6 the center (fixed64),
  7 the true track (fixed64; absent in PLAN), 8 the range (varint), 9 the mode (varint: 2 ROSE NAV, 3 ARC, 4 PLAN, sent
  explicitly), 10 the version, 11 partial (1 with WPT, which sent 101 fixes; absent otherwise), 12 the option (as the
  EFIS's 1.2: CSTR 0 to ARPT 4, absent with none).
- A point: 1 its kind (1 airport, 2, 3 or 4 a navaid, 5 a fix, 7 an NDB), 2 and 3 its position (fixed64), 4 = 3 on navaids
  and NDBs, 5 its ident.
- Every kind is sent whatever the option (about 250 airports, 190 navaids and 147 NDBs, out to about 330 NM); the ND draws
  the option's kind only, and none with CSTR or no option.
- The center is the aircraft's position in ARC and ROSE NAV, and 84 (the PLAN center) in PLAN.
- The range is the ND's reach: the range in ARC, half the range in ROSE NAV and PLAN. A range decrease in the same mode
  does not renew the reply (ARC 160 down to 10 kept range 160 and the version); in ROSE LS and ROSE VOR the last reply
  stays. So a reply agrees with the ND when its mode and option match and its range is at least the ND's reach.
