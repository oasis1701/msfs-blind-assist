# Plan 03 evidence: the ND

## Sitting 1, Part A: the header, the EFIS and the route's structure

Measured on 2026-11-03 on Fenix build 3.0.0.7613 (the app's log: "build 3.0.0.7613: checked"), MSFS 2024 1.9.21.0, an A320
with CFM engines, at KATL: first on a runway (heading 095, engines running, no flight plan), then after a restart at a gate
(heading 278, engines off) with Hadi's route KATL 08R to KCVG 18C. Claude set the captain's EFIS through the sim's Coherent
debugger (knobs and selectors written directly, buttons as a full press and release), read and paged the captain's MCDU
through the Fenix data service (the app's own key writes), and made one temporary flight plan and erased it; Hadi pressed CLR.
Raw fields came from a local recorder of the display data stream; the ND from captures of the captain's ND popped out of the
cockpit (a 752 x 729 window). The verdicts came from `fenix_channel.log` of a running app (the shared branch's build): the ND
stayed trusted throughout.

### The view (Step 1)

The captain's ND popped out into its own window, captured with PrintWindow. It is the captain's: a change of the captain's
range changed it and left the first officer's range field alone. Every later crop uses this window.

### The header as drawn (Step 1)

- Top left, line 1: "GS" white and small, the ground speed green and large, "TAS" white and small, the true airspeed green and
  large ("GS 0 TAS ---" at rest). Sent as `wsGSlgd  0wsdTASlgd---`: the ground speed is a three-character field,
  right-aligned. At each `d` the glass shows a blank gap about one character wide and draws nothing, so plan 02's ruling
  stands: `d` draws nothing, and the line has no color of its own to add.
- Top left, line 2: the wind, "---/---" at rest: the figures green, the slash white. Sent as `g---w⠀/⠀g---`; the
  spacer is U+2800, which `FenixChannelText` already reads as a space.
- Top right: the next-waypoint lines (below), or in ROSE LS and ROSE VOR the LS or VOR block in their place.
- Top center: the compass, the yellow lubber line, the green track diamond.
- Bottom right: "WXR OFF", white, in ARC and the ROSE modes; not in PLAN.
- Bottom center: the messages, each in a white box (below).

### No flight plan (Step 2)

- 68.1 is `PPOS     ` (five trailing spaces); 68.2 and 68.3 are absent. It is sent in every mode. The glass draws "PPOS" top
  right in ARC, ROSE NAV and PLAN; in ROSE LS the top right is empty (no ILS tuned, 72 holds only an empty 72.3), and in ROSE
  VOR it shows the VOR block instead (70.1 `wVOR1 w108.s00 ` drawn "VOR1 108.00", 70.2 `CRS c---°` drawn "CRS ---°").
- The first EFIS entry and 163 are present. 62 to 67 are absent.
- Field 2 is sent with no flight plan: four empty 2.13 entries, nothing else. So "no route data" does not happen at a powered
  aircraft with no flight plan: the route decodes with no legs.

  about the wire shape. CSTR is sent as 0; none is the field left out. The option buttons are exclusive: another press moves
  the light, a press on the lit one turns all off.
- The NAV selectors' numbers differ from the panel's L:vars (ADF 0, OFF 1, VOR 2): the channel sends OFF 0, VOR 1, ADF 2.
- 1.15 and 1.16 held 29.9139 and 1013.0 (QNH in inches and hectopascals, fixed32).

### The route's structure (Steps 5 and 6)

Hadi's route: KATL 08R, the PADGT2 SID (INTCPT, RONII, HOYYT 250 KT, GRABA, PADGT), a discontinuity, SMTTH, ARTUR, a
discontinuity, the JAKIE6 STAR (JAKIE +14000, NAVPE +10000, OPAVE +8000, FIDEK 210 KT +8000, UBEDE, LEWIN 210 KT 6000,
MANUAL on heading 006), a discontinuity, ZEVVE +4000, WUTVU, KCVG 18C (ILS18C), the missed approach (1500, ZIRKE +3000, a
right hold at ZIRKE, inbound 174°), and the alternate KCVG, a discontinuity, DECEE, VHP, a discontinuity, KIND. The legs
between SMTTH and ARTUR are a direct: the route has no airway. It has no overfly, and the SID does not start with an altitude
leg. Paged on the captain's MCDU beside the push.

- 2.1 (repeated, the departure and arrival runways): 1 latitude and 2 longitude (fixed64), 3 true course, 4 runway
  ("08R"), 5 length in feet, 6 airport ("KATL"), 7 a small number (53, 55: perhaps the threshold crossing height), 8 zero,
  9 magnetic course, 10 magnetic variation.
- 2.4 holds the whole route, the missed approach and the hold included: 57 elements, first the line in route order, then one
  symbol per waypoint. Each element: 1 latitude and 2 longitude (fixed64), 6 the ident, 3 its kind (varint):
  - absent (0): the line runs straight to this point;
  - 1: the line turns to this point, with 8 the side ("L" or "R"), 9 the radius (fixed32; 1.4715 at a turn, 0.0031 at a
    fly-by point), 10 and 11 the true courses in and out (fixed32);
  - 2: the line starts here: the departure runway first, then after each break;
  - 3: the line ends here, with 16 the reason: 1 at each of the five discontinuities (three in 2.4, two in 2.7); 2, with
    17 = 68, after WUTVU, where the F-PLN has no discontinuity and the line starts again at once at the same point as
    "KCVG18C" (the final approach as its own line; the crops of Part B show how it is drawn);
  - 4: a waypoint symbol, with 7 its symbol (5 for a waypoint, an airport as a waypoint included; 3 for VHP, a VOR).
- 4 = 1 marks the missed approach (1500, ZIRKE, the hold, and their symbols). The destination is the runway: "KCVG18C" with
  kind 2 at WUTVU's point, then a straight line to the threshold; the missed approach follows it.
- 5 = 1 is on the SID, STAR, approach and missed approach elements and absent on the others; what it changes on the glass is
  for Part B.
- The hold at ZIRKE is four elements (an arc, a straight, an arc, a straight) with 12 = 4 and 13 = 1 on the first (a right
  hold, "HOLD R") and 14 the inbound course: 168.1 (true) on the first, 174 (magnetic, the F-PLN's C174°) on the rest.
- The heading leg after LEWIN (LEW006, "MANUAL") is drawn as a line about 500 NM long along 006°, to 47.43 N.
- The constraints are not members of a leg: they come as 2.12 markers, at their waypoint, with the label as the ND prints it.
  Type 5 for an altitude (with any speed): "+14000" at JAKIE, "+10000", "-12000" over "+ 8000" (a window, at OPAVE),
  "+ 8000" over "210KT", " 6000" over "210KT", "+ 4000", "+ 3000" (ZIRKE, 6 = 1, the missed approach). Type 6 for a speed
  alone: "250KT" at HOYYT. All but JAKIE's have 7 = 1 (remove on declutter). None has a color member.
- Two other markers: type 8 at HOYYT, color 6 (magenta), no label; type 11 between SMTTH and ARTUR, color 2 (green), no
  label (the contract's order makes 11 the ETP; a top of climb is expected there: Part B and the flight decide). No type 0
  (top of descent) yet.
- 2.7 holds the alternate (8 elements, the same scheme): the glass draws its line cyan and dashed, VHP as a green VOR symbol
  and KIND as a green diamond, each with a green label, and no line across its discontinuity.
- 2.11 is the TO waypoint's symbol element: INTCPT, as in 68.1.
- 2.13 is four empty entries (FIX INFO not used).
- A temporary flight plan (DIR TO GRABA, not inserted): 2.5 holds the whole temporary route (48 elements, starting at
  "T-P", kind 2, at the aircraft's position) and 2.8 a copy of the alternate, while 2.4, 2.7, 2.11 and 2.12 stay the active
  plan's. The glass draws it as a yellow dashed line from T-P beside the green active route. ERASE removed 2.5 and 2.8.
- Not seen yet: 2.11 after an inserted DIR TO, and an overfly (Task 6).
